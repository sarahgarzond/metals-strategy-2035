# Appendices: Data, Sources, Calculations, and Assumptions

## A1  Definitions and evidence

Mt means million tonnes; GW means gigawatts; EBITDA is earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. Industry revenue, mined metal output, melting output and finished alloy shipments have different boundaries. Report figures retain their original currencies and observation years. No currency conversion or inflation adjustment is used for the scenario indices. Web sources for the newly completed sections were consulted on 28 October 2026; earlier observation years are used where stated.

## A2  Reproducible scenario calculations

Base year 2025 = 100; horizon = 10 years; index = 100 × (1 + annual rate)^10, rounded to the nearest integer. Example: energy base = 100 × 1.03^10 = 134.4 → 134. Aerospace upside = 100 × 1.045^10 = 155.3 → 155. The rates in Section 7 are author-selected sensitivities, not estimated statistical parameters or third-party alloy forecasts. No probabilities, company shipment weights or regional growth rates are assumed. Regional and product priorities are qualitative.

## A3  Interpretation of existing sector estimates

The automotive calculation groups battery-electric and plug-in hybrid cars together; cars with a plug can still contain combustion-engine components. Fleet growth is not a direct measure of new engine-alloy purchases, and holding cobalt intensity constant is a simplifying assumption. The medical-device value extrapolation applies a broader bio-health growth proxy and is not an independently sourced device-market forecast. These existing sector estimates should be read as illustrations, not validated specialty-metal demand projections. The scenario model therefore uses separate explicit application-demand assumptions.
