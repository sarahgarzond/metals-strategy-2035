## 3.2 Oil and gas

Oil and gas remains one of the largest outlets for corrosion resistant alloys, but its growth now comes from gas, LNG and sour reservoirs rather than from oil drilling. The team estimates the addressable high performance metal market in the sector at EUR 8.0bn in 2025, rising to about EUR 9.8bn in 2035 at 2.0% p.a. (team estimate) [MODEL_OG_2025], which makes it a mature but large pool in which the Client must choose its niches carefully.

### Market size, growth, drivers and risks

Because the sector total hides two opposite trends, the spending indicators have to be read separately for oil and for gas. Upstream oil and gas investment fell about 4% to just under USD 570bn in 2025 [IEA_WEI2025_UPSTREAM], and upstream oil spending is set to drop below USD 500bn in 2026 for a third consecutive decline [IEA_WEI2026_UPOIL]. Gas moves the other way, because total gas investment is expected to reach USD 330bn in 2026, the highest level in a decade, carried by LNG export projects in the United States and Qatar [IEA_WEI2026_GAS]. These diverging curves explain why the model grows the LNG cell at 4.5% p.a. and the sour and deepwater cell at only 2.0% p.a. [MODEL_OG_CELLS], and they lead directly to the LNG build out as the main driver.

The LNG wave is the strongest volume driver for the Client's materials through 2030. The IEA tracker counts 345 bcm/yr of new export capacity between 2025 and 2030 [IEA_LNG_TRACKER], and 68.4 Mtpa of liquefaction reached a final investment decision in 2025 [IGU_LNG_FID_2025], although LNG Prime counts 84 Mtpa with a different scope [LNGPRIME_FID_2025]. Shell expects demand to rise from 422 Mtpa in 2025 to between 650 and 710 Mtpa in 2040 [SHELL_LNG_OUTLOOK_2026], which implies roughly 3% p.a. growth (team calculation) and supports a growth rate for LNG materials well above the sector average. This optimism carries a clear geopolitical risk, however, which the next paragraph sets out.

The main risk for the sector is geopolitical, and it has already materialised in 2026. Missile strikes on Ras Laffan in March 2026 affected about 17% of Qatari LNG export capacity, repairs are expected to take three to five years and force majeure has been extended month by month [RASLAFFAN_DAMAGE_2026]. The first North Field East train, part of a 32 Mtpa expansion, is now reported for the first half of 2027 [QATAR_NFE_2026], and the Rystad forecast of more than USD 300bn offshore capex in 2026 comes with warnings about delayed sanctioning and shipyard bottlenecks [RYSTAD_OFFSHORE_2026]. Nickel price swings add a commercial risk, because CRA quotes pass metal costs through to buyers who then question solid alloy designs, which brings the discussion to the materials themselves.

### Emerging technologies and materials required

Because operators want CRA performance at carbon steel cost, the technology trend runs towards thin alloy layers on steel. Mechanically lined pipe, a carbon steel pipe with a thin Alloy 625 or 825 liner made from strip or plate, is now standard for the Brazilian pre salt, and Butting reports about 300 km or 45,000 t in production or planning for Brazil [BUTTING_BRAZIL_MLP]. Ultra large LNG carriers of 271,000 m3 with the GTT NO96 Super+ membrane are on order at Hudong-Zhonghua [GTT_FY2025], and CCS injection wells move from 13Cr towards 25Cr super duplex and nickel alloys once free water is present [AADE_CCS_CRA]. These trends define a materials list that fits the Client's forms closely, as the table below shows.

| Application | Alloy | Product form | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| LNG carrier membrane NO96 | FeNi36 Invar | Strip 0.7 mm [GTT_NO96_SPEC] | Near zero expansion at minus 163 °C |
| Lined and clad flowlines, risers | Alloy 625, 825 | Liner strip, plate, clad plate | Resistance to H2S, CO2 and chlorides |
| Subsea valves, hangers, bolts | Alloy 718, 725 | Bar | High strength with sour resistance |
| Sour well completions | Alloy 825, 718, super duplex | Extruded tube, bar | Sour service limits of carbon steel |
| 9% Ni tanks, CRA girth welds | Alloy 625 type filler | Wire, strip | Matching toughness and corrosion resistance |

### Geography, customers and competitors

Because LNG and sour gas lead the growth, demand concentrates in a few hubs that the Client can address with a small sales team. The United States took most of the 2025 LNG decisions, with 62 of 84 Mtpa in the LNG Prime count [LNGPRIME_FID_2025], the Middle East reached a record 20% share of upstream spend [IEA_WEI2025_UPSTREAM], and Korean and Chinese yards drive Invar demand [APERAM_IMPHY_2025]. Japan supplies 57% of world exports of stainless casing and tubing made without a weld seam, worth USD 2.48bn in 2024 [COMTRADE_730424_2024], which shows that well tubulars belong to integrated tube mills rather than to strip and plate producers, and this split shapes the competitor map that follows.

The customer side is equally concentrated, with Petrobras, ADNOC and QatarEnergy as operators and Allseas, Saipem, TechnipFMC, NPCC and Tecnimont as contractors. Lined pipe makers such as Butting, Cladtek and Inox Tech buy liner material directly, which makes them the natural first customers for the Client's strip and plate, while three recent projects show how these chains work in practice.

### Where high performance metals gain or lose relevance

These projects show a shift in the tonnage of nickel alloy per kilometre of pipe rather than a loss of the sector. POSCO claims its high manganese steel costs about 30% less than 9% Ni steel for cryogenic tanks [POSCO_HMS], Strohm has supplied 70 thermoplastic composite jumpers in the Stabroek block instead of steel [STROHM_WHIPTAIL_2024], and a market report claims that overlay pipe costs 40% to 60% of solid CRA when installed [CLADPIPE_MR_2023], a figure with low confidence. Nickel alloys therefore lose solid tube volume but gain thin strip, clad plate, Invar and filler metal, which points to the attractive value chain steps.

### Attractive value chain steps for the Client

Because value migrates to thin layers and joining, the Client should sell nickel alloy liner strip and plate in Alloy 625 and 825 to lined and clad pipe makers in Germany, Italy, Brazil and Asia. Nickel base welding consumables for CRA girth welds, overlays and 9% Ni tanks offer recurring project volume and fit the existing portfolio (team assessment). A second source position for FeNi36 membrane strip deserves a qualification study with GTT, whereas extruded OCTG tubes, 9% Ni plate and umbilical tube should stay outside the focus because integrated mills control them, which sets up the cross sector view in section 3.8.
