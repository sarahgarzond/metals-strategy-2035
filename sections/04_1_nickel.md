## 4.1 Nickel

Nickel is the foundation of the high performance alloy industry, and within a decade its supply has moved to a single country. USGS estimates world mine output at 3.90 Mt of contained nickel in 2025, with 2.60 Mt or about two thirds from Indonesia, while the IEA puts the total at 4.21 Mt and Indonesia at 63 percent of mining and 45 percent of refining, so the series differ in level but not in concentration [NI_USGS_MINE_W_2025, NI_USGS_MINE_ID_2025, IEA_CM_NI_SUPPLY_2025_2035]. Primary production reached 3.875 Mt against usage of 3.577 Mt, yet just over half of the primary supply in China and Indonesia was nickel pig iron for stainless steel rather than the class 1 metal that vacuum melted alloys require [NI_INSG_PROD_2025, NI_INSG_USE_2025, NI_INSG_NPI_2025]. Alloy makers therefore depend on a few class 1 sources such as Vale in Canada, Glencore Nikkelverk in Norway and Nornickel in Russia, and Russian metal produced on or after 13 April 2024 may no longer enter the United States or be warranted on metal exchanges under US and UK sanctions [COMTRADE_MIRROR_EXTRA6_2024, NI_US_TREASURY_RUS_METALS_2024].

This oversupply explains the price, which averaged USD 25,834 per tonne in 2022 and USD 15,162 in 2025 in the World Bank series, against a USGS estimate of USD 15,000 [NI_WB_AVG_2022, NI_WB_AVG_2025, NI_USGS_LME_2025E]. Indonesia now manages output through its annual ore quota, cutting the 2026 quota to 260 to 270 Mt of ore before raising it to 290 to 300 Mt, and the International Nickel Study Group cut its 2026 surplus forecast to 19 kt after 299 kt in 2025, so the price of the raw material is now set in Jakarta as much as in London [ARGUS_NI_RKAB_2026, NI_RKAB_2026_REV, NI_INSG_BAL_2025, NI_INSG_BAL_2026].

Nickel reaches industry in two fundamentally different product forms, and the gap between them reveals where value is created. Stainless steel absorbs most nickel, with 64.2 Mt of gross melt shop output in 2025, and the IEA expects nickel demand outside energy technologies, which covers stainless steel and alloys, to grow only from 2,906 kt in 2025 to 3,255 kt in 2035 [NI_WS_MELT_2025, IEA_CM_NI_OTHER_2025_2035]. Nickel alloys are sold as strip, sheet, plate, bar, wire and tube, and German exports of nickel plate, sheet and strip reached 17,775 t worth USD 578 million in 2024, about USD 32,500 per tonne or twice the price of the contained metal, a tonnage that Eurostat confirms within 2 percent [NI_CT_DE_7506_2024_T, NI_PC_DE_SHEET_X_2024, NI_WB_AVG_2024]. Buyers pay for qualification and form rather than for nickel, a pattern that cobalt makes sharper still.
