## 4.1 Nickel

Nickel is the foundation of the high performance alloy industry, and within a decade its supply has moved to a single country. USGS estimates world mine output at 3.90 Mt of contained nickel in 2025, with 2.60 Mt or about two thirds from Indonesia, while the IEA puts the total at 4.21 Mt and Indonesia at 63 percent of mining and 45 percent of refining, so the series differ in level but not in concentration [NI_USGS_MINE_W_2025, NI_USGS_MINE_ID_2025, IEA_CM_NI_SUPPLY_2025_2035]. Primary production reached 3.875 Mt against usage of 3.577 Mt, yet just over half of the primary supply in China and Indonesia was nickel pig iron for stainless steel rather than the class 1 metal that vacuum melted alloys require [NI_INSG_PROD_2025, NI_INSG_USE_2025, NI_INSG_NPI_2025]. Class 1 metal itself is no longer scarce, as Chinese origin metal rose from 47 to about 68 percent of LME open tonnage in the first nine months of 2025, so the constraint for vacuum melting is qualified feed from approved sources such as Vale in Canada and Glencore Nikkelverk in Norway [NI_INSG_LME_CHINA_2025, COMTRADE_MIRROR_EXTRA6_2024]. Russian metal produced on or after 13 April 2024 may no longer enter the United States or be warranted on metal exchanges under US and UK sanctions [NI_US_TREASURY_RUS_METALS_2024].

This oversupply explains the price, which averaged USD 25,834 per tonne in 2022 and USD 15,162 in 2025 in the World Bank series [NI_WB_AVG_2022, NI_WB_AVG_2025]. Indonesia now manages output through its ore quota, first cut to 260 to 270 Mt for 2026 and then raised to 290 to 300 Mt, while the International Nickel Study Group cut its 2026 surplus forecast to 19 kt after 299 kt in 2025, so the raw material price is now set in Jakarta as much as in London [ARGUS_NI_RKAB_2026, NI_RKAB_2026_REV, NI_INSG_BAL_2025, NI_INSG_BAL_2026].

Nickel reaches industry in two fundamentally different product forms, and the gap between them reveals where value is created. Stainless steel absorbs most nickel, with 64.2 Mt of gross melt shop output in 2025, and the IEA expects nickel demand outside energy technologies, which covers stainless steel and alloys, to grow only from 2,906 kt in 2025 to 3,255 kt in 2035 [NI_WS_MELT_2025, IEA_CM_NI_OTHER_2025_2035]. Nickel alloys are sold as strip, sheet, plate, bar, wire and tube, and German exports of nickel plate, sheet and strip reached 17,775 t worth USD 578 million in 2024, about USD 32,500 per tonne, a tonnage that Eurostat confirms within 2 percent [NI_CT_DE_7506_2024_T, NI_PC_DE_SHEET_X_2024].

That unit value is 1.9 times the 2024 nickel price, but a tonne of alloy is not a tonne of nickel, so the conversion premium has to be read on alloy content. Alloy C-276 contains about 57 percent nickel, 16 percent molybdenum and 16 percent chromium, worth about USD 19,000 per tonne at the prices of 2024 and 2025 before its tungsten, which puts the unit value at about 1.7 times metal content in a team calculation [MO_HAYNES_C276_COMP, NI_WB_AVG_2024, MO_USGS_PRICE_OXIDE_2025, CR_PRICE_METAL_2025]. Producers pass the metal part on through alloy surcharges or index clauses, as ATI does for most of its 70 million pounds of nickel and Carpenter for all but the 43 percent of sales under firm prices, and Carpenter even reports medical sales of USD 278.4 million as USD 224.3 million excluding surcharge [CO_ATI_NILBS_2025, CO_CRS_FIRMPRICE_2026, MED_CRS_MED_EXSUR_2026]. The margin therefore sits in the base price, which Special Metals raised at Huntington eight times between April 2021 and February 2024 by 5 to 20 percent each time [CO_SMC_PRICE_2021_2024]. Buyers pay for qualification and form rather than for nickel, a pattern that cobalt makes sharper still.
