## 4.1 Nickel

Nickel is the clearest bridge from the upstream metal balance to the mill products Münvest would sell, which is why the stages below are kept on separate lines before any commercial reading is taken. World mine production of contained nickel was 3.71 million tonnes in 2024 and an estimated 3.90 million tonnes in 2025, and Indonesia accounted for an estimated 2.60 million tonnes in 2025 [NI_USGS_MINE_W_2024, NI_USGS_MINE_W_2025, NI_USGS_MINE_ID_2025]. These mine figures are contained nickel in ore, and they become useful for sourcing only after primary metal, stainless melt and mill product are kept on separate lines.

Primary production is the next stage, covering ferronickel, nickel pig iron, cathode and related refined units. The International Nickel Study Group puts world primary production at 3.875 million tonnes in 2025 and usage at 3.577 million tonnes, with a stated surplus of 299 thousand tonnes, after a 2024 surplus of 116 thousand tonnes [NI_INSG_PROD_2025, NI_INSG_USE_2025, NI_INSG_BAL_2025]. The October 2026 meetings put the 2026 surplus at 19 thousand tonnes and the 2027 surplus at 70 thousand tonnes, so the near term balance is a stated tonne surplus rather than a description of that surplus as small [NI_INSG_BAL_2026]. London Metal Exchange nickel is primary metal of at least 99.80 percent purity, which is a narrower pool than the headline primary total, and the 2025 annual average cash price was about USD 15,000 per tonne [NI_LME_SPEC, USGS_NI_LME_2025].

Stainless melt is the main first use of primary nickel and is counted as gross steel. World melt shop production was 64.157 million tonnes in 2025, of which the European Union produced 5.659 million tonnes [NI_WS_MELT_2025, CR_SS_MELT_2025_EU]. That stainless system is measured in millions of tonnes of steel, while the mill forms below are measured in thousands of tonnes of plate, sheet, bar and wire, and Table 2 is the only place the full ladder is set out.

### Apparent consumption of European nickel mill forms

Where Eurostat publishes sold production, imports and exports for the same code, apparent consumption equals sold production plus imports minus exports [AC_METHOD]. For EU nickel plate, sheet, strip and foil (PRODCOM 24452200) in 2024, sold production was 80.9 million kilograms, imports were 14.4 million kilograms and exports were 37.5 million kilograms, so apparent consumption was 57.8 million kilograms [AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024]. The sold production leg carries Eurostat's estimated flag, EU trade in this dataset is extra EU trade, and hot band sold to an EU reroller and then sold again as cold strip can sit inside sold production twice. The code mixes pure nickel and nickel alloy and does not report contained nickel, and the four digit customs split between unalloyed sheet and nickel alloy sheet is not yet in the source log, so 57.8 million kilograms is an upper bound on a mixed code.

The value columns show why that upper bound is not a specialty price. EU sold production was about EUR 14,476 per tonne, German sold production was about EUR 8,788 per tonne, and EU imports were about EUR 47,824 per tonne, all team estimates from the logged value and quantity cells [TE_NI_EU_FLAT_SP_UV_2024, TE_NI_DE_FLAT_SP_UV_2024, TE_NI_EU_FLAT_IMP_UV_2024]. Acerinox High Performance Alloys sales of EUR 1,683 million on 47 thousand finished tonnes are about EUR 35,809 per finished tonne, which is a different book from the mixed flat code [TE_NI_HPA_FINISH_UV_2025, CO_ACX_HPA_2025]. German sold production jumped from 15,870 tonnes in 2019 to 36,477 tonnes in 2020 while the logged value stayed near EUR 177 million to EUR 191 million, so the quantity series has a break that a buyer should not read as a doubling of alloy demand [TE_NI_DE_FLAT_BREAK_2019_2020].

Long products have the opposite trade position, so flat and bar are planned as two balances. EU apparent consumption of nickel bar, rod and wire was 5,380,347 kilograms in 2024, from sold production of 2.5 million kilograms and imports of about 5.6 million kilograms [AC_EU_NI_BAR_2024]. The sold production cell is rounded on a 500,000 kilogram base, so apparent consumption can sit at about 5.1 million to 5.6 million kilograms, and the net importer conclusion holds inside that band [TE_NI_BAR_AC_BAND_2024]. Exports of about 2.70 million kilograms sit above the rounded production figure, which means stock or an estimate that is not a census. Special Metals at Hereford is outside the EU27 customs territory, and HS 7502 unwrought nickel, where rotating billet is often declared, is outside this mill product identity [CT_DE_7502_2024].

Tube has three logged legs and still does not support a capacity recommendation. EU sold production of nickel tubes, pipes and fittings was 21.0 million kilograms in 2024, imports were 4.7 million kilograms and exports were 16.5 million kilograms, so apparent consumption was 9.2 million kilograms, and the sold production cell is a round estimate [AC_EU_NI_TUBE_2024]. Subsea tube and chemical tube stay options with no tonnes until a plant route and a named grade are the company's own. The unit values and the bar band are set beside the tonnes in Chapter 6, which is why the next subsection only places output, trade and stainless melt on the same page.

### Output, trade and stainless melt

The German export leg of the flat balance agrees with the customs record closely enough to give the two sources different jobs. Comtrade reports German exports of HS 7506 at 17,775 tonnes in 2024, and PRODCOM reports 18,087,152 kilograms for the export leg of code 24452200 [NI_CT_DE_7506_2024_T, NI_PC_DE_SHEET_X_2024]. PRODCOM remains the base for the European balance and Comtrade remains the base for partner corridors, and neither series is qualified alloy demand.

Acerinox High Performance Alloys melted 83 thousand tonnes and finished 47 thousand tonnes in 2025, counting VDM Metals and Haynes once, and those figures are output in a weak chemical and oil and gas year rather than nameplate [NI_ACX_HPA_2025, CO_ACX_HPA_MIX_2025]. Finished product is worldwide and already includes the United States plants in the same division, so it is not a European ceiling and it is not compared with EU flat apparent consumption until an alloy split exists. A vacuum induction heat and a later remelt of the same metal would double count if the 83 thousand tonnes were added to stainless melt, which is why Appendix A4 asks for the filing footnote and for nameplate by plant and by route. The furnace and finishing steps aimed at 2028 are capacity that is not inside the 2025 tonnes [CO_ACX_HAYN_INV].

Outokumpu's about 13 thousand tonnes is a specialty stainless pocket at Avesta, inside 2025 deliveries of 1,751 thousand tonnes, and it is not 13 thousand tonnes of Alloy 625 or Alloy C-276 [CO_OUTOKUMPU_HINI_CAP_2026, CO_OUTOKUMPU_DELIVERIES_2025]. Remelt equipment is planned for early 2028, and greenfield melt and casting follow a decision in the first half of 2027 with production at the end of 2029 [CO_OUTOKUMPU_AVESTA_NI_2026]. Duplex, 904L and 254 SMO are stainless grades, and the overlap with a nickel alloy mill is mainly on nickel predominant grades such as Alloy 825, which is evidence of competitive intent at the boundary rather than current chapter 75 plate. Aperam Alloys and Specialties shipped 61 thousand tonnes in 2025 at a printed average selling price of EUR 17,475 per tonne, which is a different book and stops a reader treating Acerinox as the only European specialty mill [CO_APERAM_AS_2025]. Chromium feeds the stainless system and is the next profile for that reason.
