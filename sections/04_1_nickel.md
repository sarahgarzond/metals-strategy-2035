## 4.1 Nickel

Indonesia has become the largest source of nickel within a decade. The USGS estimates 2025 mine output at 3.90 Mt of contained nickel, with 2.60 Mt, or about two thirds, from Indonesia. The IEA estimates 4.21 Mt, with Indonesia at 63 percent of mining and 45 percent of refining, so both show concentrated supply [IEA_CM_CO_DEMAND_2025_2035, NI_USGS_MINE_ID_2025].

Primary nickel output reached 3.875 Mt against 3.577 Mt of use, and just over half of Chinese and Indonesian supply was nickel pig iron for stainless steel [NI_INSG_LME_CHINA_2025, NI_INSG_BAL_2025]. Vacuum-melted alloys need class 1 nickel, which is widely available, because the Chinese share of open LME tonnage rose from 47 to about 68 percent in the first nine months of 2025. The real constraint is approved feedstock from sources such as Vale in Canada and Glencore Nikkelverk in Norway [NI_INSG_LME_CHINA_2025, COMTRADE_MIRROR_EXTRA6_2024]. US and UK sanctions bar Russian metal produced from 13 April 2024 from US imports and exchange warrants [NI_US_TREASURY_RUS_METALS_2024].

The surplus explains the falling price, from a World Bank average of USD 25,834 per tonne in 2022 to USD 15,162 in 2025 [NI_WB_AVG_2022]. Indonesia first cut its 2026 ore quota to 260 to 270 Mt, then raised it to 290 to 300 Mt, and the International Nickel Study Group cut its 2026 surplus forecast to 19 kt, after 299 kt in 2025. The quota is therefore the main swing factor for price [ARGUS_NI_RKAB_2026, NI_RKAB_2026_REV, NI_INSG_BAL_2025].

Stainless steel uses most nickel, with melt shop output of 64.2 Mt gross in 2025. The IEA expects nickel demand outside energy technologies, including stainless steel and alloys, to rise from 2,906 kt in 2025 to 3,255 kt in 2035 [IEA_CM_CO_DEMAND_2025_2035, CR_SS_MELT_2025_WORLD].

Nickel alloys reach industry as strip, sheet, plate, bar, wire, and tube. In 2024, Germany exported 17,775 t of nickel plate, sheet, and strip worth USD 578 million, about USD 32,500 per tonne or 1.9 times the nickel price [NI_PC_DE_SHEET_X_2024, COMTRADE_7506_2019_2024]. That ratio overstates conversion value, because alloys contain other metals. Alloy C-276, with about 57 percent nickel, 16 percent molybdenum, and 16 percent chromium, held metal worth about USD 19,000 per tonne before tungsten. The team estimates the export unit value at about 1.7 times that metal content [MO_HAYNES_C276_COMP, CR_MINE_ORE_2025_WORLD, MO_USGS_MINE_CHINA_2025, NI_WB_AVG_2022].

Producers pass metal costs on through surcharges or index clauses, as ATI does for most of its 70 million pounds of nickel, and Carpenter for sales outside the 43 percent under firm prices [AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025, CO_CRS_CAPEX_2026]. Margins therefore depend on base prices. Special Metals raised its Huntington base prices eight times from April 2021 to February 2024, by 5 to 20 percent each time [CO_SMC_PRICE_2021_2024]. Form and qualification explain why buyers pay above metal value, while cobalt adds a sharper supply risk.
