# 1. Introduction

The decision this report asks the board to take is a ranking of qualified orders, not a capacity addition sized to a mixed trade code. The three priorities are aerospace billet, approved sheet and repair wire, corrosion resistant plate and welding products where a named grade is imported or replaces a specified supplier, and precision electrical strip and wire only where the grade is one the mill already finishes. EU nickel flat apparent consumption of 57.8 million kilograms in 2024 is the ceiling of a mixed code that still joins pure nickel and nickel alloy, and it is not qualified alloy demand and not room under the Acerinox finish figure [AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024]. Flat products are a net export of that mixed code, so undifferentiated flat tonnes are refused, while bar, rod and wire is a logged net import that is still a rounded estimate and is a test of the existing book rather than a case to add bar capacity [AC_EU_NI_BAR_2024, TE_NI_BAR_AC_BAND_2024].

The same ranking carries a stop list that is as specific as the pursue list. Glow plugs and the rest of the diesel only set come off the growth list, undifferentiated stainless is left where stainless already meets the duty, China implant tenders are not pursued after the recorded price cuts, and no electrolyser or powder programme is funded without a financed buyer. Oil and gas is kept only where an approval the company itself holds supports the margin, automotive is harvested only where the account is already profitable, and medical implant bar stays an option until an implant maker and a regulatory path are the company's own. The first 24 months test the European bar position against the company's bar book, cap the live qualification list, and release finishing capacity only against a repeat order that still clears the downside case.

Because those choices depend on keeping each metal at the stage the statistics measure, the chapters that follow separate supply, trade, industrial use and the path to 2035. World mine production of contained nickel was an estimated 3.90 million tonnes in 2025, while stainless melt shop output in the same year was 64.157 million tonnes of gross steel, and neither figure is a shipment of plate or bar [NI_USGS_MINE_W_2025, NI_WS_MELT_2025]. Supply asks what commercial form is produced and by whom, trade asks where that form is imported and exported, demand asks which component and buyer turn the form into an order, and the outlook tests whether that order still exists when no tonne share of the mixed code is invented. Münvest meets this system as a producer of qualified mill products, which is why the next chapter keeps the VDM Metals catalogue as an application benchmark only.
