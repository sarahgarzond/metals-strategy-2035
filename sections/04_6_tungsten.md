## 4.6 Tungsten

Tungsten's largest use is cemented carbide, so a mine statistic does not describe an alloy shipment and a carbide price does not describe a nickel mill product. World mine production was an estimated 85,000 tonnes of contained tungsten in 2025, of which China accounted for an estimated 67,000 tonnes, and United States mine production was zero [USGS_MCS2026_TUNGSTEN]. The price that names its stage is the estimated Rotterdam concentrate average of USD 380 per dry metric ton unit of tungsten trioxide in 2025, after a path during the year from USD 266 to USD 551 for concentrate and from USD 331 to USD 675 for ammonium paratungstate [USGS_MCS2026_TUNGSTEN]. About 60 percent of United States consumption goes to cemented carbide parts, which is the commercial form that dominates volume, while steels and superalloys are a smaller first use [USGS_MCS2026_TUNGSTEN].

China required an export licence from 4 February 2025 on specified tungsten items, including ammonium paratungstate, and that control does not state a quota in tonnes [W_MOFCOM_2025_10]. EU sold production of tungsten powders and unwrought metal is logged as a rounded cell and is not used here as alloy demand [EUROSTAT_PRODCOM_W]. No European mill apparent consumption of a tungsten alloy form is stated. Sandvik's powder business is evidence that recycling and powder sit with the carbide chain, not that a nickel alloy mill should add tungsten capacity from the mine total [W_SANDVIK_Q3_2026]. The remaining metals are grouped next, with the same refusal to add unlike stages together.
