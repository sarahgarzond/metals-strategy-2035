## 4.6 Tungsten

Tungsten’s largest use is in tungsten carbide for cemented carbides, with further applications in specialty alloys, electrical components and wear-resistant products (USGS, n.d.-b). These outlets differ materially from each other in processing requirements and buyer structure.

A supply assessment should separate concentrates, intermediate compounds, metal powder, carbide and finished products. Prices must identify the specific product rather than apply one quotation to the whole chain. The implication for Münvest is to define the relevant alloy or product opportunity before interpreting broad tungsten-market developments. A production ranking, current market balance and price time series are not asserted without a verified dataset.
