## 5.7 Medical Technology

Medical technology offers the steadiest growth of all seven industries, because ageing populations rather than business cycles drive the hip and knee replacements made from cobalt chromium, titanium and zirconium alloys.

### 5.7.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Joint replacement is the most measurable driver of implant metal demand, as in 2023 the OECD average was 198 hip and 156 knee replacements per 100,000 people, and rates rose over the decade in nearly every OECD country [MED_OECD_HIP_RATE_2023]. Europe's medical technology market reached about EUR 173 billion in 2025, while the United States accounts for 46.4 percent of global device sales [MED_MTE_EUR_2025, MED_MTE_EUR_2024]. China is large but unprofitable for implant metals, since central purchasing cut the prices of artificial hips and knees by about 82 percent [MED_NHSA_VBP_2022]. The best measured size is at the procedure stage, about 2.75 million hip and knee replacements in 2023 in the 35 OECD members reporting both, excluding the United States and Japan, while implant alloy tonnage is not published [MED_OECD_HK_COUNT_2023, MED_OECD_HIP_RATE_2023].

### 5.7.2 Applications and Opportunities

The buyers of implant metals are few and powerful, which makes approval and reliability more important than price. Stryker reported orthopaedic revenue of USD 9,469 million in 2025, and the four leading orthopaedic groups exceed USD 20 billion of implant revenue [CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025, CO_SNN_ORTHO_2025, MED_JNJ_ORTHO_2024]. The mill side is volatile, as bar supplier Carpenter saw medical sales excluding surcharge fall 24 percent to USD 224.3 million in fiscal 2026 on customer destocking [MED_CRS_MED_EXSUR_2026].

Specifications differ by product form, as ASTM F75 covers cast CoCrMo, both investment castings such as knee femoral components and the casting alloy as ingot or bar, ASTM F1537 covers wrought CoCrMo bar, rod and wire, and ASTM F3213 covers CoCrMo made by powder bed fusion, as in the printed talus that the FDA approved in 2023 [MED_ASTM_F75_2023, MED_ASTM_F1537_2020, MED_ASTM_F3213_2017, MED_FDA_RESTOR3D_2023]. Smith & Nephew's OXINIUM, an oxidized surface on a Zr2.5Nb alloy developed as an alternative to cobalt chromium femoral components, reaches 94.1 percent survivorship at 20 years, so it takes demand from CoCr but needs wrought Zr2.5Nb bar to ASTM F2384 from an undisclosed supplier [CO_SNN_OXINIUM_2025, REF_ASTM_INTERNATIONAL_N_D, MED_ASTM_F2384_2024].

Implications for suppliers:

* Offer CoCrMo casting alloy to ASTM F75 and bar to ASTM F1537 to implant makers in Europe and the United States.
* Develop CoCrMo powder to ASTM F3213 and Zr2.5Nb bar to ASTM F2384, and pass the supplier audits of the device makers.
