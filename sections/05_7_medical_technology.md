## 5.7 Medical Technology

Medical technology offers the steadiest growth of all seven industries, because its demand is driven by ageing populations rather than by business cycles. Every hip and knee replacement contains a few hundred grams of cobalt chromium or titanium, and the number of these procedures keeps rising in almost every developed country.

### 5.7.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Joint replacement is the most directly measurable driver of implant metal demand. In 2023 the OECD average was 198 hip and 156 knee replacements per 100,000 people, and rates rose over the decade in nearly every OECD country, with Germany, Switzerland, Australia, Finland and Denmark among the highest [MED_OECD_HIP_RATE_2023]. Europe's medical technology market reached about EUR 173 billion in 2025, while the United States accounts for 46.4 percent of global device sales [MED_MTE_EUR_2025, MED_MTE_EUR_2024]. China is large but unprofitable for implant metals, since central purchasing cut the prices of artificial hips and knees by about 82 percent [MED_NHSA_VBP_2022].

### 5.7.2 Applications and Opportunities

The buyers of implant metals are few and powerful, which makes approval and reliability more important than price. Stryker reported orthopaedic revenue of USD 9,469 million in 2025, Zimmer Biomet names cobalt chrome among its purchased commodities, and the four leading orthopaedic groups together exceed USD 20 billion of implant revenue [CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025, CO_ZBH_COMMODITY_2025, CO_ZBH_PRODUCTS_2025, CO_SNN_ORTHO_2025, MED_JNJ_ORTHO_2024]. The FDA approved an additively manufactured cobalt chromium talus implant in 2023, which confirms that the alloy remains accepted, while Smith & Nephew's oxidized zirconium, with 94.1 percent survivorship at 20 years, is a substitution threat that Smith & Nephew supplies from its own chain [MED_FDA_RESTOR3D_2023, CO_SNN_OXINIUM_2025].

Implications for suppliers:

* Focus on CoCr bar certified to ASTM F75 and F1537 for implant makers in Europe and the United States.
* Develop medical grade CoCr powder for printed implants and pass the supplier audits of the device makers.
