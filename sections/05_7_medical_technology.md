## 5.7 Medical Technology

Medical technology offers the steadiest growth of the seven industries. Aging populations drive demand for cobalt-chromium, titanium, and zirconium implants, largely independent of business cycles.

### 5.7.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Joint replacements are the most measurable driver of implant metal. In 2023, the OECD average was 198 hip and 156 knee replacements per 100,000 people [MED_OECD_HIP_RATE_2023]. Europe's medtech market was about EUR 173 billion in 2025, and the United States held 46.4 percent of 2024 device sales [MED_MTE_EUR_2024, MED_MTE_EUR_2025]. Central purchasing in China cut hip and knee prices by about 82 percent [MED_NHSA_VBP_2022].

Because implant alloy tonnage is not published, procedures are the best measure. The 35 OECD members reporting both recorded about 2.75 million hip and knee replacements in 2023, excluding the United States and Japan [MED_OECD_HIP_RATE_2023].

### 5.7.2 Applications and Opportunities

A few large companies buy most implant metals, so approval matters greatly. Stryker reported USD 9,469 million in orthopaedic revenue in 2025, and the four leading groups more than USD 20 billion in implants [MED_JNJ_ORTHO_2024, CO_SNN_ORTHO_2025, CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025]. Mill demand still swings quickly, as Carpenter's medical sales fell 24 percent to USD 224.3 million excluding surcharge in fiscal 2026, as customers cut inventories [MED_CRS_MED_EXSUR_2026].

ASTM standards differ by product form. ASTM F75 covers cast CoCrMo, including knee femoral castings and casting alloy sold as ingot or bar. ASTM F1537 covers wrought CoCrMo bar, rod, and wire, and ASTM F3213 covers powder bed fusion, used for the printed talus implant the FDA approved in 2023 [MED_ASTM_F3213_2017, MED_ASTM_F1537_2020, MED_ASTM_F75_2023, MED_FDA_RESTOR3D_2023]. Incumbents already hold these approvals, as Carpenter sells BioDur CCM Plus bar and powder to ASTM F1537 and ISO 5832-12, and ATI lists cobalt based alloys for medical use [CO_CRS_BIODUR_CCM, CO_ATI_MEDICAL].

Smith & Nephew's OXINIUM, an oxidized Zr2.5Nb femoral component with 94.1 percent survivorship at 20 years, replaces some CoCr. It needs wrought Zr2.5Nb bar to ASTM F2384 [MED_ASTM_F2384_2024, REF_ASTM_INTERNATIONAL_N_D, CO_SNN_ORTHO_2025]. The supplier is not disclosed, and ATI is a possible source because it lists zirconium niobium alloys for medical use [CO_ATI_MEDICAL].

Implications for suppliers:

* Offer CoCrMo casting alloy to ASTM F75 and bar to ASTM F1537 to implant makers in Europe and the United States.
* Develop CoCrMo powder to ASTM F3213 and Zr2.5Nb bar to ASTM F2384, and pass the supplier audits of the device makers.
