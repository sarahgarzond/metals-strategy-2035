## 5.2 Oil and Gas

Oil and gas is a stable market for high-performance metals, as investment moves from oil to gas. Sour gas, deepwater fields, and liquefied natural gas (LNG) need alloys that ordinary steel cannot replace.

### 5.2.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Global upstream oil and gas investment fell from USD 625 billion in 2019 to USD 542 billion in 2025 and was about USD 546 billion in 2026 [OG_WEI2026_SSA_UPSTREAM_2026, IEA_WEI_GASPOWER_2025].

North America remained the largest region, at USD 165 billion in 2026 against USD 219 billion in 2019. Middle East investment rose from USD 75 billion to USD 93 billion, with gas roughly doubling to USD 45.4 billion [IEA_WEI_GASPOWER_2025]. Under current policies, LNG trade could rise from 560 bcm in 2024 to 880 bcm in 2035 [OG_WEO2025_CPS_EXTRA_UPSTREAM, IEA_WEI_GASPOWER_2025]. Alloy and clad pipe tonnes are not published, so investment is the best measure.

### 5.2.2 Applications and Opportunities

Customer approval is the main entry barrier. ISO 15156 sets which alloys suit sour service, but producers still need NORSOK M-650 qualification and operator approval for each product form [OG_ISO15156_2020, OG_NORSOK_M650_2011]. Orders flow through the vendor lists of EPC contractors and subsea firms such as SLB and TechnipFMC, which booked USD 10.1 billion in subsea orders in 2025 [CO_SLB_PRODSYS_2025, CO_TECHNIPFMC_ORDERS_2025].

Forms include nickel alloy bar for wellheads and completion tools, corrosion-resistant tubing, umbilical tube, and clad pipe. Alleima's hyper duplex SAF 3007 succeeds super duplex SAF 2507 in umbilicals, and clad pipe maker BUTTING reports EUR 880 million in turnover [OG_ALLEIMA_UMBILICAL, CO_BUTTING_SALES_2025].

LNG carriers add a smaller FeNi market than trade growth suggests. Only GTT's NO96 membrane uses Invar, a 36 percent nickel steel, in 0.7 mm strakes, while Mark III and Mark III Flex use corrugated 304L stainless steel [MO_GTT_NO96, MO_GTT_MARKIII]. FeNi36 demand therefore follows the unpublished NO96 share of orders. Aperam doubled Imphy capacity in 2025 to supply shipyards with Invar M93 [CO_APERAM_IMPHY_LNG_2025, REF_APERAM_N_D].

Implications for suppliers:

* Monitor sour service: keep NORSOK M-650 and operator approvals current and serve vendor lists from existing capacity.
* Sell strip and welding wire to clad pipe makers selectively, and keep NO96 FeNi36 strip as an option.
