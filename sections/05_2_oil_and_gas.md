## 5.2 Oil and Gas

Oil and gas is a stable market for high performance metals, in which investment moves from oil to gas and sour gas, deep water and LNG need alloys that ordinary steel cannot replace.

### 5.2.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Upstream investment holds its level, but much of it only defends existing output. World upstream oil and gas investment fell from USD 625 billion in 2019 to USD 542 billion in 2025 and about USD 546 billion in 2026, and about 40 percent of it only slows the decline of existing fields [IEA_WEI_UPSTREAM_REGION_2026, OG_WEI2026_UPSTREAM_OG_2026, OG_WEI2025_UPSTREAM_OIL_2025]. Behind this flat total, North America remains the largest region at USD 165 billion in 2026 but has fallen from USD 219 billion in 2019, while the Middle East is the largest region spending above its 2019 level, at USD 93 billion against USD 75 billion, and its gas investment roughly doubled from USD 22.9 billion to USD 45.4 billion [IEA_WEI_UPSTREAM_REGION_2026, IEA_WEI_ME_GAS_2026]. Central and South America grew fastest, from USD 42 billion to USD 68 billion, while LNG trade could grow from 560 bcm in 2024 to 880 bcm in 2035 under current policies [IEA_WEI_UPSTREAM_REGION_2026, OG_WEO2025_LNG_DEMAND_2024]. Upstream investment is thus the best measured stage, since no public source counts the tonnes of nickel alloy, duplex or clad pipe that the industry buys.

### 5.2.2 Applications and Opportunities

The real barrier to entry in this market is customer approval rather than metallurgy. ISO 15156 defines which corrosion resistant alloys in which metallurgical condition suit sour service, so a listed grade is a commodity, whereas each producer and product form must pass NORSOK M-650 qualification and operator approval [OG_ISO15156_2020, OG_NORSOK_M650_2011]. Orders then flow through the approved vendor lists of EPC contractors and subsea majors such as SLB and TechnipFMC, which took USD 10.1 billion of subsea orders in 2025 [CO_SLB_PRODSYS_2025, CO_TECHNIPFMC_ORDERS_2025]. Product forms reach from nickel alloy bar for wellheads and completion tools and corrosion resistant well tubing, where Alleima saw an order of about SEK 250 million cancelled in 2026, to umbilical tube, in which its hyper duplex SAF 3007 succeeds super duplex SAF 2507, and clad pipe, for which BUTTING reports turnover of EUR 880 million [CO_ALLEIMA_OCTG_2026, OG_ALLEIMA_UMBILICAL, CO_BUTTING_SALES_2025]. LNG carriers add a FeNi market, as Aperam doubled capacity at Imphy in France in the first half of 2025 for the shipyards that build them, whose membrane tanks use its Invar M93 cryogenic alloy [CO_APERAM_IMPHY_LNG_2025, REF_APERAM_N_D].

Competitors invest despite the flat market, as Outokumpu adds about 13,000 t of high nickel flat capacity at Avesta from 2028 and Aperam has doubled its Imphy capacity, which shows how much approved positions are worth to producers that already hold them [CO_OUTOKUMPU_HINI_CAP_2026, CO_APERAM_IMPHY_LNG_2025].

Implications for suppliers:

* Win places on the vendor lists of Middle East and South American operators and of their EPC contractors and subsea majors.
* Grow strip and welding wire for clad pipe makers once NORSOK M-650 qualification is secured.
