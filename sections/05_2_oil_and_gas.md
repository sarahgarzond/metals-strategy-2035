## 5.2 Oil and Gas

Oil and gas is a keep market, because upstream spending in 2025 is a replacement flow and the alloy case depends on an approval the company itself holds. World Energy Investment 2025 puts combined upstream oil and gas at just under USD 570 billion, and about 40 percent of that combined total slows decline at existing fields [OG_WEI2025_UPSTREAM_OIL_2025, OG_WEO2025_UPSTREAM_2025]. That 2025 level is not a printed 2035 total, and it is not the wider 2026 supply boundary used in the next paragraph. The applications that follow are therefore a harvest of sour service work, not a proposal to add tube or bar capacity.

### 5.2.1 Historical and Current Market Overview

The sentence that gas spending is at a ten year high and oil spending has fallen for three years belongs to 2026 supply investment on the wider boundary, where natural gas supply investment is USD 330 billion and oil supply investment is below USD 500 billion, including infrastructure [OG_WEI2026_GAS_SUPPLY_2026]. Those figures are not attached to the just under USD 570 billion upstream total. World Energy Outlook 2025 does not print a path that stays at USD 570 billion or rises to about USD 670 billion, and Current Policies is about USD 65 billion a year above Stated Policies in the detailed chapter [OG_WEO2025_CPS_EXTRA_UPSTREAM].

### 5.2.2 Key Applications and Opportunities for the Company

Regional investment in 2026 is uneven on the same upstream boundary, and it narrows where a keep decision is even worth a visit. Sub Saharan Africa is nearly USD 24 billion and Central and South America nearly USD 68 billion, while Middle East upstream spending is down by around 1 percent [OG_WEI2026_SSA_UPSTREAM_2026]. That geography can guide which accounts to keep, and it is not a reason to add melting capacity. The benchmark applications below say which forms are even eligible for that keep decision.

The benchmark application, from the VDM Metals portfolio, covers nickel alloy bar, plate, billet, welding wire and strip for sour service tools, flanges and lined pipe. What Münvest would need before this is an order is a sour service approval in its own name, because a NACE MR0175 or ISO 15156 listing names the producer and the route and does not travel with a catalogue. Shipment, approval and share are unconfirmed, and no approval count is taken from the benchmark.

Thinner linings reduce metal per project, which is another reason the keep decision is about margin on an existing approval and not about more tonnes.

What the company should do: the company should keep oil and gas accounts where a sour service approval the company itself holds supports the margin, and it should not add tube capacity or treat bar as a new investment case. Welding wire for lined pipe can stay inside that keep decision only as a product the mill already makes, and the commercial test of bar is the one set in Chapter 8 against the logged European position.
