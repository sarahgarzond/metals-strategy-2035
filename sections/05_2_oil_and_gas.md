## 5.2 Oil and Gas

Oil and gas is a stable market for the company, in which spending holds its level to 2035 while its mix moves from oil to gas and from the Middle East toward the Americas and Africa. The company's advantage is that many of its alloys are approved for sour gas service, which keeps most competitors out.

### 5.2.1 Investment, Gas and LNG to 2035

Upstream oil and gas investment was just under USD 570 billion in 2025 and about USD 546 billion in 2026, with gas up 8 percent and oil slightly down [OG_WEO2025_UPSTREAM_2025, OG_WEI2026_UPSTREAM_OG_2026]. About 40 percent of upstream spending only slows the decline of existing fields, which supports replacement and workover demand for corrosion resistant parts [OG_WEI2025_UPSTREAM_OIL_2025].

Geography is shifting, because upstream investment rises by over 10 percent in Central and South America and in Africa in 2026, while the Middle East falls slightly and Qatar's North Field East start up slips to at least the first half of 2027 [OG_WEI2026_SSA_UPSTREAM_2026, OG_LNG_NFE_CAPACITY].

### 5.2.2 Key Applications and Opportunities for the Company

The company sells nickel alloy bar, plate, strip and welding wire for wells, pipes and LNG equipment, and the sour service standard ISO 15156 is its main barrier to entry. Clad pipe is the growing form, because it uses a thin nickel alloy liner on carbon steel, and BUTTING, the leading clad pipe maker, reports turnover of EUR 880 million [CO_BUTTING_SALES_2025].

Demand is visible in the order books of the equipment makers, as TechnipFMC reported subsea orders of USD 11.2 billion and a backlog of USD 16.6 billion for 2025 [CO_TECHNIPFMC_ORDERS_2025]. Competitors are investing at the same time, since Alleima started a fourth VAR furnace at Sandviken in 2025 and Outokumpu is adding about 13,000 t of high nickel flat capacity at Avesta from 2028 [CO_ALLEIMA_OCTG_2026, CO_OUTOKUMPU_HINI_CAP_2026]. Acerinox's alloy division, which competes most directly with the company, called oil and gas sluggish in 2025, which shows that the segment rewards approvals rather than volume [ACERINOX_HPA_SALES_2025].

What the company should do:

* Focus sales on gas and offshore projects in Central and South America and in Sub Saharan Africa, where upstream spending grows fastest.
* Sell more strip and welding wire for clad pipe through BUTTING and similar pipe makers, protected by sour service approvals.
* Defend bar for well tools with SLB and TechnipFMC through framework agreements rather than single project bids.
