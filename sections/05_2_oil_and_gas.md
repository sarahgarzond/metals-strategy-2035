## 5.2 Oil and Gas

Oil and gas is a stable market for the company. Spending stays about the same to 2035, but it moves from oil to gas. The company's alloys are approved for sour gas, which few suppliers can offer.

### 5.2.1 Historical and Current Market Overview

Oil and gas companies spent about USD 570 billion on new wells and fields in 2025 (Energy Intelligence, 2025). Gas spending is at a ten-year high, while oil spending has fallen for three years in a row (IEA, 2026b).

By 2035, spending stays near today's level in the IEA base case, or rises to about USD 670 billion in its high case (Table 3).

Table 3: Oil and gas market, today and in 2035

| Measure | Today | 2035 |
|---|---|---|
| Spending on new wells and fields | USD 570 bn (2025) | USD 570 bn (base case) to USD 670 bn (high case) |
| Oil demand | 103 mb/d (2025) | Peaks around 2030 (base case) |
| LNG trade | 560 bcm (2024) | 880 bcm (high case) |

*Sources: Energy Institute (2026), Energy Intelligence (2025), IEA (2025a). Base case = IEA Stated Policies; high case = IEA Current Policies.*

Growth is strongest in Africa and South America. The Middle East is slowing, partly because of damage to gas plants in Qatar (S&P Global Commodity Insights, 2026; Oil & Gas Journal, 2026).

### 5.2.2 Key Applications and Opportunities for the Company

The company sells nickel alloys for wells and pipes that carry corrosive gas. Eleven of its alloys are approved for sour gas, which keeps many competitors out (VDM Metals, n.d.-b).

Table 4: The company's oil and gas applications, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Grows | Lined pipes (welding wire and strip); subsea tubes | Wire, strip, tubes |
| Stable | Well tools; pipes, flanges and fittings; well casing | Bar, plate, billets |

*Source: VDM Metals (n.d.-b). The outlook is our assessment.*

The main buyers are SLB, TechnipFMC and BUTTING (BUTTING, n.d.; SLB, 2026; TechnipFMC, 2026). The main competitors are Special Metals, Alleima and Nippon Steel (Alleima, 2026; Nippon Steel & Sumitomo Metal, 2015; Special Metals, n.d.).

Two trends lower the amount of alloy per project: thinner alloy layers inside pipes, and cheaper stainless steel in some subsea tubes (BUTTING, n.d.).

What the company should do:

* Focus on gas and offshore projects in the Americas and Africa.
* Sell more welding wire for lined pipes and bar for well tools, where the sour-gas approval protects prices.
* Work with pipe makers on thinner linings, so the company still supplies the alloy.
