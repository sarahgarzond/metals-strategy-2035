## 5.3 Energy and Environmental Technology

Energy and environmental technology contains the company's fastest growing near term market, gas turbines, next to a hydrogen market whose orders have collapsed. The opportunity is therefore component specific, and growth in solar, wind or electricity consumption cannot be translated directly into nickel alloy demand.

### 5.3.1 Gas Turbines, Nuclear and Hydrogen in Numbers

Gas turbines are the tightest pocket, because data centres and grid stability have created more orders than the industry can build. Siemens Energy told investors in November 2025 that its gas turbine capacity is sold out until fiscal 2028 and will grow from about 17 GW in fiscal 2024 to above 30 GW a year from fiscal 2028 [CO_SIEMENSENERGY_CMD_2025]. Heavy duty rotor discs are mostly steel, so the nickel demand sits in the casting remelt stock for blades and vanes and in combustor sheet, and the binding constraint is hot section castings and forgings rather than nickel ore.

Hydrogen shows the widest gap between political ambition and the orders that reach equipment suppliers. Global hydrogen demand surpassed 100 Mt in 2025, but low emissions production was still only about 1 Mt, and the announced 2030 pipeline shrank to 27 Mt [EE_H2_DEMAND_2025, EE_H2_LOWEM_2025, EE_H2_PIPELINE_2030]. thyssenkrupp nucera, the only named European buyer of nickel electrode material in the company universe, saw green hydrogen orders fall from EUR 356 million to EUR 26 million in fiscal 2024/25 and abandoned solid oxide stack mass production in August 2026 [CO_TKNUCERA_ORDERS_2025, CO_TKNUCERA_SOEC_2026].

### 5.3.2 Key Applications and Opportunities for the Company

The company's benchmark portfolio covers superalloys for turbines, Nickel 201 strip for alkaline electrolyser cathodes, ferritic interconnect alloys for solid oxide cells, Alloy 690 and zirconium for nuclear plants, and corrosion alloys for flue gas cleaning and waste to energy boilers.

Table 5: The company's energy applications, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Grows strongly | Gas turbine hot section parts and repair | Casting remelt stock, combustor sheet, bar, welding wire |
| Option | Solid oxide cell interconnects at stack makers that use coated ferritic strip | Ferritic chromium strip |
| Option from 2030 | Nuclear steam generators | Alloy 690 bar and hollows for tube makers |
| Uncertain | Alkaline and solid oxide electrolysers | Nickel strip and mesh, interconnect strip |
| Stable | Flue gas desulphurisation, waste to energy overlay | Plate, welding consumables |

*Source: VDM Metals application benchmark, outlook is the team's assessment.*

The nuclear pull is real but late, as the United States government framework of October 2025 covers at least USD 80 billion of AP1000 reactors, and Alleima has reopened a tube mill at Sandviken for steam generator tube, so the company's role would be bar and hollows for such tube makers [CO_WESTINGHOUSE_USGOV_2025, CO_ALLEIMA_NUCLEAR_2025]. Fuel cladding zirconium is captive to the fuel makers and is not an open market. Bloom Energy is the fastest growing solid oxide company, with revenue of USD 2,024 million in 2025 and capacity doubling by the end of 2026, but it does not disclose its interconnect material, so the strip opportunity lies with stack makers that use coated ferritic strip [CO_BLOOM_REV_2025, CO_BLOOM_CAP_2026].

What the company should do:

* Qualify casting remelt stock and combustor sheet with the casting houses and suppliers of Siemens Energy while capacity is sold out to 2028.
* Offer coated ferritic interconnect strip to solid oxide stack makers, released only against signed sample and volume plans.
* Prepare Alloy 690 bar and hollow qualifications with tube makers for nuclear programmes that order from about 2028 onward.
* Treat electrolysers as an option, released for investment only against funded projects and signed purchase volumes.
