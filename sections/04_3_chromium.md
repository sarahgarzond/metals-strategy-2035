## 4.3 Chromium

Chromium gives corrosion-resistant and heat-resistant alloys their protective oxide layer, but it is mined and smelted in different countries. World chromite ore output reached an estimated 51 Mt gross weight in 2025. The USGS puts South Africa at about 23 Mt, while the International Chromium Development Association reports 25.2 Mt on a different definition [CR_ICDA_FECR_2025_CHN, CR_MINE_ORE_2025_WORLD]. China has little chromite but smelts 9.9 Mt of ferrochrome, mostly for stainless steel. The United States imports 79 percent of its chromium needs [CR_ICDA_FECR_2025_CHN, CR_MINE_ORE_2025_WORLD, CR_SS_MELT_2025_WORLD].

Vacuum-melted nickel alloys need purer inputs, such as low-carbon ferrochrome and aluminothermic chromium metal. The US chromium metal price rose to an estimated USD 5.90 per pound in 2025, while ferrochrome fell [CR_MINE_ORE_2025_WORLD]. AMG Chrome in the United Kingdom supplies low-gas chromium metal for single-crystal turbine blades. A vacuum melt shop can stop if it loses this qualified source (AMG Critical Materials N.V., n.d.).
