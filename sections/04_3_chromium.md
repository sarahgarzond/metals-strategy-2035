## 4.3 Chromium

Chromium is present in every corrosion and heat resistant alloy the company sells, and its value chain separates mining from smelting. World chromite ore output was an estimated 51 Mt gross weight in 2025, of which South Africa mined about 23 Mt [CR_MINE_ORE_2025_WORLD, CR_MINE_ORE_2025_ZAF]. These are ore tonnes, not contained chromium, and the alloy industry buys ferrochrome or chromium metal, which are later stages.

Smelting has moved to China although China holds almost no ore, which creates a dependence one step downstream of the mine. China produced 9.9 Mt of ferrochrome in 2025, while South Africa and Zimbabwe together fell 48 percent to 2.1 Mt [CR_ICDA_FECR_2025_CHN]. The demand pool is stainless steel, with 64.2 Mt of melt shop output in 2025, and the United States imports 79 percent of its chromium needs [CR_SS_MELT_2025_WORLD, CR_US_NIR_2025].

For a European producer the only integrated alternative is Outokumpu, which runs the Kemi mine and the Tornio ferrochrome plant in Finland and earned EUR 138 million of adjusted EBITDA in ferrochrome in 2025 [CR_OUTOKUMPU_FECR_SALES_2025]. The company should therefore treat chromium metal and low carbon ferrochrome as qualified inputs that need a second European or Japanese source. Molybdenum, the next element, is the key addition for the most corrosion resistant grades.
