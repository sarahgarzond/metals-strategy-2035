## 4.3 Chromium

Chromium enters the same plants as nickel, but the traded forms are chromite ore, ferrochromium and chromium metal, and a tonne of any one of them is not a tonne of stainless steel or of nickel plate. World chromite mine output is estimated at 51 million tonnes gross weight in 2025, and the demand pool that absorbs most ferrochromium is the stainless melt already shown in Table 2 [CR_MINE_ORE_2025_WORLD, NI_WS_MELT_2025]. The United States ferrochromium price, on a chromium content basis, is estimated at USD 1.60 per pound in 2025, while chromium metal is estimated at USD 5.90 per pound, so the two quotations are not one price [CR_PRICE_FECR_2025, CR_PRICE_METAL_2025].

The scale check against nickel flat product stays the one in Table 2. EU stainless melt of 5.659 million tonnes of steel against EU nickel flat apparent consumption of about 58 thousand tonnes of a mixed code is a gross tonne comparison only, and it does not convert melt into contained chromium and does not set a high performance market [CR_SS_MELT_2025_EU, AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024]. Outokumpu's stainless deliveries and the timed Avesta pocket are already in that table, and they stay in stainless rather than being retold here as nickel alloy plate [CO_OUTOKUMPU_DELIVERIES_2025]. Molybdenum, the next alloying metal, raises the same distinction between a mine balance and a mill product.
