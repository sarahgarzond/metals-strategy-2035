## 5.4 Aerospace

Aerospace is the growth engine of high performance metals, because every jet engine hot section is made of nickel and cobalt superalloys that the supply chain struggles to melt and forge fast enough.

### 5.4.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Fleet growth sets the long term path, as Airbus expects the passenger fleet to grow from 23,310 aircraft in 2025 to 45,550 in 2045, which requires 42,060 new aircraft, while Boeing expects 43,625 deliveries on a wider definition [AERO_AIRBUS_GMF2026_FLEET_2025, AERO_AIRBUS_GMF2026_DELIVERIES, BOEING_CMO_2026]. Engines grow faster than aircraft, as CFM delivered 1,802 LEAP engines in 2025, up 28 percent [AERO_LEAP_2025, CO_GE_LEAP_2025]. The aftermarket adds profit, since aviation services grow from USD 209 billion in 2026 to USD 422 billion in 2045 and materials drive over 85 percent of off wing maintenance cost [AERO_AIRBUS_GSF2026_SERVICES_2045].

Defence adds a third stream, with military expenditure of USD 2,887 billion in 2025 and GE delivering 635 defence engines against 490 a year earlier [AERO_SIPRI_MILEX_2025, CO_GE_DEFENG_2025]. The best measured size at the metal stage is cobalt, as the Cobalt Institute counts 11,499 t of cobalt in aviation in 2025 within 20 kt used in superalloys, and the U.S. Geological Survey reports that 51 percent of United States cobalt consumption goes into superalloys, mainly for aircraft engines, while nickel alloy tonnage is not published [AERO_CI_AVIATION_CO_2025, AERO_CI_SUPERALLOY_CO_2025, AERO_USGS_CO_SUPER_SHARE].

### 5.4.2 Applications and Opportunities

Rotating parts set the highest barrier among the applications in Table 6. Disc and shaft billet is premium melted by vacuum induction melting followed by electroslag and vacuum arc remelting, as at ATI in Lockport and Special Metals in Huntington, and the most demanding discs use superalloy powder, whose quality risk the powder metal matter on Pratt and Whitney's geared turbofan exposed [CO_ATI_MELT_2025, CO_SMC_HUN_EAF, CO_RTX_POWDER_2025]. Each approved melt route is frozen in the engine maker's own specification on top of AMS standards and NADCAP processes, and GE Aerospace states that qualifying a new supplier takes substantial time, so a first rotating approval takes several years [CO_GE_SUPPLY_2025].

Table 6: High performance metal applications in aerospace, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Grows strongly | Discs, shafts, casings and rings for LEAP, GE9X, Trent and military engines | Triple melt superalloy bar and billet, powder for discs, sheet |
| Grows | Engine repair and overhaul | Welding wire, repair alloys |
| Grows | Additively manufactured combustor and nozzle parts | Superalloy powder |
| Stable | Combustor and wear parts where nickel alloys replace cobalt | Cobalt alloy sheet and plate |

*Sources: team assessment [AERO_AIRBUS_GMF2026_DELIVERIES, AERO_LEAP_2025, AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025].*

The shortage shows in the market leaders, who lock in supply and build captive capacity. ATI earned USD 1,762.9 million from commercial jet engines in 2025 under long term agreements with GE Aerospace, Safran, Rolls-Royce and Pratt and Whitney and quotes forging lead times of more than two years [AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025, CO_ATI_LEAD_2026]. Safran committed EUR 150 million to a 30,000 t press at Gennevilliers for about 14,000 hot section parts a year from 2029 [AERO_SAFRAN_GENNEVILLIERS_2026]. Beyond the United States, voestalpine Böhler in Austria holds about EUR 1 billion of aerospace orders for the next five years, Proterial in Japan sells superalloys for gas turbines, and Aperam entered the US specialty alloy market by buying Universal Stainless in 2025 for EUR 415 million [CO_VOEST_AERO_ORDERS_2025, CO_PROTERIAL_SUPERALLOY, CO_APERAM_UNIVERSAL_2025]. The window for a new qualified European source is therefore open today but narrows as these investments come on stream around 2030.

Implications for suppliers:

* Start part level qualifications with engine makers and independent forgers now, because rotating approvals take several years.
* Build the repair wire and powder business alongside, because off wing maintenance grows by 3.7 percent a year [AERO_AIRBUS_GSF2026_SERVICES_2045].
