## 5.4 Aerospace

Aerospace is a major source of growth for high-performance metals. Jet engine hot sections use nickel and cobalt superalloys, and suppliers face limits on how quickly they can melt and forge them.

### 5.4.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Airbus expects the passenger fleet to grow from 23,310 aircraft in 2025 to 45,550 in 2045. That forecast calls for 42,060 new aircraft. Boeing expects 43,625 deliveries using a broader definition [CO_AIRBUS_DELIV_2025, AERO_BOEING_CMO2026_FLEET_2035]. Engine output is growing faster than aircraft output. CFM delivered 1,802 LEAP engines in 2025, up 28 percent [CO_GE_CES_2025, AERO_LEAP_2025].

Maintenance and repair add another source of demand. Airbus projects that aviation services will grow from USD 209 billion in 2026 to USD 422 billion in 2045. Materials account for more than 85 percent of the cost of maintenance performed off the aircraft wing [AERO_AIRBUS_GMF2026_DELIVERIES].

Defense also adds to engine demand. World military expenditure reached USD 2,887 billion in 2025. GE delivered 635 defense engines, up from 490 a year earlier [CO_GE_CES_2025, AERO_SIPRI_MILEX_2025].

Cobalt provides the best measured indicator at the metal stage. The Cobalt Institute estimates that aviation used 11,499 t of the 20 kt of cobalt that went into superalloys in 2025. The U.S. Geological Survey reports that 51 percent of U.S. cobalt consumption goes into superalloys, mainly for aircraft engines. The tonnage of nickel alloys is not published [AERO_CI_AVIATION_CO_2025, AERO_USGS_CO_SUPER_SHARE].

### 5.4.2 Applications and Opportunities

Rotating parts have the highest entry requirements among the applications in Table 6. At plants such as ATI in Lockport and Special Metals in Huntington, disc and shaft billet goes through vacuum induction melting, electroslag remelting, and vacuum arc remelting. The most demanding discs use superalloy powder. Pratt and Whitney's geared turbofan powder metal issue showed how important powder quality is [AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025, CO_RTX_POWDER_2025, CO_SMC_HUN_EAF].

Engine makers specify approved melt routes in addition to AMS standards and NADCAP processes. GE Aerospace says that qualifying a new supplier takes substantial time. Initial approval for a rotating part can take several years [CO_GE_CES_2025].

Table 6: High performance metal applications in aerospace, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Grows strongly | Discs, shafts, casings and rings for LEAP, GE9X, Trent and military engines | Triple melt superalloy bar and billet, powder for discs, sheet |
| Grows | Engine repair and overhaul | Welding wire, repair alloys |
| Grows | Additively manufactured combustor and nozzle parts | Superalloy powder |
| Stable | Combustor and wear parts where nickel alloys replace cobalt | Cobalt alloy sheet and plate |

*Sources: team assessment [CO_AIRBUS_DELIV_2025, AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025, AERO_LEAP_2025].*

Major producers respond to shortages by signing supply agreements and building their own capacity. ATI earned USD 1,762.9 million from commercial jet engines in 2025 under long-term agreements with GE Aerospace, Safran, Rolls-Royce, and Pratt and Whitney. ATI reports forging lead times of more than two years [AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025, CO_ATI_LEAD_2026]. Safran committed EUR 150 million to a 30,000 t press at Gennevilliers to produce about 14,000 hot section parts a year from 2029 [AERO_SAFRAN_GENNEVILLIERS_2026].

Other suppliers are active as well. Austria's voestalpine Böhler has about EUR 1 billion of aerospace orders over five years. Japan's Proterial sells gas turbine superalloys. Aperam entered the U.S. specialty alloy market by buying Universal Stainless for EUR 415 million in 2025 [CO_APERAM_INVEST_2026, CO_PROTERIAL_SUPERALLOY, CO_VOEST_AERO_ORDERS_2025]. A new qualified European supplier has a chance to enter today, but that chance will narrow as the investments come online around 2030.

Implications for suppliers:

* Start part level qualifications with engine makers and independent forgers now, because rotating approvals take several years.
* Build the repair wire and powder business alongside, because off wing maintenance grows by 3.7 percent a year [AERO_AIRBUS_GMF2026_DELIVERIES].
