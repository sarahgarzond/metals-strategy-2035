## 5.4 Aerospace

Aerospace is a major growth source for high-performance metals. Jet engine hot sections use nickel and cobalt superalloys, which suppliers cannot melt and forge fast enough.

### 5.4.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Airbus expects the passenger fleet to grow from 23,310 aircraft in 2025 to 45,550 in 2045, needing 42,060 new aircraft, and Boeing expects 43,625 deliveries on a broader definition [AERO_AIRBUS_GMF2026_FLEET_2025, AERO_AIRBUS_GMF2026_DELIVERIES, BOEING_CMO_2026]. Engine output grows faster, and CFM delivered 1,802 LEAP engines in 2025, up 28 percent [CO_GE_CES_2025, AERO_LEAP_2025].

Maintenance and defence add further demand. Airbus projects aviation services to grow from USD 209 billion in 2026 to USD 422 billion in 2045, with materials driving over 85 percent of off-wing maintenance [AERO_AIRBUS_GSF2026_SERVICES_2026, AERO_AIRBUS_GSF2026_SERVICES_2045]. GE delivered 635 defence engines in 2025, up from 490 [CO_GE_CES_2025].

Cobalt is the best measured indicator. The Cobalt Institute estimates that aviation used 11,499 t of the 20 kt of cobalt in superalloys in 2025, and the USGS reports that 51 percent of US cobalt goes into superalloys, mainly for engines. Nickel alloy tonnage is not published [AERO_CI_AVIATION_CO_2025, AERO_USGS_CO_SUPER_SHARE].

### 5.4.2 Applications and Opportunities

Rotating parts set the highest entry bar in Table 6. At ATI in Lockport and Special Metals in Huntington and New Hartford, disc and shaft billet passes through vacuum induction melting, electroslag remelting, and vacuum arc remelting. The most demanding discs use powder, whose quality Pratt and Whitney's geared turbofan issue put in focus [AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025, CO_RTX_POWDER_2025, CO_SMC_HUN_EAF, CO_SMC_NH_VIM]. Engine makers also specify approved melt routes, and GE Aerospace says first approval for a rotating part can take several years [CO_GE_CES_2025].

Table 6: High performance metal applications in aerospace, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Grows strongly | Discs, shafts, casings and rings for LEAP, GE9X, Trent and military engines | Triple melt superalloy bar and billet, powder for discs, sheet |
| Grows | Engine repair and overhaul | Welding wire, repair alloys |
| Grows | Additively manufactured combustor and nozzle parts | CoCr and nickel superalloy powder |
| Stable | Combustor and wear parts, with nickel alloys replacing some cobalt grades | Cobalt alloy sheet and plate |

*Sources: team assessment [CO_AIRBUS_DELIV_2025, AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025, AERO_LEAP_2025].*

Producers answer shortages with supply agreements and new capacity. ATI earned USD 1,762.9 million from commercial jet engines in 2025 under long-term agreements with GE Aerospace, Safran, Rolls-Royce, and Pratt and Whitney [AERO_ATI_JETENG_2025]. It reports forging lead times above two years [CO_ATI_LEAD_2026].

The buyers of billet fall into two groups. Integrated groups melt for their own forges and foundries. PCC makes castings, forgings and alloys in all mill forms, and Howmet makes alloy and billet at Glossop and Ickles in England [CO_PCC_PROFILE_2025, CO_HWM_SITES_2025]. Forgers without a melt shop buy on the open market. Otto Fuchs forges Inconel 718 engine parts in Meinerzhagen, and Safran committed EUR 150 million to a 30,000 t press at Gennevilliers for about 14,000 hot section parts a year from 2029 [CO_OTTOFUCHS_NI, AERO_SAFRAN_GENNEVILLIERS_2026]. Casting stock is a more closed market, because PCC Airfoils counts Cannon-Muskegon, which melts nickel and cobalt casting alloys, among its operations, and Doncasters melts its own vacuum superalloys [CO_PCCAIRFOILS_OPS, CO_CANNON_MUSKEGON_VM, CO_DONCASTERS_VIM].

Competitors are expanding too, such as voestalpine Böhler Aerospace in Austria, which forges nickel base engine discs and holds about EUR 1 billion of orders over five years. Aperam bought Universal Stainless for an enterprise value of USD 537 million in January 2025 [CO_BOHLER_AERO_DISCS, CO_VOEST_AERO_ORDERS_2025, CO_APERAM_USAP_EV_2025]. In China, Western Superconducting's superalloy unit raised first half 2025 revenue 83 percent to RMB 282 million, while superalloy and special steel maker Fushun Special Steel lost RMB 805 million in 2025. A market report puts national output near 64.5 kt [CO_WST_SEGMENTS_2025, CO_FUSHUN_REVENUE_2025, CO_BAOWU_CHINA_SUPERALLOY_2025]. A new qualified European supplier can still enter, but that window narrows as this capacity arrives around 2030.

Implications for suppliers:

* Start part level qualifications with engine makers and open market forgers such as Otto Fuchs now, because rotating approvals take several years.
* Build the repair wire and powder business alongside, because off wing maintenance grows by 3.7 percent a year [AERO_AIRBUS_GSF2026_SERVICES_2045].
