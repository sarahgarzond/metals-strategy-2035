# 9. Conclusion

The choice is the same one Chapter 1 stated, and it is repeated here only so the close matches the opening. Qualify aerospace billet, approved sheet and repair wire, and qualify chemical plate and welding products only where a named grade clears the capacity rule. Qualify electrical strip and wire only in the code that grade ships in. Keep oil and gas where the company's own approval supports the margin, and harvest automotive accounts that are already profitable. Treat electrolysers, powders and medical implant bar as options against a financed programme or a regulatory path the company holds. Stop glow plugs and the diesel only set, undifferentiated stainless, China implant tenders, and any powder or electrolyser spend without a financed buyer.

The scale behind that choice is Table 2, not a specialty reading of one trade code. Mine production and primary use are millions of tonnes of nickel, stainless melt is tens of millions of tonnes of steel, and EU flat apparent consumption of 57.8 million kilograms in 2024 is the ceiling of a mixed code that still joins pure nickel and nickel alloy [NI_USGS_MINE_W_2025, NI_WS_MELT_2025, AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024]. That ceiling is not qualified alloy demand and it is not room under the Acerinox finish figure. Acerinox High Performance Alloys finished 47 thousand tonnes in 2025, worldwide, with VDM and Haynes counted once, and that figure is output rather than a European capacity ceiling [NI_ACX_HPA_2025]. Flat product is a net export of the mixed code, and bar is a logged net import that is still a rounded estimate, so the bar test is the client's own book and not a case to add capacity [AC_EU_NI_BAR_2024, TE_NI_BAR_AC_BAND_2024].

The 2035 indices stay in Chapter 7 and are not converted into tonnes, because the 2025 share of each priority inside the relevant code is unknown until the shipment map. The first 24 months use the trade result and the stop list, cap the qualification list, and release finishing capacity only against a repeat order that still clears the downside. Internal sales, margins, approvals and nameplate by plant are still required before those gates become a capital request, and they are not a reason to reopen the ranking.
