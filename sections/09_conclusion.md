# 9. Conclusion

The report asked how the production, availability, trade and industrial uses of selected metals shape opportunities in high performance materials through 2035, and the answer is that value lies in conversion and qualification rather than in metal volume. Raw materials are plentiful in nickel and policy constrained in cobalt and tungsten, Germany earns its trade surplus only after conversion, and the industries that pay most for qualified conversion are aerospace and energy technology, whose base case demand rises by about half by 2035.

The company should therefore concentrate investment on vacuum melted superalloy bar, billet, repair wire and powder for engine makers and turbine builders in Europe and the United States, defend its chemical, precision, medical and sour service franchises with targeted sales, and treat fuel cells, nuclear and electrolysers as options released by customer milestones. The central limitation of this study is the absence of internal sales, margin, approval and capacity data, so the next step is to validate the priorities against that evidence and convert the strongest opportunities into qualified orders.
