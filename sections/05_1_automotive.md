## 5.1 Automotive

Electrification cuts demand for the heat-resistant nickel alloys of combustion vehicles, because electric cars need no turbochargers, exhaust sensors, or glow plugs.

### 5.1.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

The world produced 96.4 million motor vehicles in 2025, 34.5 million of them in China. EU car registrations reached 10.8 million, 17 percent below the 2019 level [ACEA_CAR_REG_2025, OICA_CHINA_PROD_2025].

Over 20 million electric cars were sold in 2025, about a quarter of the market. The IEA expects about half of global sales by 2035 and more than 90 percent in China and the EU [IEA_GEVO_CN_CARS_2035]. Batteries do not replace the lost engine alloy nickel, because lithium iron phosphate cathodes contain none and held over 55 percent of the 2025 market [IEA_GEVO_CN_CARS_2035].

European policy may slow the shift. In October 2026, two European Parliament committees backed a 95 percent cut in new car CO2 emissions by 2035, or 87.5 percent with low-carbon steel credits, ahead of a December plenary vote [AUTO_EU_COMMITTEE_VOTE_2026].

The best nickel measure covers battery cathodes, at 439 kt in 2025 and 1,294 kt to 1,536 kt in 2035, while declining engine alloy tonnage is not published [IEA_CM_CO_DEMAND_2025_2035].

### 5.1.2 Applications and Opportunities

Hybrid and gasoline engines keep turbochargers in production for another decade, after close to 50 million units in 2025. Garrett expects output to fall toward its 2022 level by 2030 and the turbo share of gasoline engines to peak at 54 percent in 2032 [CO_GARRETT_GAS_PEN_2032]. BorgWarner won three gasoline turbocharger programs in October 2026, starting in 2027 [CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026].

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Grows | Current sensors and magnetic shielding in electric drives and chargers | Soft magnetic FeNi strip |
| Stable to 2030 | Turbocharger parts for hybrids and gasoline engines | Heat resistant nickel alloy bar and strip |
| Falls | Glow plugs, diesel exhaust parts, valves and injectors | Nickel alloys, heat resistant stainless strip |

*Sources: team assessment [CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026, CO_GARRETT_GAS_PEN_2032, IEA_GEVO_CN_CARS_2035].*

Implications for suppliers:

* Treat engine alloys for cars as a cash business to 2030 and avoid new capacity for diesel only parts.
* Shift product development toward soft magnetic FeNi strip for sensors and shielding in electric drives.
