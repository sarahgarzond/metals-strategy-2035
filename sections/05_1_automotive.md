## 5.1 Automotive

Automotive is a large but shrinking market for the company, because most of its car alloys go into engine and exhaust parts that electrification removes. The remaining value sits in hybrids and North American gasoline engines in the medium term and in soft magnetic alloys for electric drives in the long term.

### 5.1.1 Market Size and Electrification to 2035

The world built 96.4 million motor vehicles in 2025, up from 92.7 million in 2024, and China alone built 34.5 million, or about 36 percent [OICA_VEH_PROD_2025, OICA_CHINA_PROD_2025]. Europe is not growing in volume, since EU car registrations of 10.8 million in 2025 were still 17 percent below 2019 [ACEA_EU_CAR_REG_2025]. Electric cars, meaning battery electric and plug in hybrid models, exceeded 20 million sales in 2025 and reached 25 percent of new car sales [IEA_GEVO_EV_SALES_2025, IEA_GEVO_EV_SHARE_2025].

The regional split matters more than the world average, because electric cars exceed 90 percent of sales in China and the EU by 2035 while internal combustion sales never return to their 2017 peak in any IEA scenario [IEA_GEVO_EV_SHARE_2035]. Lithium iron phosphate, which contains no nickel, already took over 55 percent of electric vehicle batteries in 2025, so even the battery does not compensate for lost engine alloy [IEA_GEVO_LFP_2025].

### 5.1.2 Key Applications and Opportunities for the Company

Nickel alloys in cars serve turbochargers, exhaust sensors, spark and glow plugs, gaskets and catalyst foil, and eight of the eleven applications in VDM's published automotive benchmark depend on the combustion engine [REF_VDM_METALS_N_D_A]. Garrett names a nickel, iron and chromium alloy among its main raw materials and expects gasoline turbo penetration to peak at 54 percent in 2032, while BorgWarner won three gasoline turbocharger programmes in South America and Europe in October 2026 with production from 2027 [CO_GARRETT_GAS_PEN_2032, CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026]. Höganäs, the Swedish powder maker, saw its powder volume fall from 412 kt to 393 kt in 2025 on restrained automotive demand, which shows the same pressure in powder metallurgy parts.

The buyers with filed evidence are Garrett and BorgWarner, and the competing mills are Aperam Alloys Imphy in FeNi strip and Special Metals and Carpenter in engine alloys. The profit pool is shrinking rather than moving, which is why automotive should finance the company's growth areas rather than receive new capacity.

What the company should do:

* Keep supplying turbocharger, exhaust and sensor makers through 2030, with priority for North American gasoline and hybrid platforms.
* Grow soft magnetic FeNi strip for current sensors and shielding in electric drives with European Tier 1 suppliers.
* Exit diesel only parts such as glow plugs first, because their volume falls fastest after 2027.
