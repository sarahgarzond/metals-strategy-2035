## 5.1 Automotive

Automotive is a shrinking market for the company. Most of its car alloys go into engine and exhaust parts. By 2035, about half of new cars will be electric, so fewer cars need these parts. The one growing area is magnetic alloys for sensors in electric cars.

### 5.1.1 Historical and Current Market Overview

In 2025, the world built 96.4 million vehicles and sold 77.6 million cars. China alone built 36% of all vehicles (OICA, 2026; ACEA, 2026).

About a quarter of new cars sold in 2025 were electric. The IEA expects about half by 2035, and more than 90% in China and Europe (IEA, 2026a). As a result, the number of cars without a plug falls by about a quarter (Table 1).

Table 1: Car sales, 2025 and 2035

| Measure | 2025 | 2035 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cars sold (million) | 77.6 | 85.7 | +10% |
| Share electric | about 25% | about 50% | +25 points |
| Cars without a plug (million) | 58.2 | 42.9 | -26% |

*Sources: ACEA (2026), IEA (2026a). 2035 values are our estimate, assuming car sales grow 1% a year.*

In October 2026, two EU Parliament committees voted to soften the 2035 rule for new cars. If the full Parliament agrees in December, some petrol and hybrid cars could still be sold after 2035 (electrive, 2026).

### 5.1.2 Key Applications and Opportunities for the Company

The company sells nickel alloys, stainless steel and catalyst foil to car parts makers. Eight of its eleven car applications depend on the engine (Table 2).

Table 2: The company's car applications, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| Grows | Sensors and magnetic shielding in electric cars | Nickel-iron alloys |
| Stable | Airbag parts; catalyst foil | Nickel alloys; iron-chromium-aluminium foil |
| Falls | Spark and glow plugs, valves, fuel injectors, turbocharger parts, exhaust parts and sensors | Nickel alloys; heat-resistant stainless steel |

*Source: VDM Metals (n.d.-a). The outlook is our assessment.*

Turbochargers are the most important of these. About 50 million were built in 2025, and newer designs use more alloy per unit, which slows the decline (Garrett Motion, 2026).

The main buyers are Garrett Motion, BorgWarner, Niterra, Eberspächer and Autoliv (Autoliv, 2026; BorgWarner, 2026; Eberspächer, 2026; Niterra, n.d.). The main competitors are Special Metals, Carpenter Technology and Aperam (Aperam, n.d.; Carpenter Technology, 2026; Special Metals, n.d.).

What the company should do:

* Keep supplying turbocharger, exhaust and sensor makers while petrol cars are still sold, especially in North America.
* Grow sales of magnetic alloys for sensors in electric cars.
* Avoid diesel-only parts, such as glow plugs, which fall fastest.
