## 5.1 Automotive

The car industry is the clearest example of a market in which high performance alloys are losing ground, because electrification removes precisely the components that consume them. Turbochargers, exhaust sensors, glow plugs and gaskets have been steady outlets for heat resistant nickel alloys, but none of them exists in a battery electric car.

### 5.1.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Vehicle output is still growing, yet the growth has shifted toward electric cars and toward China. The world built 96.4 million motor vehicles in 2025, up from 92.7 million in 2024, and China alone built 34.5 million, about 36 percent of the total, while EU car registrations of 10.8 million remained 17 percent below their 2019 level [OICA_VEH_PROD_2025, OICA_CHINA_PROD_2025, ACEA_EU_CAR_REG_2025]. Electric cars exceeded 20 million sales in 2025, a quarter of the market, and the IEA expects about half of global car sales and more than 90 percent in China and the EU to be electric by 2035 [IEA_GEVO_EV_SALES_2025, IEA_GEVO_EV_SHARE_2025, IEA_GEVO_EV_SHARE_2035, IEA_GEVO_CN_CARS_2035]. Even the battery offers little compensation, since lithium iron phosphate cathodes, which contain no nickel, already took over 55 percent of electric vehicle batteries in 2025 [IEA_GEVO_LFP_2025].

### 5.1.2 Applications and Opportunities

The shrinking market is not disappearing overnight, because hybrids and gasoline engines in North America and emerging markets keep turbochargers in production for another decade. Turbocharger output reached close to 50 million units in 2025, and Garrett expects it to fall back toward the 2022 level by 2030 while gasoline turbo penetration peaks at 54 percent in 2032 [CO_GARRETT_TURBOMKT_2025, CO_GARRETT_GAS_PEN_2032]. BorgWarner still won three gasoline turbocharger programmes in October 2026 with production from 2027, [CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026], whereas Höganäs saw its metal powder volume fall from 412 kt to 393 kt in 2025 on restrained automotive demand [CO_HOGANAS_VOL_2025].

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Grows | Current sensors and magnetic shielding in electric drives and chargers | Soft magnetic FeNi strip |
| Stable to 2030 | Turbocharger parts for hybrids and gasoline engines | Heat resistant nickel alloy bar and strip |
| Falls | Glow plugs, diesel exhaust parts, valves and injectors | Nickel alloys, heat resistant stainless strip |

*Source: team assessment based on Garrett Motion, BorgWarner and IEA evidence [CO_GARRETT_TURBOMKT_2025, CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026, IEA_GEVO_EV_SHARE_2035].*

Implications for suppliers:

* Treat engine alloys for cars as a cash business to 2030 and avoid new capacity for diesel only parts.
* Shift product development toward soft magnetic FeNi strip for sensors and shielding in electric drives.
