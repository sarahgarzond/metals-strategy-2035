## 5.1 Automotive

Electrification reduces demand for the heat-resistant nickel alloys used in combustion vehicles. Fully electric cars do not use the same turbochargers, exhaust sensors, glow plugs, and gaskets.

### 5.1.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

The world produced 96.4 million motor vehicles in 2025. China produced 34.5 million of them. EU car registrations reached 10.8 million, which was 17 percent below the 2019 level [ACEA_CAR_REG_2025, OICA_CHINA_PROD_2025].

More than 20 million electric cars were sold in 2025, about a quarter of the market. By 2035, the IEA expects electric cars to make up about half of global sales and more than 90 percent in China and the EU [IEA_GEVO_CN_CARS_2035]. The shift does not replace all nickel demand lost from engine alloys. Lithium iron phosphate cathodes contain no nickel and accounted for over 55 percent of electric vehicle batteries in 2025 [IEA_GEVO_CN_CARS_2035].

European policy may slow the shift. In October 2026, two European Parliament committees supported a 95 percent cut in new car CO2 emissions by 2035, or 87.5 percent with credits for low-carbon steel. A plenary vote was due in December 2026 [AUTO_EU_COMMITTEE_VOTE_2026].

The best available nickel measure covers battery cathodes. The IEA estimates electric vehicle nickel demand of 439 kt in 2025 and 1,294 kt to 1,536 kt in 2035. Demand for engine alloys is declining, but public data do not show its tonnage [IEA_CM_CO_DEMAND_2025_2035].

### 5.1.2 Applications and Opportunities

Hybrid and gasoline engines are expected to keep turbochargers in production for another decade. Production reached close to 50 million units in 2025. Garrett expects output to fall toward its 2022 level by 2030 and the share of gasoline engines with turbochargers to peak at 54 percent in 2032 [CO_GARRETT_GAS_PEN_2032]. BorgWarner won three gasoline turbocharger programs in October 2026, with production starting in 2027 [CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026].

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |

| Grows | Current sensors and magnetic shielding in electric drives and chargers | Soft magnetic FeNi strip |
| Stable to 2030 | Turbocharger parts for hybrids and gasoline engines | Heat resistant nickel alloy bar and strip |

*Sources: team assessment [CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026, CO_GARRETT_GAS_PEN_2032, IEA_GEVO_CN_CARS_2035].*

Implications for suppliers:

* Treat engine alloys for cars as a cash business to 2030 and avoid new capacity for diesel only parts.
* Shift product development toward soft magnetic FeNi strip for sensors and shielding in electric drives.
