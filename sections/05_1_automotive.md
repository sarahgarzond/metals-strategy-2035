## 5.1 Automotive

The car industry is the clearest case of a market in which high performance alloys lose ground, because electrification removes the turbochargers, exhaust sensors, glow plugs and gaskets that have long consumed heat resistant nickel alloys.

### 5.1.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Vehicle output still grows but shifts toward China and electric cars, as the world built 96.4 million motor vehicles in 2025, 34.5 million of them in China, while EU car registrations of 10.8 million stayed 17 percent below their 2019 level [OICA_VEH_PROD_2025, OICA_CHINA_PROD_2025, ACEA_EU_CAR_REG_2025]. Electric cars exceeded 20 million sales in 2025, a quarter of the market, and the IEA expects about half of global car sales and more than 90 percent in China and the EU to be electric by 2035 [IEA_GEVO_EV_SALES_2025, IEA_GEVO_EV_SHARE_2025, IEA_GEVO_EV_SHARE_2035, IEA_GEVO_CN_CARS_2035]. The battery offers little compensation, since lithium iron phosphate cathodes, which contain no nickel, took over 55 percent of electric vehicle batteries in 2025 [IEA_GEVO_LFP_2025].

European policy may soften the shift, since in October 2026 the European Parliament's industry and transport committees backed a 95 percent cut in new car CO2 emissions for 2035, or 87.5 percent with credits for European low carbon steel, ahead of a plenary vote expected in December 2026 [AUTO_EU_COMMITTEE_VOTE_2026]. The best measured metal figure is therefore a battery figure, IEA nickel demand from electric vehicles of 439 kt in 2025 and 1,294 kt to 1,536 kt in 2035, which is cathode material, while engine alloy tonnage is unmeasured and declining [IEA_CM_NI_EV_2025_2035].

### 5.1.2 Applications and Opportunities

Engine alloys decline slowly, because hybrids and gasoline engines keep turbochargers in production for another decade. Turbocharger output reached close to 50 million units in 2025, and Garrett expects it to fall back toward the 2022 level by 2030 while gasoline turbo penetration peaks at 54 percent in 2032 [CO_GARRETT_TURBOMKT_2025, CO_GARRETT_GAS_PEN_2032]. BorgWarner still won three gasoline turbocharger programmes in October 2026 with production from 2027 [CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026].

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Grows | Current sensors and magnetic shielding in electric drives and chargers | Soft magnetic FeNi strip |
| Stable to 2030 | Turbocharger parts for hybrids and gasoline engines | Heat resistant nickel alloy bar and strip |
| Falls | Glow plugs, diesel exhaust parts, valves and injectors | Nickel alloys, heat resistant stainless strip |

*Sources: team assessment [CO_GARRETT_TURBOMKT_2025, CO_BORGWARNER_Q3_2026, IEA_GEVO_EV_SHARE_2035].*

Implications for suppliers:

* Treat engine alloys for cars as a cash business to 2030 and avoid new capacity for diesel only parts.
* Shift product development toward soft magnetic FeNi strip for sensors and shielding in electric drives.
