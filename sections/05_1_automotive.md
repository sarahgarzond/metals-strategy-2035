## 5.1 Automotive

Automotive is a harvest market for engine and exhaust parts, because the number of cars without a plug falls in the team estimate while a car with a plug can still carry an engine. Table 3 takes world car registrations from 77.6 million in 2025 to 85.7 million in 2035 and cars without a plug from 58.2 million to 42.9 million, which is a loss of about a quarter of that set and not the loss of every engine part [ACEA_CAR_REG_2025, TE_AUTO_UPSIDE_2035]. Magnetic sensors and magnetic alloys are not counted again here, because section 5.0 assigned them to electronics so the scenario model would not count them twice. The applications below are therefore the engine and exhaust book, and the close keeps only the accounts that are already profitable.

### 5.1.1 Historical and Current Market Overview

In 2025 the world built 96.4 million motor vehicles, China built 34.53 million of them, and passenger car registrations were 77.6 million [OICA_VEH_PROD_2025, OICA_CHINA_PROD_2025, ACEA_CAR_REG_2025]. Those are activity totals for the vehicle industry, and they are not tonnes of nickel alloy, so they sit beside the basket in Table 2 only as the driver of the component set.

The 2035 car figures in Table 3 are a team estimate that holds the electric share near half of new cars and lets registrations grow by 1 percent a year, and the October 2026 committee vote on the European rule is left out of that estimate because it is not a logged value row. A hybrid can still need a turbocharger, a valve or an exhaust part, which is why the upside in Chapter 7 is no longer a flat zero [TE_AUTO_UPSIDE_2035]. The table is the physical base for that rate, and the applications that follow say which of those parts are worth harvesting.

Table 3: Car sales, 2025 and 2035

| Measure | 2025 | 2035 | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cars sold (million) | 77.6 | 85.7 | +10 percent |
| Share electric | about 25 percent | about 50 percent | team estimate |
| Cars without a plug (million) | 58.2 | 42.9 | -26 percent |

*Sources: Registrations in 2025 are taken from ACEA [ACEA_CAR_REG_2025], and the 2035 column together with the cars without a plug is a team estimate that applies the shares in this table to a 1 percent a year path rather than an official forecast [TE_AUTO_UPSIDE_2035].*

### 5.1.2 Key Applications and Opportunities for the Company

The benchmark application, from the VDM Metals portfolio, covers nickel alloys, stainless and catalyst foil in turbochargers, valves, spark plugs, glow plugs, exhaust parts and airbag components. What Münvest would need before this is an order is its own grade, form, approval and buyer, and shipment, approval and share are unconfirmed. Eight of the eleven catalogue lines sit on the engine and exhaust, and that count is a reading of the benchmark rather than a count of Münvest approvals.

Garrett Motion reported net sales of USD 3,584 million in 2025, with gasoline at USD 1,592 million and diesel at USD 837 million, and BorgWarner reported Turbos and Thermal sales of USD 5,772 million [CO_GARRETT_SALES_2025, CO_BORGWARNER_TURBOSEG_2025]. Those figures name the buyers and the year, and they do not name the alloy mill or the tonnes. The contest in European spark plug and sensor strip is Aperam Alloys, Proterial, Nippon Yakin and Acerinox High Performance Alloys, counted once, while Carpenter and Special Metals are minor in that European strip [CO_APERAM_AS_2025, NI_ACX_HPA_2025].

What the company should do: the company should keep and harvest automotive accounts that are already profitable, including turbocharger and exhaust business while cars without a plug are still built, and it should take glow plugs and the rest of the diesel only set off the growth list now. Magnetic alloys for sensors are pursued in section 5.6 rather than here, because that assignment stops the scenario model from counting the same component twice.
