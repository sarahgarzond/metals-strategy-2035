## 2.7 Additive manufacturing powders and welding consumables

Additive manufacturing (AM) powders and welding consumables are the two product families in which the Client's nickel and cobalt alloy know how leaves the mill as a processing material and not as a semifinished part. The evidence is thinner than for the bulk alloys, because neither family has its own customs code or an official production statistic, so this section combines trade data, company reports and clearly labelled vendor estimates.

### Production

Because no agency publishes AM powder output, production has to be read from the producers themselves. Carpenter Additive operates seven atomisation sites and reported about 4,000 t per year at Torshälla in Sweden, although that plant mainly serves powder metallurgy steels and not only AM grades [CARPENTER_TORSHALLA]. Nickel superalloy powders depend on vacuum melted feedstock, and ATI will start a fifth vacuum induction melting furnace in Monroe, North Carolina, with 9 kt per year from the second half of 2027 [ARGUS_SUPERALLOY]. For welding consumables, Lincoln Electric sold about USD 2.28bn of consumables in 2025 [LECO_2025_CONS] and ESAB reached total sales of USD 2.84bn [ESAB_2025_SALES]. ### Trade

Since filler metals move under the heading for coated electrodes and cored wires, HS 8311 is the best available proxy. The reporting countries exported goods worth USD 3.7bn in 2023 [COMTRADE_8311_X_2023], up from USD 3.1bn in 2019 [COMTRADE_8311_X_2019], which is about 4.3% per year (team estimate). China shipped USD 966m or 585.6 kt, which is 53% of world export weight but only 26% of value, whereas Germany exported USD 159m at a much higher unit value [COMTRADE_8311_X_2023]. The largest importers were the USA with USD 238m, China with USD 222m and Mexico with USD 216m [COMTRADE_8311_M_2023].

For powders, HS 7504 covers all nickel powders and flakes, so it overstates AM demand but shows the main corridors. Exports of USD 1.1bn in 2023 were led by Canada with USD 357m, followed by the UK and the USA [COMTRADE_7504_X_2023]. Imports of USD 1.8bn went mainly to Korea with USD 463m and China with USD 354m [COMTRADE_7504_M_2023], which reflects battery and electronics chains more than AM. Value grew from USD 950m in 2019 [COMTRADE_7504_X_2019], about 3.8% per year (team estimate). Copper and aluminium powder flows were not usable because Malaysia appears with implausible volumes (TODO(data): clean HS 7406 and 7603 or use Eurostat PRODCOM).

### Consumption

Given these flows, demand sits where aerospace, medical and energy hardware is built. One paid report puts aerospace at 39% of metal AM powder spend [GMI_AMPOWDER_2025], and a second report assigns 35.7% of nickel alloy AM powder to North America and 28.4% to Europe [MARKETINTELO_NIAM_2025]. For welding consumables, a vendor summary places about 70% of consumption in Asia Pacific and about 14% in Europe [WELD_MKT_2024]. Both regional splits come from paid summaries and serve only as triangulation.

### Applications

Since the property demanded decides the alloy, the application map is short. Alloys 718 and 625 powders are printed into combustor and turbine hardware because they hold creep strength at high temperature, CoCr powders are printed into implants for their wear and biocompatibility, and copper alloys such as CuCrZr serve thrust chambers and heat exchangers for conductivity with strength. Stainless powders such as 316L serve tooling and valves for corrosion resistance. Welding wires and flux coated electrodes in alloy 625 and C-276 are used for overlay cladding and dissimilar joints in oil and gas, chemical and power plants, because the weld must match the corrosion resistance of the base metal.

### Market size

With that scope, the AM market is easier to size than the filler market. Wohlers Associates counted global AM revenue of USD 21.9bn in 2024 [WOHLERS_2025] and USD 24.2bn in 2025, which is 10.9% growth [WOHLERS_2026]. Materials of all kinds were USD 4.4bn in 2024 [WOHLERS_2025_MAT] and USD 4.9bn in 2025 [WOHLERS_2026_MAT], but this includes polymers, so metal powder is a fraction (TODO(data): metal share of AM materials). Vendor reports for metal powder alone give USD 650.8m for 2025 [GMI_AMPOWDER_2025] and USD 555.3m for 2024 [TMR_AMPOWDER_2035], while a nickel alloy powder estimate of USD 1.2bn [MARKETINTELO_NIAM_2025] contradicts them. We therefore treat USD 0.65bn to 4.9bn as a bracket and do not state a headline value.

For welding consumables, the figures reported by listed companies give a firmer floor than the vendor estimates. Lincoln's consumables of USD 2.28bn [LECO_2025_CONS] and ESAB's total sales of USD 2.84bn [ESAB_2025_SALES] sit inside a vendor range of USD 12.7bn to 17.7bn for the whole market [WELD_MKT_2024]. ### Prices

Because powder is priced per kilogram on top of alloy cost, conversion dominates. Gas atomised alloy 718 powder was quoted at USD 80 to 260 per kg in 2025 depending on lot size, with supplier tables reaching USD 485 per kg, against USD 45 to 110 per kg for mill products, so powder costs two to five times the bar price [INCO718_POWDER_PRICE_2025]. These quotes come from supplier blogs with a sales interest, so their confidence is low. Lincoln attributes its 2025 consumable growth to pricing while volumes stayed steady [LECO_2025_CONS]. ### Capacity and supply

Because prices reward scale, the capacity decisions of the main producers show who is committed to the market. Carpenter invested USD 40m of a planned USD 52m in its Athens, Alabama center that atomises, prints and finishes in one site [CARPENTER_TORSHALLA]. Oerlikon moved AM production from Barleben and Shanghai to the United States during 2024 and cited adverse market conditions in Germany [OERLIKON_AM_2023]. Acerinox completed the Haynes purchase in November 2024 and joined it with VDM Metals into one high performance alloys division [ACX_HAYNES], which creates a direct competitor for the Client. Raw material concentration adds risk, since Indonesia mines about 67% of nickel [USGS_NI_MINE_2025] and the DR Congo about 73% of cobalt [USGS_CO_MINE_2025].

### Outlook 2035

Considering supply and demand together, vendor forecasts of USD 2.9bn [GMI_AMPOWDER_2025] and USD 3.9bn [TMR_AMPOWDER_2035] by 2035 imply 16% to 19.5% growth per year, which looks high next to total AM growth of 9.1% in 2024 [WOHLERS_2025] and 10.9% in 2025 [WOHLERS_2026]. Wohlers describes uneven growth by segment, with printing services growing fastest and machine sales slowest [WOHLERS_2026]. The team base case should therefore sit between total AM growth and the vendor rates (TODO(data): base case value set in model.py). Welding consumables should follow industrial output and pricing, and ESAB guides core organic growth of 2% to 4% for 2026 [ESAB_2025_SALES]. Aircraft build rates from Airbus and Boeing, already recorded in the model, remain the strongest driver for aerospace powder and filler demand.

### Interesting facts

1. In December 2023 Oerlikon announced it would consolidate AM powder and part production in the US and moved its Barleben and Shanghai output during 2024, citing weak German demand [OERLIKON_AM_2023].
2. In November 2024 Acerinox closed the Haynes acquisition and combined it with VDM Metals, which puts two nickel and cobalt alloy makers for strip, sheet, bar, wire and powder under one owner [ACX_HAYNES].
3. From the second half of 2027 ATI adds a fifth VIM furnace in Monroe with 9 kt per year of nickel superalloy and titanium for jet engines [ARGUS_SUPERALLOY].
4. In 2023 China exported 585.6 kt of coated electrodes and wires, 53% of world export weight but 26% of value, while Germany exported USD 159m at far higher unit value [COMTRADE_8311_X_2023].
