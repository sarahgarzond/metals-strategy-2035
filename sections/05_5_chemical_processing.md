## 5.5 Chemical Processing

Chemical processing has long used corrosion-resistant alloys, but European demand now comes mainly from maintenance while new plants rise in Asia.

### 5.5.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Europe's chemical industry produces less and has spare capacity, with EU27 sales of about EUR 635 billion in 2024. Europe's share of world sales fell from 21 to 13 percent since 2009, while China's rose from 24 to 46 percent [CEFIC_EU_SHARE_2024, CEFIC_CN_SHARE_2024]. EU chemical output fell 3.2 percent in 2025, and utilization was 75.0 percent in the third quarter of 2026, against a long-term 81.3 percent [CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026, EUROSTAT_C20_EU27_2025].

New investment follows that growth to Asia. BASF expects Asia Pacific to provide over 80 percent of chemical growth by 2035 and opened its Zhanjiang Verbund site in November 2025 [BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035]. The closest metal stage measure is EU use of nickel flat products, 57.8 kt in 2024 across all end uses, with an unknown chemical share [AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024].

### 5.5.2 Applications and Opportunities

Corrosion-resistant alloys are needed where stainless steel fails, as in sulfuric acid, hydrochloric acid, or chlor-alkali service. In Table 7, European demand is mainly replacement, while new plants and clad plate move to Asia. Alleima is investing SEK 220 million in finishing at Mehsana, India, where its range includes Sanicro 625 (UNS N06625) tube of 6 to 42 mm outside diameter [MO_ALLEIMA_625_MO, CO_ALLEIMA_MEHSANA_2026].

Table 7: High performance metal applications in chemical processing, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Stable | Replacement heat exchangers, vessels and piping in European plants | C-276, C-22 and 59 type NiCrMo alloys and Alloy 625 as plate, sheet and welding consumables |
| Grows | New plants in China, India and Southeast Asia | Plate, clad plate, zirconium plate and tube |
| Selective | Battery material plants, where titanium and linings dominate | Plate for selected duties, welding consumables |
| Falls | New build in European base chemicals | Plate |

*Sources: team assessment [CO_ALLEIMA_MEHSANA_2026, BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035, CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026].*

Implications for suppliers:

* Harvest European replacement demand: sell plate and welding consumables from existing capacity on documented life cycle cost.
* Serve Asian EPC tenders only from existing plate capacity, without new investment.
