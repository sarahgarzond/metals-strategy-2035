## 5.5 Chemical Processing

Chemical processing has long used corrosion-resistant alloys. In Europe, demand now comes mainly from maintenance. Much new plant construction takes place in Asia.

### 5.5.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Europe's chemical industry is producing less and has spare capacity. EU27 chemical sales were about EUR 635 billion in 2024. Europe's share of world sales fell from 21 percent in 2009 to 13 percent, while China's rose from 24 to 46 percent [CEFIC_EU_SHARE_2024, CEFIC_CN_SHARE_2024]. The EU chemical production index fell 3.2 percent in 2025. Capacity utilization was 75.0 percent in the third quarter of 2026, compared with a long-term average of 81.3 percent [CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026, EUROSTAT_C20_EU27_2025].

Investment is following growth to Asia. BASF expects Asia Pacific to account for over 80 percent of chemical industry growth by 2035. It opened its Zhanjiang Verbund site in November 2025 [BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035]. The closest measure at the metal stage is EU apparent consumption of nickel plate, sheet, strip, and foil. This was 57.8 kt in 2024 across all end uses. The share used in chemical processing is unknown [AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024].

### 5.5.2 Applications and Opportunities

Corrosion-resistant alloys are needed when stainless steel cannot withstand conditions such as sulfuric acid, hydrochloric acid, or chlor-alkali service. Table 7 shows that European demand comes mainly from replacement. New plants and demand for plate and clad plate are shifting to Asia. Alleima is investing SEK 220 million in further processing and finishing at Mehsana, India. Its chemical and petrochemical product range includes Sanicro 625 (UNS N06625) tube with an outside diameter of 6 to 42 mm [MO_ALLEIMA_625_MO, CO_ALLEIMA_MEHSANA_2026].

Table 7: High performance metal applications in chemical processing, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Stable | Replacement heat exchangers, vessels and piping in European plants | C-276, C-22 and 59 type NiCrMo alloys and Alloy 625 as plate, sheet and welding consumables |
| Grows | New plants in China, India and Southeast Asia | Plate, clad plate, zirconium plate and tube |
| Selective | Battery material plants, where titanium and linings dominate | Plate for selected duties, welding consumables |
| Falls | New build in European base chemicals | Plate |

*Sources: team assessment [CO_ALLEIMA_MEHSANA_2026, BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035, CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026].*

Implications for suppliers:

* Defend European replacement demand with plate and welding consumables sold on documented life cycle cost.
* Follow new construction to India and Southeast Asia through the engineering contractors that build Asian plants.
