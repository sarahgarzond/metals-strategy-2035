## 5.5 Chemical Processing

Chemical processing is a core market for corrosion-resistant materials, but growth in chemical sales does not automatically create new equipment orders. Replacement cycles, operating severity and plant investment determine demand for alloy plate, strip, bar and welding consumables.

The strongest opportunity is equipment where longer service life offsets a higher material price. Maintain access to European replacement demand and qualify export projects through equipment makers; avoid broad exposure to commodity plant expansion without evidence of funding and competitive material selection.

### 5.5.1 Historical and Current Market Overview

Cefic reports EU chemical sales of about EUR 635 billion in 2024. The EU share of global chemical sales declined from 21% in 2009 to 13% in 2024, while China’s rose from 24% to 46%. Global chemical sales in 2024 were 3.6 times their 2004 level in nominal terms (Cefic, 2025). These figures describe chemicals, not process equipment or alloy demand, and include price effects.

Near-term conditions remain uneven. Acerinox’s 2025 results identify a slowdown in chemical processing (Acerinox, 2026a). European market weakness supports a cautious approach to new-build demand, while the installed base still requires maintenance. Regional sales shares should guide prospecting, not determine a regional alloy-demand forecast.

### 5.5.2 Key Applications and Opportunities for the Company

The benchmark portfolio covers Nickel 200, Alloy 59, C-276, 625, 602 CA and zirconium-based solutions for demanding process conditions. Applications include acid and caustic service, heat exchangers, vessels, pumps, valves and industrial furnaces (VDM Metals, n.d.-f). Grade selection must follow the actual medium, concentration, temperature and welding requirements.

Prioritise corrosion-resistant plate and welding consumables for replacement equipment, then high-temperature products for furnace upgrades. The direct sales route is usually through fabricators and equipment manufacturers; plant operators influence specifications. Require documented life-cycle savings and application testing before proposing a premium grade. Where stainless steel, linings or alternative materials meet the duty economically, accept a smaller addressable market rather than assume universal substitution by nickel alloys.
