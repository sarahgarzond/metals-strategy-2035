## 5.5 Chemical Processing

Chemical processing is the traditional heartland of corrosion resistant alloys, but in Europe it has become a maintenance market while new plants are built in Asia. The industry still needs alloys that survive hot acids and chlorides, yet the place and the reason for buying them are changing.

### 5.5.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Europe's chemical industry is shrinking in volume and running with spare capacity. EU27 chemical sales were about EUR 635 billion in 2024, but Europe's share of world chemical sales fell from 21 percent in 2009 to 13 percent, while China's rose from 24 to 46 percent [CEFIC_EU27_SALES_2024, CEFIC_EU_SHARE_2024, CEFIC_CN_SHARE_2024]. The EU chemical production index fell 3.2 percent in 2025, capacity utilisation was 75.0 percent in the third quarter of 2026 against a long term average of 81.3 percent, and German plants ran at only 72.5 percent in 2025 [EUROSTAT_C20_EU27_2025, CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026, VCI_CAPUTIL_2025]. New capital follows growth to Asia, as BASF expects over 80 percent of chemical industry growth to be in Asia Pacific by 2035, opened its Zhanjiang Verbund site in November 2025 and plans 2026 capital spending below depreciation [BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035, BASF_ZHANJIANG_2025, CO_BASF_CAPEX_2026].

### 5.5.2 Applications and Opportunities

Corrosion resistant alloys remain indispensable wherever stainless steel fails, from sulphuric and hydrochloric acid service to chlor alkali plants and heat exchangers.

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |

| Stable | Replacement heat exchangers, vessels and piping in European plants | Alloy C-276, 59 and 625 plate, sheet, welding consumables |
| Grows | New plants in China, India and Southeast Asia | Plate, clad plate, zirconium plate and tube |

*Source: team assessment based on Cefic, BASF and Alleima evidence [CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026, BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035, CO_ALLEIMA_MEHSANA_2026].*

The market rewards approvals and service more than volume, and the leading suppliers are moving closer to the new demand. Alleima is investing SEK 220 million in finishing at Mehsana in India to follow Asian growth, even though it reported a slowdown in its chemical and petrochemical segment in the same quarter [CO_ALLEIMA_MEHSANA_2026]. The buyers are fabricators and engineering contractors, while plant operators such as BASF set the specification, and surcharges pass most nickel and molybdenum cost changes through to them.

Implications for suppliers:

* Defend European replacement demand with plate and welding consumables sold on documented life cycle cost.
* Follow new construction to India and Southeast Asia through the engineering contractors that build Asian plants.
