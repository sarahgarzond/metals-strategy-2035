## 5.5 Chemical Processing

Chemical processing is the traditional heartland of corrosion resistant alloys, but in Europe it has become a maintenance market while new plants are built in Asia.

### 5.5.1 Demand, Geography and Market Size

Europe's chemical industry is shrinking in volume and running with spare capacity. EU27 chemical sales were about EUR 635 billion in 2024, but Europe's share of world chemical sales fell from 21 percent in 2009 to 13 percent, while China's rose from 24 to 46 percent [CEFIC_EU27_SALES_2024, CEFIC_EU_SHARE_2024, CEFIC_CN_SHARE_2024]. The EU chemical production index fell 3.2 percent in 2025, and capacity utilisation was 75.0 percent in the third quarter of 2026 against a long term average of 81.3 percent [EUROSTAT_C20_EU27_2025, CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026]. New capital follows growth to Asia, as BASF expects over 80 percent of chemical industry growth to be in Asia Pacific by 2035 and opened its Zhanjiang Verbund site in November 2025 [BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035, BASF_ZHANJIANG_2025]. The closest measured size at the metal stage is EU apparent consumption of nickel plate, sheet, strip and foil, 57.8 kt in 2024 across all end uses, of which chemical processing holds an unmeasured share [AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024].

### 5.5.2 Applications and Opportunities

Corrosion resistant alloys remain indispensable wherever stainless steel fails, from sulphuric and hydrochloric acid to chlor alkali service. Table 7 shows that European demand is mostly replacement, while new construction and its plate and clad plate move to Asia.

Table 7: High performance metal applications in chemical processing, outlook to 2035

| Outlook | Applications | Materials and product forms |
|---|---|---|
| Stable | Replacement heat exchangers, vessels and piping in European plants | C-276, C-22 and 59 type NiCrMo alloys and Alloy 625 as plate, sheet and welding consumables |
| Grows | New plants in China, India and Southeast Asia | Plate, clad plate, zirconium plate and tube |
| Selective | Battery material plants, where titanium and linings dominate | Plate for selected duties, welding consumables |
| Falls | New build in European base chemicals | Plate |

*Sources: team assessment [CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026, BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035, CO_ALLEIMA_MEHSANA_2026].*

The leading suppliers are moving closer to that new demand, and Alleima is investing SEK 220 million in finishing at Mehsana in India to serve Asian chemical and petrochemical customers [CO_ALLEIMA_MEHSANA_2026].

Implications for suppliers:

* Defend European replacement demand with plate and welding consumables sold on documented life cycle cost.
* Follow new construction to India and Southeast Asia through the engineering contractors that build Asian plants.
