# Appendices: Data, Sources, Calculations, and Assumptions

## A1 Definitions and evidence

Mt means million tonnes, GW means gigawatts, and EBITDA means earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. Industry revenue, mined metal, melting output and finished alloy shipments have different boundaries, and each figure keeps its original currency and observation year. No currency conversion or inflation adjustment is applied to the scenario indices. Web sources for the newly completed sections were consulted on 28 October 2026, and earlier observation years are used where the text states them.

Apparent consumption equals sold production plus imports minus exports for one PRODCOM code, one area and one year [AC_METHOD]. The logged results are EU nickel flat products at 57.8 million kilograms, German nickel flat products at 30.4 million kilograms, EU nickel bar, rod and wire at 5,380,347 kilograms, and EU nickel tube, pipes and fittings at 9.2 million kilograms, all for 2024 [AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024, AC_DE_NI_FLAT_2024, AC_EU_NI_BAR_2024, AC_EU_NI_TUBE_2024]. The flat result is an upper bound on a mixed code, the EU flat sold production leg is estimated, EU trade in the dataset is extra EU trade, and the identity is not end use alloy demand and not stainless melt. Unit values are team estimates: about EUR 14,476 per tonne of EU flat sold production, about EUR 8,788 per tonne of German flat sold production, and about EUR 47,824 per tonne of EU flat imports [TE_NI_EU_FLAT_SP_UV_2024, TE_NI_DE_FLAT_SP_UV_2024, TE_NI_EU_FLAT_IMP_UV_2024]. The bar band of about 5.1 million to 5.6 million kilograms is the rounding range around the published point, and the net importer conclusion holds inside it [TE_NI_BAR_AC_BAND_2024]. German flat sold production rose from 15,870 tonnes in 2019 to 36,477 tonnes in 2020 while value stayed near EUR 177 million to EUR 191 million [TE_NI_DE_FLAT_BREAK_2019_2020].

## A3 Interpretation of existing sector estimates

The automotive calculation groups battery electric and plug in hybrid cars together, and cars with a plug can still contain combustion engine components. That is why the automotive upside is not a flat zero, and fleet growth is not a direct measure of new engine alloy purchases, and holding cobalt intensity constant is a simplifying assumption rather than a nickel forecast. The medical device value extrapolation applies a broader bio health growth proxy from 2023 to 2035 at 5.3 percent a year and is not the Chapter 7 medical rate, which runs from 2025. Upstream oil and gas spending, chemical sales, device sales and aviation cobalt stay illustrations of activity. They are not alloy demand, and they are not an addressable market total.

## A4 Information needed before investment

The external choices in Chapters 6 and 8 do not wait for this list. The first gate uses the logged bar position, the mixed code ceiling and the stop list. Before a capital request, the company still needs shipments and net of metal sales by grade, form, application, region, margin and approval, contribution per bottleneck hour, and the nameplate by plant and by route. The melt footnote should be quoted before anyone adds the 83 thousand tonnes of 2025 melt to stainless melt, because a vacuum induction heat and a later remelt of the same metal would double count. The 2028 furnace and finishing steps are not inside the 2025 tonnes. VDM and Haynes are counted once inside Acerinox, and the alloy split of HS 7506, United Kingdom bar supply, and HS 7502 billet declarations are required before any bar capacity case. Tube stays off the capacity list until a route the company owns is distinguished from the rounded PRODCOM tube code. Scenario assumptions should be replaced with customer quantities when those quantities exist, and the hurdle rate used in the memo should be the company's own.
