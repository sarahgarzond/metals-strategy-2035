Alloy 718 engine bar and billet is the only priority 1 that rests on sourced grades and holds under every weighting. It carries about 5 percent niobium from Brazil and at most 1 percent cobalt, and engine deliveries grow 3.7 percent a year on a pool of up to EUR 4.0 bn [MO_SM_IN718_COMP, NB_USGS_BRAZIL_2025, MODEL_GROWTH_LABELS_2026]. Buyers are European and US open market forgers such as Safran at Gennevilliers, Otto Fuchs and Leistritz, because PCC and ATI melt for their own forges [AERO_SAFRAN_GENNEVILLIERS_2026, CO_OTTOFUCHS_NI, CO_LEISTRITZ_AERO_DISKS, CO_PCC_REV_2025].

Turbine alloys pass the threshold only in cobalt lean grades. Cobalt lean casting stock scores 3.75, but its grade list (IN738, GTD 111) is not yet sourced. With alloy 188 and Nimonic 263 counted, casting stock scores 3.60 and combustor sheet 3.55 [MO_SM_UD188_CO, MO_SM_NIM263_CO]. Turbine demand grows 3.4 percent a year, sold to casting houses such as Howmet and CPP [MODEL_GROWTH_LABELS_2026, CO_HWM_SEG_2025, CO_GE_CPP_2026]. With cobalt grades, a combined vacuum melt programme is priority 2 under all four weightings, and the 718 only programme is priority 1 under all four [MODEL_COMBINED_PROGRAMME_2026].

Fab gas strip and tube grows 4.2 percent a year on only EUR 0.11 bn, but it uses existing rolling lines and reaches Applied Materials and Lam Research. Priority 2 also covers CoCr implant bar for Stryker and Zimmer Biomet (3.45), Alloy 690 (3.15) and AM powder (3.00), while FeNi36 foil and NO96 strip stay options [CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025, CO_ZBH_COMMODITY_2025].

## 8.2 Recommended Actions and Implementation Roadmap

Producers should qualify first and add capacity after customers sign, because remelting, billet conversion and inspection are the bottleneck. Non rotating 718 bar to AMS 5662 and 5663 qualifies fastest and brings the first revenue [MO_SM_IN718_COMP].

A team estimate built on the Avesta remelt programme puts the step at EUR 2.3k to 17.3k per annual tonne. That is EUR 3.5 to 26 m for 1.5 kt or EUR 6.9 to 51.9 m for 3 kt. Sales of EUR 38 to 71 m per 1.5 kt earn EUR 5.3 to 17.5 m of operating profit, a payback of 0.6 to 5.9 years [MODEL_INVESTMENT_CASE_2026, CO_OUTOKUMPU_AVESTA_NI_2026].

A decision in Q1 2028 gives the first approved tonne in 2030 in the fast case and 2031 in the central case. Competitor capacity is on stream by the end of 2029, so only the fast case lands before the window narrows [MODEL_INVESTMENT_CASE_2026, ARGUS_SUPERALLOY].
