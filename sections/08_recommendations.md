The highest-ranked opportunities include vacuum-melted superalloys in the United States and Europe. Gas turbines need remelt stock and combustor sheet for Siemens Energy and casting houses such as CPP. Aircraft engine forgers that supply GE Aerospace and Safran need triple-melt Alloy 718 and 720Li billet.

The main constraint is qualified capacity. Siemens Energy is sold out to 2028, and ATI reports forging lead times beyond two years. Established suppliers such as ATI, PCC, Carpenter, Aubert & Duval, and Daido hold long-term agreements. The competition score is therefore 2, and entrants can win business as qualified second sources [CO_ATI_LEAD_2026, CO_GE_CPP_PRESS_2026, CO_SIEMENSENERGY_CMD_2025].

East Asian FeNi36 mask foil for DNP and BOE also ranks in priority 1, with a P50 of 157. Maskless patterning could replace invar foil in some applications (Display Daily, n.d.).

Priority 2 covers products that require customer approval. They include CoCr bar for Stryker and Zimmer Biomet, sour-service products in the Americas, chemical plate sold through Asian fabricators, and Alloy 690 for tube makers [CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025, CO_ZBH_COMMODITY_2025]. All three priority 1 opportunities keep their ranks when the criteria receive equal weights or when entry barriers receive a 20 percent weight.
