The ranking points to vacuum melted superalloy in the United States and Europe, namely remelt stock and combustor sheet for Siemens Energy and casting houses such as CPP, and triple melt Alloy 718 and 720Li billet for the forgers of GE Aerospace and Safran. Capacity rather than metal is scarce, as Siemens Energy is sold out to 2028 and ATI quotes forging lead times beyond two years, but ATI, PCC, Carpenter, Aubert & Duval and Daido hold long term agreements, so competition scores 2 and entrants win only as qualified second sources [CO_SIEMENSENERGY_CMD_2025, CO_GE_CPP_PRESS_2026, CO_ATI_LEAD_2026]. FeNi36 mask foil for DNP and BOE in East Asia completes priority 1 with a P50 of 157, although maskless patterning could replace invar foil [CO_DNP_FMM_SUPPLY_2025].

Priority 2 holds approval protected franchises, CoCr bar for Stryker and Zimmer Biomet, sour service products in the Americas, chemical plate through Asian fabricators and Alloy 690 for tube makers [CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025, CO_ZBH_COMMODITY_2025]. All three priority 1 positions keep their rank under equal weights or with entry barriers at 20 percent.
