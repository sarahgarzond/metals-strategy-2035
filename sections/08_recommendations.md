# 8. Strategic Recommendations

The ranking follows Table 2, the flat and bar positions in Chapter 6, and the four verbs already used in Chapter 5. Aerospace billet, approved sheet and repair wire come first, corrosion resistant plate and welding products come second only where a named grade is imported or replaces a specified supplier, and precision electrical strip and wire come third only in the code that grade ships in. Oil and gas and profitable automotive accounts are kept, electrolysers, powders and medical implant bar stay options, and undifferentiated flat tonnes, diesel only parts, China implant tenders and unfunded programmes are stopped. No aggregate market value is stated, and 57.8 million kilograms is not treated as room under the 47 thousand finished tonnes.

## 8.1 Priority Products, Industries, Regions, and Customers

Three sentences are enough for a plant director to use without a second reading of Chapter 4. Bar, rod and wire is a logged EU net import, with apparent consumption of 5,380,347 kilograms inside a band of about 5.1 million to 5.6 million kilograms, and the commercial test in months 0 to 3 is the client's own bar book against that position rather than a proposal to add bar capacity [AC_EU_NI_BAR_2024, TE_NI_BAR_AC_BAND_2024]. Flat products are a net export of a mixed code, so chemical plate and electrical strip are pursued only where a named grade is imported or a replacement order displaces a specified supplier, and undifferentiated flat tonnes are refused [AC_EU_NI_FLAT_2024]. The 57.8 million kilograms is the ceiling of the code, the qualified alloy slice is smaller and still unmeasured, and there is no bar investment case until the rounding footnote, United Kingdom supply and HS 7502 are in the comparison [CT_DE_7502_2024].

The ranking has to sit on the code the product ships in, so qualified aerospace product for a wrought mill is billet, bar, sheet and weld wire, read against HS 7505 and HS 7502, not against the flat balance. Corrosion resistant plate is alloyed flat, read against the alloy split of HS 7506 once that split exists. Expansion alloys 29-18 and 42, Crofer 22, Aluchrom and much of the soft magnetic book are iron predominant and sit in chapter 72, Konstantan and other copper nickel resistance alloys sit in chapter 74, and Nickel 201 electrolyser strip is unalloyed nickel at a different price and a different buyer from Alloy C-276 plate. One flat balance cannot size all three priorities, and in this industry engine means the aero engine and the industrial gas turbine, so the words declining engine only applications are reserved for automotive engine and exhaust parts such as turbochargers, valves, plugs and exhaust. Haynes, inside Acerinox High Performance Alloys, is the book facing aerospace and industrial gas turbines, and VDM is the book facing the weak chemical and oil and gas year, with the two still counted once [CO_ACX_HPA_MIX_2025, NI_ACX_HPA_2025].

## 8.2 Recommended Actions and Implementation Roadmap

Months 0 to 3 test the European bar position from Chapter 6 against the client's bar book, and they take glow plugs off the growth list now. Months 3 to 12 cap the live qualification list at five accounts and cap sample spend at EUR 200,000 per account until a quantity is agreed, and both caps are team planning choices rather than market facts [TE_PLAN_ACCOUNTS, TE_PLAN_SAMPLE]. Months 12 to 24 release finishing capacity only against a repeat order and only where contribution per bottleneck hour still clears the Chapter 7 downside. The hurdle rate stays the company's, and the memo shows the number the company uses rather than a rate invented here.

Two clocks run at the same time through the full 24 months, so fabricated chemical equipment can be trialled inside a year, while rotating parts and sour service casing stay on a multi year approved supplier path, and a 3 to 12 month trial does not cover a disc. The kill rules sit in that same sequence, so no second sample is cut without a quantity, no finishing capital is released without a repeat order and a downside case that clears the company's hurdle, and no powder or electrolyser spend is released without a financed programme.

The invoice has to be written the way the buyer is charged. Nickel alloy plate is a conversion price plus a monthly alloy adjustment on the nickel, molybdenum, chromium, cobalt, tungsten and niobium in the grade, plus extras for width, testing, positive material identification and non destructive testing. The stainless invoice, a base price plus an alloy surcharge, is a different document. The first 90 day map has to say who carries nickel, molybdenum and cobalt between order and invoice, and whether scrap returns to the same mill, and it has to ask for contribution per bottleneck hour in the company's own grade and form codes. The melt routes stay distinct: vacuum induction plus vacuum arc remelting for rotating grades, vacuum induction plus electroslag remelting for corrosion plate, and a stainless argon oxygen route for Crofer and for the lower nickel stainless grades. Appendix A4 remains the list of internal facts still required, and it is not a reason to postpone the external choices already made from the trade result and the stop list.
