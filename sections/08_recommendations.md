Turbine remelt stock and sheet scores 3.85. It covers vacuum melted superalloy casting stock and combustor sheet, with cobalt as a minor element. Gas turbine output grows 3.6 percent a year, and Siemens Energy is sold out to FY2028. Buyers are casting houses such as PCC and CPP in the United States, which German plate and strip already reaches [CO_SIEMENSENERGY_CMD_2025, CO_GE_CPP_PRESS_2026, COMTRADE_7506_2019_2024]. Its weak points are captive casting, as GE Aerospace agreed to buy CPP, and a driver built on one turbine maker.

Alloy 718 engine bar also scores 3.85. Alloy 718 carries about 5 percent niobium, mined 93 percent in Brazil, and at most 1 percent cobalt. New engines grow 3.7 percent a year on a pool of up to EUR 4.0 bn. Forgers for GE Aerospace and Safran buy triple melt billet, and Germany ships about USD 111 m of nickel bar each to the United States and the United Kingdom [MO_SM_IN718_COMP, NB_USGS_BRAZIL_2025, COMTRADE_7505_2019_2024]. ATI, PCC, Carpenter, Aubert & Duval and Daido hold long term agreements, so entrants win as second sources [CO_ATI_LEAD_2026].

Cobalt rich grades such as Waspaloy cut supply security to 2, so engine bar in all lead grades misses priority 1 by 0.05 [MODEL_SCORING_2026]. Because one melt shop serves both priority 1 rows, the combined vacuum melt programme is priority 1 under three of four weightings, and the 718 variant under all four [MODEL_COMBINED_PROGRAMME_2026].

Electronics enters through fab gas strip and tube, growing 4.2 percent a year on a pool of only EUR 0.11 bn, sold to Applied Materials and Lam Research through gas system makers. Priority 2 also covers CoCr for Stryker and Zimmer Biomet, Alloy 690 for steam generator tube makers, and AM powder [CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025, CO_ZBH_COMMODITY_2025]. FeNi36 mask foil for DNP and BOE and FeNi36 strip for GTT NO96 tanks stay options until volumes are measured [DNP_KUROSAKI_2024, MO_GTT_NO96].

## 8.2 Recommended Actions and Implementation Roadmap

Producers should qualify first and add capacity after customers sign. For 718 billet, VAR and ESR remelting, billet conversion and ultrasonic inspection are the bottleneck, not VIM.

Table 18: Implementation roadmap 2026 to 2035, owners, benchmarks and gates

The main risks are slow qualification, ceramic composites, Asian capacity and cobalt quotas, each with a kill criterion in Table A15. Producers should stop investing in diesel only parts, whose index falls to 69, and in new European base chemical plants at 75.0 percent utilisation. They should hold electrolyser capacity until orders are funded and avoid captive zirconium cladding [MODEL_T1_RESULTS_2035, CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026].
