## 2.5 Specialty stainless steels

Specialty stainless steels sit at the top of a commodity family of enormous volume, so the section first sizes the family and then isolates the duplex, superduplex, superaustenitic and high molybdenum grades that matter to the Client. No public source reports these grades separately, which is why total stainless statistics and customs data serve as the structured base and the specialty share is flagged as open.

### Production

World stainless melt shop output reached 62.6 Mt in 2024 and 64.2 Mt in 2025 [WSA_SS_MELT_2024, WSA_SS_MELT_2025]. China produced 39.4 Mt in 2024, and its share of about 64% in 2025 means that the price level of the whole family is set by Chinese and Indonesian 300 series volumes [WSA_SS_CN_2024, WSA_SS_MELT_2025]. Europe contributed only 6.1 Mt in 2024, which explains why the European mills concentrate on higher value grades [WSA_SS_EU_2024]. ### Trade

Customs data show how strongly the flat product trade has moved towards Asia and Indonesia. In 2024 Indonesia exported flat rolled stainless of 600 mm width or more (HS 7219) worth USD 5.8bn, ahead of China at USD 5.0bn, and it also shipped USD 2.4bn of semifinished slabs and ingots under HS 7218 [CT_7219_ID_X_2024, CT_7219_CN_X_2024, CT_7218_ID_X_2024]. Europe stays a net buyer of this commodity flow, since Germany imported USD 3.8bn of HS 7219 and took USD 1.14bn of it from Finland alone, where the Tornio mill is the likely source [CT_7219_DE_M_2024, CT_7219_DE_M_FI_2024]. Belgium and Italy exported USD 3.3bn and USD 2.3bn respectively, so the European trade pattern is an intra regional exchange of flat product [CT_7219_BE_X_2024, CT_7219_IT_X_2024].

Bars behave differently, because Germany exported USD 962m and imported USD 971m of stainless bars and rods (HS 7222), which is a balanced position that suggests specialised mix trading [CT_7222_DE_X_2024, CT_7222_DE_M_2024]. India and Italy exported USD 1.07bn and USD 1.14bn of bars [CT_7222_IN_X_2024, CT_7222_IT_X_2024]. Across the 15 reporters in our extract, exports reached USD 29.1bn for flat product above 600 mm, USD 4.2bn for strip and USD 6.0bn for bars, so the Client's strip and bar forms address smaller trade pools than plate and coil [CT_7219_X_SUM15_2024, CT_7220_X_SUM15_2024, CT_7222_X_SUM15_2024]. Unit values from the same extract, which are a team estimate of value divided by net weight, run from about USD 1.4 per kg for Chinese flat product to about USD 5.5 per kg for German bars, and they indicate that European flows carry a richer mix.

### Applications

Duplex grades combine austenitic and ferritic phases, which gives them higher strength than 304 and 316 and better resistance to chloride stress corrosion, so designers can reduce wall thickness in tanks, pressure vessels and pipes. Superduplex and the 6Mo superaustenitic grades extend this to seawater and aggressive acid service, and the property that makes them necessary is the combination of high molybdenum, chromium and nitrogen content. ### Prices

Nickel is the main cost driver of the austenitic and duplex families, and the World Bank annual average moved from USD 14,744 per tonne in 2005 to USD 21,809 in 2010, USD 11,863 in 2015 and USD 25,834 in 2022 [WB_NI_2005, WB_NI_2010, WB_NI_2015, WB_NI_2022]. By 2024 and 2025 it had fallen to USD 16,814 and USD 15,162, which is about 41% below the 2022 peak by team estimate [WB_NI_2024, WB_NI_2025]. The policy response in Indonesia has since changed the picture, because a 2026 ore quota of 250 to 270 Mwt against about 379 Mwt a year earlier supported a January 2026 high of USD 18,950, and the average from January to August 2026 reached USD 17,494 [ARGUS_NI_RKAB_2026, DEN_NI_PRICE_2026]. ### Interesting facts

In 2024 Indonesia overtook China in flat stainless exports by value with USD 5.8bn against USD 5.0bn, which shows how fast nickel pig iron integration changed trade [CT_7219_ID_X_2024, CT_7219_CN_X_2024]. In 2024 Finland supplied about 30% of German flat stainless imports by value, a team estimate from USD 1.14bn of USD 3.79bn, and Tornio is the likely mill [CT_7219_DE_M_FI_2024, CT_7219_DE_M_2024]. Between 2022 and 2025 the nickel price fell from USD 25,834 to USD 15,162 per tonne, which cut alloy surcharges for buyers of duplex plate [WB_NI_2022, WB_NI_2025]. In 2026 Indonesia reduced its nickel ore quota to 250 to 270 Mwt, and that decision lifted LME nickel to USD 18,950 on 29 January [ARGUS_NI_RKAB_2026].
