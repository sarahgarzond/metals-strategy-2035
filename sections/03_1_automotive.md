## 3.1 Automotive

Automotive is a large but slow sector for the Client, because the shift from engines to batteries moves value from heat resistant nickel alloys towards plated strip and copper products with fierce price competition. The team model sizes the sector at EUR 6.0bn in 2025, growing 1.5% p.a. to about EUR 7.0bn in 2035 [MODEL_AUTO_2025, MODEL_AUTO_2035], roughly 12% of the portfolio market [MODEL_TOTAL_2025] (team estimate), so the analysis below asks why value shifts while volume stays stable.

### Market size and growth to 2035

Vehicle volume provides that stable base, since world motor vehicle production rose 3.9% to 96.4 million units in 2025 [OICA_PROD_2025] and Asia Pacific supplied more than 61% of it [OICA_APAC_PROD_2025]. Electrification changes the content of each vehicle, because electric car sales passed 20 million in 2025, one quarter of all new cars [IEA_GEVO_SALES_2025], and the IEA expects 23 million in 2026 [IEA_GEVO_FCST_2026]. Press summaries put the IEA electric share near 50% in 2035, but the scenario label is unconfirmed [IEA_GEVO_2035_SHARE]. Inside the model, battery and power strip grows 8% p.a. from EUR 1.4bn to EUR 3.0bn, while heat resistant strip and wire for engines shrinks 3% p.a. from EUR 0.9bn to EUR 0.7bn [MODEL_AUTO_V1, MODEL_AUTO_V2]. The remaining EUR 3.7bn of stainless exhaust, aluminium and wire products therefore declines by about 1.2% p.a. [MODEL_AUTO_REST] (team estimate, TODO(data): PRODCOM product split). No 2035 vehicle production forecast was found outside paywalled S&P Global Mobility data (TODO(data)), which is the main uncertainty behind the drivers below.

### Drivers and risks

These growth rates rest on three drivers that lift copper and nickel strip demand per vehicle. First, copper rises from about 23 kg in a combustion car to 83 kg in a battery electric car [ICA_CU_VEHICLE_2017], a ratio the German copper association confirms with 25 kg against just under 70 kg [KI_CU_VEHICLE]. Second, large cylindrical cells are built from deep drawn and wall ironed nickel plated steel cans with nickel tab strip [CSIRO_CELLCANS]. Third, the EU Automotive Package of December 2025 cuts the 2035 target to 90% and keeps plug in hybrids on the market [EC_AUTO_PKG_2025], which extends the life of engine parts. The risks are just as concrete, because LFP chemistry already supplies more than half of electric car batteries [IEA_GEVO_LFP_2025] and favours prismatic aluminium cans, while 48 V architectures such as the Tesla Cybertruck reduce harness copper [MOTOR1_CYBERTRUCK_48V]. Chinese carmakers delivered 60% of electric car sales in 2025 [IEA_GEVO_CN_OEM_2025], which places the fastest growth next to Chinese strip mills.

### Emerging technologies and materials required

Emerging cell and power technologies decide which alloy and product form wins inside the vehicle. Cylindrical 46xx cells, such as BMW's Gen6 4695 and 46120 formats [BMW_GEN6_CELLS], need nickel plated, diffusion annealed low carbon steel strip that combines deep drawability with electrolyte and contact resistance [TATA_HILUMIN], and Waelzholz offers strip plated before forming [WAELZHOLZ_NI_STRIP]. Platforms at 800 V raise demand for Cu ETP and oxygen free copper busbars and for CuNiSi connector strip such as C19005, which holds contact force at elevated temperature [AURUBIS_CONNECTOR]. Engine applications still need Alloy 601 and Alloy 600 in strip, wire and tube for glow plug tubes, sensor caps and manifolds, because these grades resist oxidation in hot exhaust gas [VDM_ALLOY601_DS]. Fuel cell cars add a niche, since Toyota uses titanium and Hyundai steel bipolar plates [IDTECHEX_BPP], which opens room for coated 316L foil linked to hydrogen in section 3.3.

### Geographic concentration of demand

Because these technologies scale fastest in Asia, demand for the Client's automotive materials concentrates there. China produced 34.53 million vehicles in 2025, about 36% of world output by team calculation [OICA_CN_PROD_2025, OICA_PROD_2025], and its electric share reached almost 55% [IEA_GEVO_CN_SHARE_2025]. The Americas made 18.74 million vehicles [OICA_AM_PROD_2025], including about 10.2 million in the United States [MND_US_JP_PROD_2025], where the electric share stayed around 10% [IEA_GEVO_US_SHARE_2025]. The EU registered about 10.8 million cars, of which 17.4% were battery electric, 34.5% hybrid, 9.4% plug in hybrid and only 8.9% diesel [ACEA_EU_REG_2025, ACEA_BEV_2025, ACEA_HEV_2025, ACEA_PHEV_2025, ACEA_DIESEL_2025]. ### Customers, competitors and competitor map

The customers in these hubs fall into three groups with different buying logic. Carmakers such as BMW, Volkswagen, Tesla and BYD specify materials, cell makers such as CATL and EVE Energy buy can and tab strip [BMW_GEN6_CELLS], and Tier 1 suppliers such as Bosch, Denso, Niterra, TE Connectivity and Aptiv buy alloy strip and wire. Niterra shows the engine exposure, with spark plugs, glow plugs and sensors at about 80% of revenue [NITERRA_AUTO_SHARE]. Tata Steel, Toyo Kohan, Jiangsu Jiutian and Zhongshan Sanmei compete in nickel plated can strip through scaled plating lines [TATA_HILUMIN, MR_NIPS_PLAYERS]. Connector strip is served by Wieland, Aurubis, KME, Mitsubishi Materials, Proterial and Materion [GNW_CU_CONNECTOR], and KME strengthened its position in 2022 by buying the Zutphen plant and three slitting centres from Aurubis [AURUBIS_KME_FRP]. VDM Metals, now within Acerinox alongside Haynes [ACX_HAYNES], and Special Metals hold the Alloy 601 niche through melting depth and long approvals, voestalpine High Performance Metals earns about 20% of divisional revenue in automotive [VOEST_HPM_AUTO_2026], and distributors such as thyssenkrupp Materials Services and Jacquet serve small lots, so relevance shifts by application.

### Where high performance metals gain or lose relevance

High performance metals gain where electrification demands conductivity, formability and plating quality at thin gauges, which favours nickel strip in cylindrical cells, CuNiSi in 800 V connectors and coated stainless foil if fuel cell trucks scale. They lose where combustion parts disappear, as diesel already holds only 8.9% of EU registrations [ACEA_DIESEL_2025] and ceramic glow plugs compete with metal sheaths. They also lose to aluminium cans in LFP prismatic cells [IEA_GEVO_LFP_2025], to thinner harnesses at 48 V [MOTOR1_CYBERTRUCK_48V] and to titanium in bipolar plates [IDTECHEX_BPP], which narrows the value chain steps worth pursuing.

### Attractive value chain steps

Given this balance, the model ranks both automotive cells in tier C, with scores of 2.75 for battery and power strip and 2.35 for engine alloys [MODEL_AUTO_V1, MODEL_AUTO_V2]. The most attractive step for the Client is precision thin strip with slitting and plating service for European connector and module makers, where qualification counts more than tonnage. Alloy 601 should run as a cash niche, coated foil for bipolar plates should stay a hydrogen option, and the can strip volume race against Tata Steel and Toyo Kohan should be avoided, as the spotlights below show.

### Spotlights

**BMW Neue Klasse, Debrecen.** BMW began series production of the iX3 in Debrecen, Hungary, in late October 2025, and the plant is the first of five to assemble Gen6 batteries [BMW_DEBRECEN_SOP]. The 46 mm round cells come from CATL and EVE Energy, with up to 120 GWh planned at six plants [BMW_GEN6_CELLS], so nickel plated can strip and nickel tab strip are the relevant forms, with Tata Steel, Toyo Kohan and Waelzholz as likely suppliers, although BMW has not disclosed the can material.

**EU Automotive Package, Brussels.** The Commission's proposal of 16 December 2025 lets plug in hybrids and range extenders register after 2035 [EC_AUTO_PKG_2025], which sustains demand for Alloy 601 and Alloy 600 strip and wire in glow plug tubes and sensor caps [VDM_ALLOY601_DS] bought by Bosch, Niterra and Denso from VDM Metals, Special Metals or Nippon Yakin.

**Tesla Cybertruck, Texas.** Tesla moved the Cybertruck low voltage network to 48 V in 2023 [MOTOR1_CYBERTRUCK_48V], cutting copper wire and shifting value to CuNiSi and CuSn connector strip from Wieland, Aurubis or Mitsubishi Materials for harness makers such as Yazaki and Aptiv (TODO(data): copper saving unverified).
