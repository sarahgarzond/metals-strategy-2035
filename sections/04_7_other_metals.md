## 4.7 Other Selected Metals

### 4.7.1 Zirconium

Zirconium shows again why mineral output and specialty metal supply stay apart. World mine production of zirconium mineral concentrates is estimated at 1,200 thousand tonnes gross weight in 2025, led by Australia, and the leading end use of zircon is ceramics rather than implant bar [ZR_MINE_GROSS_2025_WORLD, ZR_MINE_GROSS_2025_AU, ZR_USGS_USES_2026]. The price at the mineral stage is an estimated premium grade of USD 1,800 per tonne, cost insurance and freight China, in 2025 [ZR_PRICE_PREMIUM_CIF_2025]. EU sold production of unwrought zirconium and powders was 4,000 kilograms in 2024 with a rounded flag, so no European mill apparent consumption is stated as implant demand [ZR_PRODCOM_UNWROUGHT_EU_2024]. Oxidized zirconium joint parts are Smith and Nephew's OXINIUM route, not a generic zirconium bar inferred from zircon sand, and the VDM portfolio reference remains an application benchmark only.

### 4.7.2 Copper

Copper keeps mine production, refining and a cathode price on separate lines, because none of them is a high performance plate order. World mine production of recoverable copper was a rounded 23 million tonnes in 2024, and world refinery production is estimated at a rounded 29 million tonnes in 2025 [CU_USGS_MINE_2024, CU_USGS_REF_2025]. The 2025 mean of monthly Pink Sheet prices was USD 9,947 per tonne, which is a refined metal benchmark and not a conversion price for alloy plate [PINK_CU_AVG_2025]. Copper and aluminium plate workings stay out of the recommendation until the same three leg standard used for nickel is the basis of a capacity case, because a commercial assessment still needs an identified product, customer and route.

### 4.7.3 Aluminium

Aluminium is a comparison point for transport and electrical uses, and primary metal is not a proxy for nickel or cobalt alloys. World primary smelter production is a rounded 74 million tonnes in 2025, with China at 45 million tonnes, and the Pink Sheet annual average was about USD 2,632 per tonne [USGS_MCS2026_AL, AL_PINK_2025]. Bauxite, alumina, primary metal and recycled metal stay separate stages, and the International Aluminium Institute's 73.8 million tonnes for 2025 sits close to the rounded USGS total without changing that separation [IAI_PRIMARY_2025]. Aluminium demand is not used as a substitute for the nickel balances in Table 2.

### 4.7.4 Manganese

Manganese is an alloying addition in steel and in some nickel grades, and it is also a battery chemical, so ore tonnes are not a mill product franchise. World mine production on a manganese content basis was a rounded 20 million tonnes in 2025, led by South Africa and Gabon, and the United States ore price was about USD 4.5 per metric ton unit of manganese content [USGS_MCS2026_MN]. The International Manganese Institute reports 21.4 million tonnes metal contained for the same year, which is a second mine series and not a ferroalloy shipment [IMNI_AR_2025]. No European apparent consumption of a manganese mill form is used in the recommendation. For Münvest, the task is to establish which grades overlap with the intended book before any conclusion is drawn from aggregate demand.

### 4.7.5 Additional Metals

Other metals may be included where a specific application or a sourcing dependency makes them relevant, and this category stays an extension rather than a residual total of unlike materials. Iron and steel provide context for several of the metals above, and finished steel and stainless steel are not added to the output of their alloying constituents. The reason is Table 2: specialty nickel flat apparent consumption is an upper bound in the tens of thousands of tonnes, and stainless melt is tens of millions of tonnes.

Company comparisons across the profiles need the same separation of unlike businesses. On grades such as 625, 718, Alloy C-276, 188 and 230, Acerinox High Performance Alloys is one line, with VDM in Germany and Haynes in the United States inside that line, and their tonnes are not added again to the 83 thousand and 47 thousand already counted [NI_ACX_HPA_2025]. ATI, Carpenter Technology, Special Metals, Alleima and Aperam Alloys are relevant to particular forms, and their total revenues do not establish comparable market shares. Chapter 5 now asks which industries buy the forms these profiles have kept distinct.
