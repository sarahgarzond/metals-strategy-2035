## 4.7 Other Selected Metals

The remaining metals are relevant either as niche product lines or as competing materials, so their profiles concentrate on what changes the company's position.

### 4.7.1 Zirconium

Zirconium illustrates why mineral output and specialty metal must be kept apart. World production of zirconium mineral concentrates was about 1.2 Mt gross weight in 2025, led by Australia with 400 kt, and its main uses are ceramics, foundry sand and refractories [ZR_MINE_GROSS_2025_WORLD, ZR_MINE_GROSS_2025_AU]. Premium zircon sold at about USD 1,800 per tonne in 2025, and Iluka received USD 1,514 per tonne in the first half of 2026 [ZR_PRICE_PREMIUM_CIF_2025, ZR_ILUKA_ZIRCON_H1_2026]. The metal market that matters to the company is much smaller and is driven by nuclear fuel cladding and chemical equipment. There are 443 operable reactors and 81 under construction worldwide, and installed nuclear capacity rises from 420 GW in 2024 to 710 GW in 2035 in the IEA Net Zero Scenario [WNA_OPERABLE_2026, WEO_NZE_NUCLEAR_GW]. Reactor grade zirconium is a captive business, because fuel makers such as Westinghouse and Framatome and integrated producers such as ATI control sponge, alloy and tube [ZR_ATI_ZR_SITES_2025]. The open market for the company is chemical grade zirconium plate and tube for acid service.

### 4.7.2 Copper

Copper connects the company to electrification, but the relevant product is a small, specialised part of a large metal market. World mine production was about 23.2 Mt in 2025 and refined production 28.6 Mt [CU_ICSG_MINE_2025, CU_ICSG_REFPROD_2025]. Flat rolled plate, sheet and strip account for only 11 percent of first use of copper semis, against 63 percent for wire [CU_IWCC_FIRSTUSE_2024]. The copper price averaged USD 9,947 per tonne in 2025 and set LME records above USD 14,500 per tonne in 2026 [LME_CU_RECORD_2026], while the IEA expects a primary supply deficit of about 25 percent by 2035 [CU_WB_CU_2025, CU_IEA_GAP_2035]. Data centres illustrate the new demand, since NVIDIA states that a 1 MW rack at 54 VDC needs up to 200 kg of copper busbar [NVDA_800VDC]. The company's opportunity lies in copper nickel and specialty strip, where Aurubis, Wieland and Mitsubishi Materials are established, rather than in tonnage copper [CU_AURUBIS_FLAT_FY2425].

### 4.7.3 Aluminium

Aluminium is mainly a competing material for the company, because it replaces heavier alloys wherever temperature and corrosion allow it. World primary production reached about 74 Mt in 2025, of which China produced 61 percent, and the price averaged USD 2,632 per tonne [USGS_MCS2026_AL, IAI_PRIMARY_2025, AL_PINK_2025]. European primary production has fallen to about 955 kt, against demand of about 9 Mt a year [FACE_EU_AL_2024, EA_DEMAND_PRIMARY]. The strategic question for the company is therefore substitution in heat exchangers and busbars, not entry into aluminium production.

### 4.7.4 Manganese

Manganese is an alloying addition whose tonnage is driven by carbon steel. World mine output was about 20 Mt of contained manganese in 2025, led by South Africa, Gabon, Ghana and Australia, which together supplied about 81 percent [USGS_MCS2026_MN, IMNI_AR_2025]. The ore price fell to about USD 4.5 per metric ton unit, so manganese is neither a supply risk nor a growth market for the company.

### 4.7.5 Additional Metals

Other metals enter the portfolio only through specific applications, so this category stays a scoped extension rather than a residual total. Iron matters through FeNi alloys such as FeNi36 for display masks and FeNi42 for lead frames, whose value is set by rolling capability rather than by iron supply. Niobium and rhenium matter for superalloys, because Alloy 718 contains about 5 percent niobium and single crystal turbine blades contain rhenium, of which only about 81 t were mined worldwide in 2025, mostly as a byproduct of copper and molybdenum [USGS_MCS2026_RHENIUM, CO_RTX_RAWMAT_2025]. Together with the metal profiles above, these supply facts frame the industry analysis that follows, which asks where each metal turns into a paying application.
