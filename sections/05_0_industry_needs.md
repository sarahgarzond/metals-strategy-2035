# 5. Industry Needs: Demand, Geography and Market Size

Metals create value only when an industry needs them, so the chain now moves from material to demand. Each of the seven industries is examined along the same links, from industry size and regional concentration to the applications and product forms through which high performance metals enter it. Because industry revenue is not alloy demand, each section uses the most direct measurable indicator and closes its first subsection with the best measured size at a named stage of the chain. Where no alloy tonnage is published, the industry enters chapter 7 only as a team index, and appendix A5 holds the indicator tables. To avoid double counting, electrolysers and fuel cells belong to energy, process vessels to chemicals, and sensors and magnetic parts to electronics.
