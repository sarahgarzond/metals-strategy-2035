# 5. Industry Needs

The following seven industry blocks retain the report’s existing analysis, tables and citations. They examine demand through the chain from industry driver to component, material, delivery form and buyer. Their role in the revised framework is to explain how the metals introduced in Chapter 4 become commercially relevant applications.

Industry revenue indicates economic activity, not addressable alloy demand. Potential customers remain prospects unless a relationship is documented. To limit overlap in the scenario model, electrolysers and fuel cells are assigned to energy, process vessels to chemicals, and sensors and magnetic components to electronics. The preserved industry evidence is then brought together in Chapter 7’s application-demand scenarios.
