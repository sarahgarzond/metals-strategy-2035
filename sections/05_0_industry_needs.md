# 5. Industry Needs: Demand, Geography and Market Size

Metals create value only when an industry needs them, so the analysis now follows the chain from material to demand. Each of the seven industries is examined along the same three links, starting with the size and growth of the industry, continuing with the regions where its demand and production are concentrated, and ending with the applications and product forms through which high performance metals enter it. Because industry revenue is not alloy demand, each section relies on the most direct measurable indicator available, such as engine deliveries, gas turbine capacity, fab equipment spending or implant procedures, and the detailed indicator tables are collected in appendix A5. To avoid double counting, electrolysers and fuel cells are assigned to energy, process vessels to chemicals, and sensors and magnetic parts to electronics.
