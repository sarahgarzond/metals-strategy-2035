# Grading rubric (German scale: 1.0 best, 4.0 pass, 5.0 fail)

| Criterion | Weight | What a 1.0 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Research depth and source quality | 25% | Primary databases (USGS, Comtrade, Eurostat, IEA, OECD, UNIDO) drive the numbers. Latest editions. Spotlight facts are specific, dated and named. |
| Model transparency and reproducibility | 20% | Clear Material > Form > Application > Industry > Geography > Size > Growth chain. Assumptions documented. Totals reconcile. Scenarios explained. |
| Storyline from data to strategy | 20% | Phase I feeds Phase II, which feeds Phase III. Every recommendation traces back to evidence. The answer comes first. |
| Actionability and strategic insight | 15% | Priorities are specific (alloy, form, customer, region). Initiatives are timed, sized and owned. Trade offs and things to stop are explicit. |
| Writing and house style | 10% | Effortless to read, no style violations, linked paragraphs, no filler. |
| Visuals and design | 10% | Every chart has a message title, a source and readable labels. Consistent design from the brief. 30 pages or fewer. |

Grade bands: 1.0 to 1.3 exceptional and usable by industry as is. 1.7 to 2.3 very good with minor gaps.
2.7 to 3.3 solid but generic. 3.7 to 4.0 weak but passing.

Every grader must return: the grade per criterion, the overall grade, and the ten highest impact fixes
(each with location, problem, concrete fix and expected grade impact).
