In 2023 Japan reports no net weight for 750522 and 750620 exports (USD 35 m and 321 m), so the 2023 flat and wire tonnage and alloy shares are biased low. In 2024 only Indonesia 750522 (USD 0.2 m) and Italy 750620 (USD 7.9 m) lack a weight. Unit values here are customs values per kg of net weight and include every alloy from Alloy 625 to Alloy 400, so they are not superalloy prices. EUR per kg uses 1.08 USD per EUR (team assumption).
