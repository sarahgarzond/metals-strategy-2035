Reading: bars, wire, flat and tubes of nickel are almost all alloy in the traded floor, so the HS 7505, 7506 and 7507 floors used for the family tonnes overstate alloy tonnage by only the pure nickel share shown above. 2024 is the basis. In 2023 Japan reports no net weight for 750522 and 750620 exports (USD 35 m and 321 m), so the 2023 flat and wire tonnage and alloy shares are biased low. In 2024 only Indonesia 750522 (USD 0.2 m) and Italy 750620 (USD 7.9 m) lack a weight. Unit values here are customs values per kg of net weight and include every alloy from Alloy 625 to Alloy 400, so they are not superalloy prices. EUR per kg uses 1.08 USD per EUR (team assumption).
