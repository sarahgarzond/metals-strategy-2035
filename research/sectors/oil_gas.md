## 2. Key growth drivers and risks

The volume driver is decline, not a large rise in oil use. Most upstream money replaces lost production from existing fields. Gas grows inside that total because LNG export projects are already sanctioned and because Stated Policies gas demand still rises from 4,250 billion cubic metres to 4,750 billion cubic metres in 2035 before it levels off. Current Policies oil and gas demand together are 16 percent higher by 2035 than in 2024.

The price and security shock of 2026 cuts the other way for near term oil spending. World Energy Investment 2026 says higher prices did not produce a broad rise in oil investment, because project cycles are long, offshore rigs are tight, exploration inventories are thin, and companies continued to return cash to shareholders. The effective closure of the Strait of Hormuz is the IEA's frame for that report. Middle East export income for producers that depend on the Strait is lower, which removes the usual source of capital spending.

LNG has a supply wave and a demand risk at the same time. The capacity is large enough to oversupply Stated Policies in 2030 and to be fully used in Current Policies. Attacks at Ras Laffan delay Qatar's North Field East, so the easing of the gas market that the industry expected in 2026 is postponed. Price sensitive importers in Asia are the demand swing. If they do not take the surplus, utilisation falls. In the Net Zero case in World Energy Outlook 2025, projects already under construction would run at about 75 percent in 2030 and 50 percent in 2035.

For the alloy slice, the commercial risks are visible in the company reports. Acerinox said oil and gas was sluggish in 2025 because of a lack of new projects, and in the first half of 2026 it said the sector should recover when the Middle East conflict ends. Outokumpu said European project business in energy, oil, gas and chemicals was on hold. Alleima said oil and gas demand in 2025 stayed stable at a high level, then in the third quarter of 2026 reported a cancelled oil country tubular goods order of about SEK 250 million. SLB's 2025 revenue fell where activity fell in Saudi Arabia, Mexico and offshore Sub Saharan Africa, while the third quarter of 2026 commentary pointed to deepwater awards and a later Middle East recovery. The alloy market can therefore be weak in a year when LNG capacity investment is strong, because the two use different equipment.

Nickel price is a cost risk, not a demand forecast. The move from about USD 16,800 per metric ton in 2024 to about USD 15,000 to USD 15,200 in 2025 lowers the cost of nickel units in alloy plate and welding wire. It does not, by itself, say how many tonnes oil and gas will buy.

## 3. Emerging technologies and applications

High pressure, high temperature and sour deepwater wells continue to need nickel alloy bar and forgings in completion tools, hangers, packers and subsea pressure parts. TechnipFMC's 2026 memorandum with bp covers 20K systems for the Paleogene programme. The 10 K does not name the alloy. The relevant product form, on the VDM benchmark, is bar and billet in alloys such as Alloy 718 CTP and Alloy 625, which the brochure also ties to API 6A CRA.

Clad and lined pipe, and weld overlay on carbon steel, are the growth form relative to solid nickel pipe. The VDM benchmark sells plate and strip into long seam welded pipe and sells welding strip and wire, including FM 625 and FM 825 CTP, for cladding. BUTTING's clad range (mechanically clad BuBi, bonded GluBi, metallurgically clad pipe, clad spools and SmartClad) is the fabricator end of that chain. The website does not name the alloy grades or the strip supplier.

Umbilical tubing is a duplex and super duplex market that is moving up in strength rather than toward solid nickel. Alleima's SAF 2507 (UNS S32750) is the established offshore umbilical grade. SAF 3007 (UNS S83072) is presented as the lighter and stronger successor. Chomutov in the Czech Republic makes coiled and straight umbilical tube.

LNG liquefaction is the large equipment market of this decade. Named projects on the October 2026 tracker include Golden Pass at 21.2 billion cubic metres a year, Port Arthur Phase 1 and Phase 2 at 18.4 billion cubic metres a year each, Rio Grande Trains 1 to 3 at 23.9 billion cubic metres a year, Louisiana LNG at 22.4 billion cubic metres a year, LNG Canada Phase 1 at 19.0 billion cubic metres a year, Mozambique LNG at 17.5 billion cubic metres a year with first LNG targeted for 2029, NLNG Train 7 at 10.3 billion cubic metres a year, and North Field East at 43.5 billion cubic metres a year (32.0 million tonnes a year), now delayed. Linde Engineering designs LNG plants and reported a project backlog, but it does not publish alloy tonnes per plant. Cryogenic nickel steel plate for tanks is a different product from the sour service nickel alloys in the VDM benchmark. This note does not assign a nickel percentage to any named LNG tank.

Sour gas treatment and acid gas handling remain inside ISO 15156, which covers wells, wellheads, flowlines, field facilities and natural gas treatment plants, and which does not by itself cover refineries. Downstream refinery investment, on World Energy Investment 2025, was set to fall to a ten year low in 2025. That is a reason not to treat refinery capex as the alloy opportunity.

## 4. Materials required

Nickel alloys. The benchmark brochure lists plate, sheet, strip, rod, bar, wire, billet, welding consumables and powder. For sour production it highlights rod and bar in Alloy 718 CTP, Alloy 625 and Alloy K 500 for hangers, packers, completion tools and pump shafts. Plate and strip in Alloy 625 (UNS N06625) and Alloy 825 (UNS N08825) are marked to API 5LD for lined and clad pipe. Welding products FM 625 and FM 825 CTP are marked for cladding of low alloy pipe. Other alloys on the ISO 15156-3 rows read from the brochure are Alloy 22, Alloy 59, Alloy 400, Alloy 405, Alloy 825 CTP, Alloy 926 and Alloy C 276. The column layout of the PDF extract is messy, so the count of eleven should be checked on the original pages before it is printed as a hard claim. Haynes, in the same group, supplies the C 276 family in sheet, plate, strip, tube, wire and bar. Its last standalone 10 K is fiscal 2023. Oil and gas sat inside an "other" market and is not a published revenue line after the Acerinox purchase. Special Metals supplies Inconel and Incoloy in billet, bar, rod, wire, flat products, tubulars, powder and welding products. Alleima competes in seamless tube with Sanicro 625, outside diameter 6 to 42 millimetres, and Sanicro 925.

Why these alloys. ISO 15156-3 sets environmental limits by alloy group and metallurgical condition for cracking in hydrogen sulphide. A grade name is not acceptance for every well. Duplex grades are used where chloride is high and hydrogen sulphide is still inside the duplex envelope. Solid solution nickel alloys are specified when temperature, hydrogen sulphide and chloride go beyond that envelope, and as the corrosion layer in clad and overlay where a solid nickel wall would be unnecessary. This note does not copy partial pressure limits from commercial websites, because those limits were not read from the standard itself.

Duplex. Outokumpu's flat grades Forta DX 2205 and Forta SDX 2507, and Alleima's SAF 2507 and SAF 3007 tube, are the duplex and super duplex products documented here. They compete with nickel alloy where the service is only mildly sour, and they are the usual umbilical metals. Outokumpu also lists Ultra 825 as an Alloy 825 equivalent in flat product, which is a nickel alloy overlap, not a duplex grade.

Clad pipe. The product form the company can sell is strip, plate and welding wire. The pipe maker supplies the carbon steel wall and the fabrication. BUTTING says clad pipe can save up to 40 percent against solid wall pipe. Thinner liners and overlays reduce the alloy kilograms per metre of line. That is a loss of tonnes and a reason the qualified filler and strip business still matters.

ISO 15156 and NACE MR0175 are the same joint standard in the current edition sold as ANSI/NACE MR0175-2021 / ISO 15156:2020, parts 1, 2 and 3, covering carbon and low alloy steels, corrosion resistant alloys and other alloys for hydrogen sulphide service in oil and gas production and in natural gas treatment plants.

That demand geography is not the same as the geography of alloy intensive deepwater projects.

The Energy Institute says the Americas produced 20 percent more oil than the Middle East in 2025, and that India, China and Europe imported 86 percent, 73 percent and 75 percent of their oil consumption. Those are trade dependence figures, not alloy demand.

## 7. Competitor map

Acerinox High Performance Alloys (one group). VDM benchmark forms are bar and billet for sour completions, plate and strip for clad and welded pipe, and welding wire and strip for overlay. Haynes adds US melting and the C 276 family, with aerospace and chemical processing as the stronger published markets. A US platform of about USD 200 million, including a vacuum induction furnace and bar finishing, is aimed at 2028. At VDM, welding wire at Werdohl has run since December 2024, a strip expansion since August 2025, and a new remelting plant was commissioned in June 2026. Strength is qualification breadth and the welding filler range. Weakness in this sector is the company's own comment that oil and gas lacked new projects in 2025. VDM and Haynes must not be scored as separate competitors.

Special Metals. Strength is the Inconel and Incoloy franchise, a full mill at Huntington, and a parent that also forges and casts, so it can sit on both sides of a subsea order. Oil and gas is acknowledged as a market and, inside Precision Castparts, power products for that market declined in 2025. No oil and gas revenue is published.

Alleima. Strength is melt to tube integration, umbilical tube at Chomutov, and oil country tubular goods. A fourth VAR furnace at Sandviken was operational in autumn 2025 for high nickel and stainless bar and tube, including oil and gas. The third quarter of 2026 cancellation of an oil country tubular goods order of about SEK 250 million shows the volume risk in that form. Strategy stated at the November 2025 capital markets day is to move toward more highly alloyed nickel grades in bar and tube.

Outokumpu. Strength today is duplex and super duplex plate and coil (Forta DX 2205, Forta SDX 2507) and a wide Avesta grade book, including Ultra 825. The threat to the company is the EVOLVE programme at Avesta, about EUR 150 million for about 13,000 tonnes of flat high nickel capacity. Phase 1 is EUR 30 million of remelting equipment, operational early 2028. Phase 2 is a go or no go decision in the first half of 2027 and production at the end of 2029 if it proceeds. Target markets include energy, oil and gas, electrical equipment and defence. The August 2026 investor presentation calls high nickel alloys, defined there as 5.5 to 12 percent nickel, a pocket of growth of 5.5 to 12 percent a year. That rate is a company claim across several markets, not an oil and gas statistic. European oil and gas projects were on hold in 2025, so the new capacity is not full of oil and gas orders today.

BUTTING is not a mill competitor. It is the reference clad pipe fabricator and therefore a route to market for strip and plate, and a reason solid alloy pipe loses tonnes.

## 8. Where high performance metals gain or lose relevance

They gain where hydrogen sulphide, chloride, temperature and pressure push the service outside duplex limits, and where a thin qualified layer is enough. That is sour completion bar, weld overlay and clad strip, and selected heat exchanger and treatment equipment in gas plants. They also gain, in a limited way, if operators specify solid CRA tubulars for the most severe wells. Alleima's order book shows that this last pocket is lumpy.

They lose tonnes, not necessarily relevance, when clad and overlay replace solid nickel pipe. BUTTING's claim of cost savings of up to 40 percent is the commercial version of that shift. They lose applications when hyper duplex replaces super duplex in umbilicals, because that stays inside stainless steel. Alleima's SAF 3007 is the documented example. They do not automatically gain from the LNG capacity wave. Most of that capex is carbon steel, stainless and cryogenic nickel steel plate. Sour service nickel alloys are a small part of an LNG plant unless the feed gas is sour. Flexible pipe, which TechnipFMC describes as steel and polymer layers, is an alternative to rigid clad flowlines in some subsea layouts and is not a clad pipe order.

Composites and ceramics are not documented in the sources read here as replacements for sour service bar or for ISO 15156 tubulars. TODO(data): a sourced comparison of composite spoolable pipe or ceramic coatings against nickel alloy cladding in oil and gas was not found in the IEA, Energy Institute or company filings used for this note.

Coated carbon steel and stainless overlays take the mild end of corrosion service. That is already how duplex and 300 series stainless limit the nickel alloy share. The standard itself forces the step up only when the environment exceeds the lower alloy table.

## 9. Where the value chain is attractive for the company

The attractive positions are the ones where a qualification is hard to copy and the product form survives the shift to less alloy per project.

Welding wire and strip for overlay and clad pipe is the first. Operators and pipe makers are trying to use less alloy per metre, and the VDM benchmark already sells FM 625 and FM 825 CTP into that use. BUTTING's economics push projects toward this form. The company should treat pipe fabricators as the channel. Volume grows more slowly than upstream capex, because each line uses a thinner layer.

Bar and billet for sour completion tools and subsea forgings is the second. ISO 15156 and API 6A CRA keep the supplier list short. Buyers are SLB, TechnipFMC and the other tree and completion shops, not the oil company itself. The 2026 deepwater names in the filings are Brazil, Suriname, Mozambique and the US Paleogene programme. Alleima and Special Metals are the competitors in this form. Haynes is not an outside competitor. It is part of the Acerinox group with VDM.

Flat high nickel plate is attractive only if the company is already qualified, because Outokumpu intends to add about 13,000 tonnes of capacity by the end of the decade. Waiting until that melt shop is approved would mean selling into a more crowded plate market. The 2025 comment from Outokumpu that European oil and gas projects were on hold is a reason to tie plate offers to the export projects in the Americas and Africa rather than to European refinery work.

Solid nickel oil country tubular goods and nickel umbilical tube are the weak volume bets. Alleima is integrated from melt to tube, umbilical demand is moving toward hyper duplex, and a large tubular order was cancelled in 2026. Supplying billet to a tube mill is a different and narrower sale than trying to become the tube mill.

LNG project lists are a useful map of where gas spending is, and a poor map of where high nickel alloy tonnes are. Follow them when the scope includes sour gas treatment or a named CRA heat exchanger. Do not scale alloy demand from the 330 billion cubic metre capacity wave or from the USD 330 billion gas supply budget.

Regions to work first, on the 2026 investment evidence, are Brazil, Guyana, Suriname, Angola, Namibia, Nigeria, Mozambique and selected US Gulf deepwater projects. The Middle East is a recovery case after the conflict, which is how Acerinox described it, not the 2026 growth pocket. Qatar's delayed liquefaction trains matter for cryogenic plate and for project timing. They are not, on the evidence here, a near term surge in sour service nickel alloy.
