# Chemical processing

Scope: NACE C20 chemical output is kept separate from demand for corrosion resistant alloys. Chemical sales, production indices and capacity utilisation describe the plants. Alloy demand is the plate, sheet, strip, tube, bar and weld consumables those plants and their fabricators buy when the medium, temperature and weld procedure justify a premium grade. No sourced figure converts one into the other.

The company is the high performance alloy producer. VDM Metals and Haynes International belong to Acerinox and are one competitor. The VDM grade list is an application benchmark only.

## Key findings

1. EU27 chemical turnover was about EUR 635 billion in 2024, with 1.2 million people employed. Europe's share of world chemical sales fell from 21 percent in 2009 to 13 percent in 2024, and China's rose from 24 percent to 46 percent, in nominal sales. World chemical sales in 2024 were 3.6 times the 2004 level. The same Cefic profile page also says China holds 45 percent of global sales. The 46 percent figure is the one tied to the 2009 to 2024 comparison and to the 15 December 2025 launch note [CEFIC_EU27_SALES_2024, CEFIC_EU_SHARE_2024, CEFIC_CN_SHARE_2024, CEFIC_WORLD_VS_2004].
2. Volume is weaker than turnover. The Eurostat industrial production index for NACE C20, calendar adjusted, 2021 = 100, was 83.5 in 2024 and 80.8 in 2025 for the EU27, a fall of 3.2 percent. Germany moved from 81.0 to 78.6, a fall of 3.0 percent. Cefic reports EU27 chemical production down 2.0 percent in January to July 2026 against 2025, while January to June sales rose 2.1 percent because producer prices rose 5.1 percent [EUROSTAT_C20_EU27_2025, EUROSTAT_C20_DE_2025, CEFIC_PROD_JANJUL_2026, CEFIC_SALES_H1_2026].
3. Plants are running with spare capacity. Cefic, using Eurostat NACE 20, puts EU27 chemical capacity utilisation at 75.0 percent in the third quarter of 2026, against a long term average of 81.3 percent. Germany was 72.9 percent and Belgium 71.8 percent. VCI puts average utilisation of German chemical plants at 72.5 percent for the whole of 2025 [CEFIC_CAPUTIL_EU27_Q3_2026, CEFIC_CAPUTIL_DE_Q3_2026, VCI_CAPUTIL_2025].
4. There is no primary tonne or euro series for corrosion resistant alloy plate, sheet, tube and weld consumables used in chemical processing, and none to 2035. The usable slices are company disclosures. Haynes, last standalone year ended 30 September 2023, took 15.6 percent of sales from chemical processing. Volume fell 20.8 percent and revenue was flat because the price per pound rose 26.7 percent. Quarterly chemical processing revenue sums to USD 92 million [HAYNES_CPI_SHARE_FY2023, HAYNES_CPI_REV_FY2023]. Alleima's chemical and petrochemical revenue was SEK 3,035 million in 2025, 16 percent of group revenue, mostly tube rather than plate [ALLEIMA_CHEM_2025]. Outokumpu's August 2026 presentation puts chemical and petrochemical at 2 percent of group sales [OUTOKUMPU_CHEM_SHARE].
5. Acerinox's high performance alloys division, which is VDM plus Haynes and therefore one group, reported EUR 1,683 million of sales in 2025 across all end markets, not chemical processing alone. Management called chemical processing significantly weaker in 2025 and expected the chemical industry to stay weak through 2026 [ACERINOX_HPA_SALES_2025].
6. New chemical capital expenditure is not following European operating rates. BASF expects over 80 percent of growth in the chemical industry to be concentrated in Asia Pacific by 2035, and states that Asia Pacific, especially China, already contributes almost 70 percent of global chemicals market growth. The Zhanjiang Verbund produced its first products in November 2025. A strategy extract says China, India and five ASEAN countries will account for around 80 percent of global chemical growth by 2035 [BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035, BASF_ZHANJIANG_2025].
7. Nickel supply is not the constraint. USGS estimates world mine production at 3.90 million metric tons of contained nickel in 2025, of which Indonesia was 2.60 million. The World Bank nickel price averaged USD 15,162 per metric ton in 2025, against USD 25,834 in 2022. USGS says stainless and alloy steels and nickel containing alloys typically account for more than 85 percent of U.S. primary nickel use, which is not a chemical processing share [USGS_NI_MINE_2025, WB_NICKEL_AVG_2025, USGS_NI_ALLOY_SHARE].
8. German exports of HS 7506 nickel plate, sheet, strip and foil were USD 580 million and 14,237 metric tons in 2023, and USD 578 million and 17,775 metric tons in 2024. Value was flat while weight rose about 25 percent. This series mixes pure nickel and nickel alloys and all end uses. It is not chemical processing demand [COMTRADE_DE_7506_2024].

The public note does not give a path to 2035. An older ACC note, 2024, said U.S. chemical demand could grow nearly 15 percent by 2033 and capacity about 10 percent. That is not the latest outlook and is not used as the forecast [ACC_US_VOL_2025, ACC_CAPEX_2025].

Near term chemical growth to the early 2030s is therefore a split story. Europe is in a volume recession with low utilisation. The United States is barely growing in volume, with capital spending still positive. Asia carries the growth. BASF's 2025 report, material investments section, which BASF marks as voluntary and outside the statutory audit, says over 80 percent of chemical industry growth will be concentrated in Asia Pacific by 2035. The strategy PDF says seven countries (China, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam) will account for around 80 percent of global chemical growth by 2035. Those are company expectations about chemical production, not alloy tonnes [BASF_APAC_GROWTH_2035].

### Corrosion resistant alloy demand

Do not scale the EUR 635 billion, the STAN output, or the ACC volume rates into alloy demand. Equipment orders follow project sanctions, turnarounds and the severity of the service, not chemical tonnes.

What is sized:

- Haynes fiscal 2023 chemical processing sales were 15.6 percent of total sales. Net revenue was USD 590 million, and the four fiscal 2023 quarters of chemical processing revenue (USD 22.7 million, 28.6 million, 17.7 million and 23.0 million) sum to USD 92 million. Volume was down 20.8 percent and the average selling price per pound was up 26.7 percent, so revenue was flat. Sheet, coil and plate together were about 64 percent of net product revenue. This is the last year Haynes published a chemical split. It is not a 2025 or 2035 market [HAYNES_CPI_SHARE_FY2023, HAYNES_CPI_REV_FY2023, HAYNES_FLAT_SHARE_FY2023].
- Alleima fiscal 2025 chemical and petrochemical revenue was SEK 3,035 million, shown as 16 percent of group revenue of SEK 18,630 million. The prior year line in the same note is SEK 3,411 million and 17 percent. The offering in that segment is hydraulic and instrumentation tubing and heat exchanger tubing, plus bar, not plate mills. Organic group growth was 0 percent, and management said short cycle industrial and chemical and petrochemical demand declined [ALLEIMA_CHEM_2025, ALLEIMA_REV_2025].

- Acerinox high performance alloys sales of EUR 1,683 million in 2025 cover aerospace, industrial gas turbines, oil and gas, chemical processing and other markets. The chemical share inside that EUR 1,683 million is not published [ACERINOX_HPA_SALES_2025].

## 2. Growth drivers and risks

Drivers that can still create alloy orders while European chemical volumes fall:

- Replacement and turnaround of an installed base of acid, caustic and halogen equipment in Europe and the United States. Low utilisation delays some shutdowns, but it does not remove corrosion. Haynes in fiscal 2023 already shifted the chemical book toward higher value alloys and away from what it called the commodity part of the chemical processing market.
- New complexes where production is growing. BASF's Zhanjiang Verbund, Geismar MDI expansion (380,000 metric tons a year to about 600,000, startup 2026), and Ludwigshafen plants for semiconductor grade sulfuric acid and electronic grade ammonium hydroxide (startup 2027) are the concrete projects in the 2025 report. Alloy grades are not named [BASF_MDI_2026, BASF_LUDWIGSHAFEN_ACID_2027].
- U.S. chemical capital spending of USD 39.8 billion in 2025, still rising, on ACC's account, even though volumes are almost flat. Linde's project backlog was USD 10.0 billion at the end of 2025, and 2025 capital expenditure was USD 5,261 million. Linde Engineering builds air separation, hydrogen, syngas and LNG plants. That is a buyer of cryogenic nickel steel, stainless and nickel alloy equipment, with tonnes not disclosed [LINDE_BACKLOG_2025, ACC_CAPEX_2025].
- A lower nickel price cuts the surcharge, which can make a premium grade easier to specify, and it also cuts mill revenue per ton if volume does not rise. Acerinox said in the first half of 2026 that the nickel price decline was expected to affect alloy surcharges. The World Bank annual average fell from USD 25,834 per metric ton in 2022 to USD 15,162 in 2025 [WB_NICKEL_AVG_2025].

Risks:

- European demand destruction and import pressure. Cefic's 15 December 2025 note says European gas prices were still three times U.S. prices, capacity utilisation was 9.5 percent below the 2014 to 2019 level, and China had become the EU's leading source of chemical imports. The August 2026 trends file, page 7, says European gas was EUR 59.1 per MWh in July 2026 against about EUR 11.1 per MWh in the United States, more than five times. Extra EU27 chemical trade surplus fell by EUR 2.2 billion to EUR 21.4 billion in January to July 2026 [CEFIC_GAS_JUL_2026, CEFIC_SURPLUS_2026].
- Project slippage in the chemical segment itself. Alleima's third quarter 2026 report says the chemical and petrochemical segment slowed, alongside a weak oil country tubular market (a cancelled order of about SEK 250 million). Outokumpu said European project business in energy, oil, gas and chemicals was on hold in 2025. Acerinox expects the chemical industry to remain weak through 2026.
- VCI, 13 March 2026, withdrew a reliable 2026 forecast because of the Iran war and the risk of shortages of ammonia, phosphate, helium and sulfur, and of a Strait of Hormuz disruption. That is a chemical feedstock risk, not a nickel risk, but it freezes equipment decisions.
- Mix and substitution, treated in section 8. A recovered chemical volume that is specified in duplex or in clad plate does not restore solid C-276 plate tonnes.

## 3. Emerging technologies and applications

The applications that change the specification, as opposed to a repeat order for an existing acid plant, are these.

Semiconductor chemicals. BASF is building semiconductor grade sulfuric acid and electronic grade ammonium hydroxide at Ludwigshafen for startup in 2027, aimed at European chip plants. High purity sulfuric acid is a classic zirconium and nickel alloy problem once concentration and temperature leave the stainless range. BASF does not name the grade. Treat the plant as a prospect for plate, tube and weld filler, not as a booked alloy order [BASF_LUDWIGSHAFEN_ACID_2027].

Isocyanates and chlorinated intermediates. The Geismar MDI expansion handles phosgene and hydrogen chloride chemistry. The Nickel Institute names C-276 as the alloy used once stainless and superduplex grades are exceeded, and USGS notes that titanium alloys substitute for nickel alloys in corrosive environments. The BASF filing does not name the metal, so Geismar is a prospect list, not a specified grade [BASF_MDI_2026].

Hydrogen, syngas and ammonia. Linde's 2025 shareholder letter cites oxygen and nitrogen supply to the Blue Point low carbon ammonia project and describes blue and green hydrogen. Reformers and high temperature hydrogen service use heat resistant nickel alloys and stainless. Linde's PEM electrolysis route, including the 24 MW Leuna plant with ITM Power, is low in nickel alloy content. Do not treat electrolyser megawatts as C-276 demand. Alkaline cells are a nickel cathode market and were not quantified in the filings read.

High nickel flat products moving down from superalloys into energy and process. Outokumpu's August 2026 presentation says specialty products grow faster than standard stainless, with higher margins and lower cyclicality, and names energy, oil and gas, electrical and electronics, and defence. The Avesta program adds about 13,000 metric tons of flat high nickel alloy capacity. That is a supply side entry into the company's flat product space, not proof that chemical processing will absorb 13,000 metric tons [OUTOKUMPU_HNI_KT].

Weld overlay and clad construction. Where solid alloy plate loses a bid to carbon steel plus an Alloy 625 or C-276 overlay, the mill loses plate tonnes and can keep weld wire and strip. VDM started welding wire production at Werdohl in December 2024. That is the form that follows overlay practice [ACERINOX_WELD_WIRE_2024].

A claimed growth rate of 5.5 to 12 percent a year for high nickel alloys, which appears in an earlier Outokumpu company note, is not in the August 2026 slides that were read. Those slides say "faster growth than standard stainless" and do not give a percent. The 5.5 to 12 percent rate is not used.

## 4. Materials, grades and product forms

Selection is by medium, concentration, temperature, crevice geometry and whether the joint is welded. The Nickel Institute process engineering note sets the ladder. Type 304 for cryogenic, caustic and oxidizing acids. Type 316 where molybdenum is needed for chlorides and reducing acids. Duplex 2205 (UNS S32205), 6 percent molybdenum austenitics (UNS S31254 and UNS N08367) and superduplex 2507 (UNS S32750) when 316 pits or cracks. Nickel alloys, of which C-276 (UNS N10276) is the best known, when those stainless grades are exceeded. The Institute names derivatives UNS N06059, N06200 and N06686 for more specific environments. Alloy 59 in the company's benchmark list is the N06059 type [NI_PROCESS].

## 5. Geographic concentration of demand

Chemical production and alloy orders do not share a map.

Chemical output, nominal and volume:

- EU27 is a large installed industry (EUR 635 billion of sales in 2024) with a shrinking world share and a falling volume index (80.8 in 2025 against 100 in 2021).
- Germany is the core European location in the STAN file (EUR 166 billion of C20 output in 2023) and one of the weakest on utilisation (VCI 72.5 percent in 2025, Cefic 72.9 percent in the third quarter of 2026). Cefic's January to July 2026 commentary says output was still declining in Germany, the Netherlands and Italy, while Spain grew 2.8 percent and France stabilised.
- The United States is the largest STAN output in this file (USD 659 billion in 2024) with almost no volume growth in the ACC forecast.
- Asia Pacific is where BASF places over 80 percent of chemical industry growth through 2035. Alleima's tube revenue is already shifting: Asia organic growth was 23 percent in the Tube division in 2025, while Europe Tube revenue fell 10 percent (SEK 6,442 million against SEK 7,417 million).

Alloy relevant geography, from the evidence that exists:

- Maintenance and replacement: Europe and North America, where the mills and the fabricators already are, and where Haynes, VDM and Alleima report a weak but continuing chemical business rather than a new build boom.
- New tube finishing close to growth markets: Alleima, third quarter 2026, SEK 220 million at Mehsana, India, for further processing and finishing, explicitly including chemical and petrochemical.
- New high nickel flat capacity aimed at several end markets including process: Outokumpu at Avesta, Sweden, serving global customers, not a German demand boom.
- German HS 7506 exports in 2024, all uses, went first by value to Austria (USD 91 million), Italy (USD 70 million), China (USD 61 million), the United States (USD 55 million) and France (USD 53 million). Austria as the largest partner is consistent with distribution and further processing, not with a single chemical customer. Do not read the partner list as a chemical demand map [COMTRADE_DE_7506_2024].

## 6. Customers and competitors

Customers are operators, licensors and equipment makers. The filings name the operators and the engineering companies. They rarely name the mill.

- BASF is a buyer of equipment, not of mill plate. Sales were EUR 59,657 million in 2025. Capex falls from a 2024 peak (Zhanjiang) to about EUR 3 billion in 2026 and about EUR 13 billion over 2026 to 2029, planned below depreciation. European closures (adipic acid and related intermediates at Ludwigshafen in 2025) reduce the local installed base. Growth capex is in China, Geismar and selected European specialty plants. No alloy supplier is named [BASF_SALES_2025].
- Linde is an engineering and gas company. Sales were USD 33,986 million in 2025, operating profit USD 8,923 million, project backlog USD 10.0 billion, capital expenditure USD 5,261 million. Fourth quarter 2025 volume growth of 2 percent came mainly from electronics and from chemicals and energy start ups. Engineering order intake in that quarter was USD 434 million against sales of USD 615 million, so the equipment backlog was drawn down. Third quarter 2026 engineering sales were USD 582 million. Alloy tonnes per plant are not published [LINDE_SALES_2025].
- Alleima describes chemical companies and EPC contractors as customers and does not name them. Outokumpu names Metso for hydrometallurgical equipment in duplex and lean duplex, which is process equipment adjacent to chemical leaching, not a C-276 vessel order.

Competitors, counted as the task requires. VDM and Haynes are one group.

- Acerinox high performance alloys (VDM Metals in Werdohl, Altena and Unna, plus Haynes in Kokomo, Arcadia and Mountain Home). One competitor. Flat products, bar, wire, powder, welding wire, and Haynes tube. Not two shares of the market.
- Special Metals, inside Precision Castparts and Berkshire Hathaway. Inconel and Incoloy. Billet, bar, rod, wire, sheet, strip, plate, tubulars and welding products. Berkshire's fiscal 2025 10-K names chemical processing among the uses of PCC's nickel, titanium and cobalt alloys. Special Metals revenue is not broken out. PCC revenue was USD 10.8 billion in 2025, mostly aerospace structures and castings, so that total is not an alloy market size.
- Outokumpu. Stainless flat products, with duplex and high nickel grades, and a stated plan to add about 13,000 metric tons of high nickel flat capacity. Chemical and petrochemical is 2 percent of sales. This is the competitor for high performance stainless plate and, if phase 2 proceeds, for part of the nickel alloy plate market.
- Alleima. Seamless tube and bar in advanced stainless and nickel alloys. Chemical and petrochemical is 16 percent of revenue. This is the competitor for tube, not for wide plate.
- Nippon Yakin Kogyo. Nickel alloy and stainless flat products in Japan, including NAS825 and NAS800 types, with its own ferronickel at Oheyama. The integrated report was not opened. Sales and a chemical end market split are not used as findings. See gaps.

## 8. Where high performance metals gain or lose relevance

Gains, with a source:

- Severe mixed acids, wet chlorine and hydrochloric acid, where the Nickel Institute says super austenitic and superduplex grades are no longer enough and C-276 or a named derivative is specified. Overlay in Alloy 625 keeps a nickel alloy in the joint even when the wall is steel.
- Acetic acid and sulfuric acid up to about 70 percent at high temperature, where ATI positions zirconium 702 and 705 ahead of nickel alloys. That is a gain for zirconium and a loss for nickel alloy plate in those media.
- Wet chlorine and other oxidizing chloride duties where USGS records titanium as a substitute for nickel alloys. Titanium gains the exchanger tube. C-276 remains where titanium is not suitable, including many reducing acids.
- High nickel flat and tube where Outokumpu and Alleima both describe the high alloy end as the part of the book they want to grow, even while standard chemical project work is weak.

Losses, with a source:

- Austenitic stainless replaced by low nickel duplex or ultrahigh chromium stainless in construction, and nickel free specialty steels in power and petrochemical service. USGS states both substitutions in the 2026 nickel chapter. They hit commodity stainless and the bottom of the chemical alloy range, not C-276.
- Solid alloy plate replaced by clad plate or weld overlay. The metal stays in the plant in a thinner section or as filler. Plate tonnes fall. Wire and strip can rise. No tonne ratio was found.
- Haynes fiscal 2023 already abandoned part of the lower value chemical processing volume. That is a commercial loss of tonnes, not a technical substitution, and it kept revenue flat for that year only.
- European new build. If the plant is not sanctioned, every grade loses. Utilisation at 75 percent in the EU27, and chemical weakness through 2026 on Acerinox's account, is the current version of that loss.

Composites and coated carbon steel are not evidenced as replacements for pressure equipment in the sources read. Linings and graphite or tantalum for the most extreme acids are industry practice and were not given a sourced share. ## 9. Attractive parts of the value chain for the company

The attractive step is not chemical production and not standard stainless coil. It is the qualified corrosion resistant semi, in the forms fabricators already buy from this type of mill, where the specification is written to a nickel alloy or to zirconium and the order survives a European volume recession.

More attractive:

- Plate, sheet and strip in C-276, 625, 825 and Alloy 59 for vessels, exchanger plates and shells, sold to fabricators and equipment makers who serve BASF type operators and Linde type engineers. Haynes's own mix in fiscal 2023 shows that this is where price per pound offset lost tonnes. The company should treat that as a quality of mix, not as a growing tonnage.
- Matching weld wire and strip. Overlay and clad construction take plate share away and leave the consumable. VDM's Werdohl line, inside the competitor group, shows the competitor has already added this form (December 2024).
- Replacement and upgrade of the European and North American installed base, while new greenfield capex is qualified through the same fabricators on Asian and U.S. projects. Prospecting should follow equipment makers who export, not a country share of chemical sales.
- Zirconium for acetic acid and for sulfuric acid in the window ATI documents, if the company's benchmark range in zirconium is real capacity and not only a catalogue line. Tonnes are small. The differentiation is high because few mills melt it.
- Tested life cycle cost against 6Mo and superduplex, so a premium grade is proposed only where 316 and duplex fail. Outokumpu's 2 percent chemical share is the stainless market. It is not the company's market.

Less attractive:

- Broad exposure to European commodity plant builds. Utilisation, the volume index and Acerinox's 2026 comment all point the same way.
- Seamless heat exchanger tube as a volume business. Alleima has the chemical tube revenue (SEK 3,035 million), the size range in 625, and new finishing in India. Competing there without a tube mill is a different company.
- Standard austenitic flat products for tanks and pipe. Outokumpu's scale, and a chemical end use that is 2 percent of its sales, is a price market.
- Counting Haynes and VDM as two competitors, or using the VDM catalogue as evidence of the company's own customers.

## Corrections to the existing section 5.5

The draft's Cefic figures are kept.
