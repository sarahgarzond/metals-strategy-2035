# Aluminium

## Key findings
1. World primary (smelter) aluminium production reached about 74,000 thousand metric tons in 2025 and 72,800 thousand metric tons in 2024 on a USGS rounded basis, with China alone at 45,000 thousand metric tons in 2025 (about 61% of the world total) [USGS_MCS2026_AL, USGS_MCS2026_DATA].
2. The International Aluminium Institute reports world primary production of 73.784 million tonnes in 2025 and 73.009 million tonnes in 2024, within about 1% of the USGS rounded smelter totals, confirming the same China-centred structure [IAI_PRIMARY_2025].
3. United States apparent aluminium consumption was 5,700 thousand metric tons in 2025, with net import reliance of 60% of apparent consumption, while U.S. primary smelter output was only 660 thousand metric tons [USGS_MCS2026_AL].
4. World Bank Pink Sheet aluminium averaged USD 2,632 per metric ton in 2025 and closed December 2025 at USD 2,875.53 per metric ton, above the 2019 average of USD 1,794.5 [WB_PINKSHEET, AL_PINK_2025].
5. EU27 sold production of aluminium plates, sheets and strip (PRODCOM 24422430) was about 440 thousand tonnes in 2024 (rounded flag) with a sold value of about EUR 1.12 billion, of which Germany alone reported 244,216 tonnes and EUR 307 million [EUROSTAT_PRODCOM, data/clean/prodcom_2019-2024.csv].
6. European primary capacity has contracted sharply: FACE cites EU primary output near 955 kt in 2024 versus 3,233 kt in 2004, while European Aluminium states that European demand for primary aluminium is about 9 million tonnes per year [FACE_EU_AL_2024, EA_DEMAND_PRIMARY].
7. For the company, aluminium is mainly a competing and complementary mill product (aerospace plate and sheet, heat exchangers, busbars) rather than a core nickel alloy franchise, so the strategic question is substitution and customer dual sourcing, not smelting.

## Production
USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 reports world smelter production of 74,000 thousand metric tons in 2025 (rounded) and 72,800 in 2024. Country smelter production in 2025 includes China 45,000, India 4,200, Russia 3,900, Canada 3,300, United Arab Emirates 2,700, Bahrain 1,600, Australia 1,500, Norway 1,300, Brazil 1,200 and the United States 660 (all thousand metric tons of primary metal) [USGS_MCS2026_AL]. Bauxite mine production on a dry tonnage basis was about 440,000 thousand metric dry tons worldwide in 2025, led by Guinea 150,000, Australia 97,000 and China 87,000 [USGS_MCS2026_BAUXITE]. Figures are primary smelter metal or bauxite dry tons as labelled, not wrought mill product.

IAI primary aluminium production for 2025 is 73.784 million tonnes versus USGS 74.0 million tonnes rounded, a difference of about 0.3% that is treated as rounding and estimate coverage rather than a competing world total [IAI_PRIMARY_2025, USGS_MCS2026_AL].

## Consumption
U.S. apparent consumption was 5,700 thousand metric tons in 2025, 5,830 in 2024 and 6,210 in 2023 [USGS_MCS2026_AL]. European Aluminium states yearly European demand for primary aluminium of about 9 million tonnes and forecasts total European aluminium demand of 18 million tonnes in 2050 [EA_DEMAND_PRIMARY]. The IEA World Energy Outlook 2025 links aluminium to electrified systems, cable and clean technology demand and notes that aluminium can substitute for copper in some conductors [WEO2025_AL]. Consumption figures above are primary and semi-fabricated metal, not alloy mill product for high performance applications alone.

## Applications
Aerospace uses aluminium plate and sheet for airframes alongside titanium and nickel superalloys in engines. Automotive uses sheet for body and battery enclosures. Electrical uses include cable and busbars where copper is displaced on cost or mass. Packaging and construction dominate tonnage but are outside the company's high performance focus. Product forms of interest are plate, sheet, strip, foil and wire (PRODCOM 24422430 for plates, sheets and strip).

## Prices
World Bank Pink Sheet aluminium ($/mt): 2019 average 1,794.5, 2022 average 2,705.0, 2023 average 2,255.7, 2024 average 2,419.0, 2025 average 2,631.7, December 2025 2,875.53 [WB_PINKSHEET, AL_PINK_2025]. USGS U.S. spot ingot averaged 180 cents per pound in 2025, 129.5 in 2024 and 125.9 in 2023 [USGS_MCS2026_AL]. Price drivers cited in primary outlooks are power costs at smelters, China capacity policy near the 45 Mt ceiling, and trade measures on steel and aluminium [WEO2025_AL, IAI_PRIMARY_2025].

## Capacity and supply
China's smelter output of 45 Mt in 2025 sits at the policy ceiling discussed by IAI-linked reporting [USGS_MCS2026_AL, IAI_PRIMARY_2025]. EU primary output near 955 kt in 2024 with nine smelters, versus 3,233 kt and 25 smelters in 2004, shows a lasting European capacity loss [FACE_EU_AL_2024]. European Aluminium reports that the EU lost about 30% of primary capacity since 2008 and about 1 Mt (52%) after 2020 to 2023 under high power prices [EA_DEMAND_PRIMARY]. Secondary (scrap) production in the United States exceeded primary in 2025 (old scrap 1,600 and new scrap 2,000 thousand metric tons versus primary 660) [USGS_MCS2026_AL].

## Outlook to 2035
European Aluminium projects European aluminium demand of 18 Mt in 2050, with clean technologies as a growth driver [EA_DEMAND_PRIMARY]. WEO 2025 treats aluminium as an electrification and industry energy demand metal and flags recycling rates under different scenarios [WEO2025_AL]. For high performance mill products, aerospace plate and auto sheet grow with aircraft deliveries and vehicle light-weighting, while copper-to-aluminium substitution in conductors is application specific. No team estimate of a 2035 high performance aluminium addressable value is stated without a comparable wrought series.

## Company evidence
1. Airbus SE FY 2025 results press release and investor materials report group revenue of EUR 73,420 million and a commercial aircraft order book of 8,754 aircraft, which pulls aluminium airframe plate and sheet as well as titanium and nickel engine alloys [research/companies/airbus.md].
2. Boeing Form 10-K for fiscal 2025 reports revenue of USD 89,463 million and a commercial backlog above 6,300 aircraft at end 2025, another airframe aluminium demand anchor [research/companies/boeing.md].
3. Acerinox 2025 annual report shows High Performance Alloys (VDM Metals and Haynes) sales of EUR 1,683 million. That division is a nickel alloy peer, not an aluminium mill, so aluminium appears as a substitute or companion material at shared aerospace customers [research/companies/acerinox.md].
4. Eramet 2025 annual report and earnings release describe manganese, nickel and lithium mining, not aluminium smelting, so the company is adjacent critical minerals supply rather than an aluminium mill competitor [research/companies/eramet.md].

## Spotlight facts
1. In 2025 China smelted 45,000 thousand metric tons of primary aluminium, about 61% of the USGS world total of 74,000 thousand metric tons, concentrating primary supply in one jurisdiction [USGS_MCS2026_AL].
2. In 2024 Germany sold 244,216 tonnes of aluminium plates, sheets and strip (PRODCOM 24422430) for EUR 307.1 million, a concrete EU mill product form relevant to aerospace and industrial sheet [EUROSTAT_PRODCOM].
3. In December 2025 the World Bank Pink Sheet aluminium price was USD 2,875.53 per metric ton, versus a 2019 average of USD 1,794.5, a rise of about 60% on that comparison [WB_PINKSHEET].
