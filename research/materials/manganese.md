## Key findings
1. World mine production of manganese, on a manganese-content basis, was about 20,000 thousand metric tons in 2025 and 18,700 in 2024 (USGS rounded), led by South Africa 7,600, Gabon 5,000, Ghana 2,000 and Australia 1,600 thousand metric tons in 2025 [USGS_MCS2026_MN, data/clean/usgs_mcs2026.csv].
2. IMnI Annual Review 2025 reports global manganese ore production of 21.4 million metric tons metal contained in 2025, up 10% year on year, with South Africa, Gabon, Australia and Ghana together about 81% of output [IMNI_AR_2025].
3. The United States mined no manganese in 2025 and had 100% net import reliance. Apparent consumption on a manganese-content basis was about 640 thousand metric tons in 2025 [USGS_MCS2026_MN].
4. World reserves on a manganese-content basis were about 1.7 to 1.8 million thousand metric tons in 2025, concentrated in Australia (580,000), South Africa (550,000), Brazil (300,000) and China (260,000) thousand metric tons [USGS_MCS2026_MN].
5. IMnI reports high-carbon ferromanganese production near 4 million metric tons in 2025 (up 2%) and refined ferromanganese about 1.8 million metric tons (up 11%), with China and India together about 70% of HC FeMn and 72% of refined FeMn [IMNI_AR_2025].
6. The World Bank Pink Sheet has no manganese price series (data/clean/worldbank_pinksheet_monthly.csv). USGS reports a U.S. average ore price (manganese content, cif) of about USD 4.5 per metric ton unit in 2025, USD 5.53 in 2024 and USD 4.8 in 2023 [USGS_MCS2026_MN].
7. For high performance alloys, manganese is an alloying addition (stainless, Hadfield, some nickel grades) and a battery sulphate feedstock, not a freestanding mill product franchise for the company. Bulk steel alloys dominate tonnage. OECD STAN output for stainless-related manufacturing (data/clean/oecd_stan_P1.csv, activity C24 where available) is the industrial demand backdrop, and Eurostat PRODCOM stainless codes in data/clean/prodcom_2019-2024.csv show EU mill activity that consumes manganese in the melt [EUROSTAT_PRODCOM, OECD_STAN_P1].

## Production
USGS MCS 2026 mine production (manganese content, thousand metric tons, 2025 estimated): South Africa 7,600, Gabon 5,000, Ghana 2,000, Australia 1,600, Brazil 800, India 790, China 700, Côte d’Ivoire 350, other countries 1,300, world total rounded 20,000 [USGS_MCS2026_MN]. IMnI’s 21.4 Mt metal contained for 2025 is about 7% above the USGS rounded world total. The difference is treated as coverage and grade aggregation. For country ranking both sources put South Africa first and Gabon second [IMNI_AR_2025, USGS_MCS2026_MN].

## Consumption
U.S. apparent consumption, manganese content, was 640 thousand metric tons in 2025, 670 in 2024 and 653 in 2023 [USGS_MCS2026_MN]. USGS states that manganese consumption closely follows the steel industry and cites World Steel Association finished steel consumption as unchanged in 2025 versus 2024 [USGS_MCS2026_MN]. Battery grade manganese sulphate is a smaller but faster growing pocket. WEO 2025 notes that manganese-rich battery chemistries are gaining share and that China dominates manganese sulphate supply [WEO2025_MN].

## Applications
1. Steel: ferromanganese and silicomanganese as bulk alloy additions.
2. Stainless and specialty steels: manganese as an austenite former in 200-series and as a controlled addition in other grades.
3. Batteries: electrolytic manganese metal and manganese sulphate for LFP and manganese-rich cathodes.
4. High performance alloys: limited tonnage relative to steel. Product forms relevant to the company are alloy content inside stainless and nickel strip, plate and bar, not mined ore.

## Prices
Pink Sheet: not applicable (no manganese series). USGS average unit value for manganese content in ore, cif, USD 4.5 per metric ton unit in 2025, 5.53 in 2024, 4.8 in 2023. USGS also notes that China 44% grade spot prices fell about 22% year to date to October 2025 versus the 2024 annual average [USGS_MCS2026_MN].

## Capacity and supply
South Africa and Gabon dominate ore. IMnI reports Gabon about 19% and South Africa about 46% of 2025 ore production in its country pie narrative [IMNI_AR_2025]. Australia’s northern mine resumed in May 2025 after a 2024 cyclone stoppage [USGS_MCS2026_MN]. China and India dominate ferroalloy conversion. Gabon’s planned ore export ban from January 2029 would force more conversion in country if enforced [WEO2025_MN].

## Outlook to 2035
Steel remains the volume driver. Battery sulphate adds a second growth path under electrification scenarios in WEO 2025 [WEO2025_MN]. High performance alloy demand for manganese grows only with stainless and specialty melt, a small share of the 20 Mt mine total. No separate 2035 team estimate is stated for manganese mill product.

## Company evidence
1. Eramet 2025 investor results and annual report guidance target manganese transport of 6.4 to 6.8 Mt in 2026 from Gabon, making it a primary ore supplier rather than a competitor in nickel alloy mill products [research/companies/eramet.md].
2. Outokumpu Annual Report 2025 describes Tornio and Avesta stainless melt shops that consume manganese as an alloying input, so ore price and ferroalloy availability affect stainless cost rather than a separate manganese product line [research/companies/outokumpu.md].
3. Acerinox 2025 annual report covers stainless melt that uses manganese as an alloy addition in standard and specialty stainless [research/companies/acerinox.md].
4. Aperam 2025 annual report and investor presentation cover stainless and electrical steel routes that take manganese as a melt addition [research/companies/aperam.md].

## Spotlight facts
1. In 2025 South Africa produced about 7,600 thousand metric tons of manganese content, roughly 38% of the USGS world mine total of 20,000 thousand metric tons [USGS_MCS2026_MN].
2. In 2025 IMnI reports global manganese ore production of 21.4 million metric tons metal contained, the highest since 2021 on that series [IMNI_AR_2025].
3. Gabon announced an intention to ban raw manganese ore exports from January 2029, which would re-route ore currently shipped for overseas ferroalloy conversion [WEO2025_MN].
