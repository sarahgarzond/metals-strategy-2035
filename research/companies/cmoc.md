## Segments and end markets
CMOC mines copper and cobalt in the DRC (Tenke Fungurume and Kisanfu), plus molybdenum and tungsten in China, niobium and phosphate in Brazil and gold. 2025 output: copper 741,100 t, cobalt 117,549 t, all from the DRC [CO_CMOC_COPPER_PROD_2025, CO_CMOC_COBALT_PROD_2025] (2). ## Relevance to the Client
CMOC is a supplier of cobalt (hydroxide and metal) for Co containing alloys and a source of molybdenum and tungsten, which matter for Alloy C-276 type NiMoCr grades and Ni base superalloys. It is not an alloy competitor. Molybdenum is a direct input to the Client's corrosion resistant grades.

## Market statements
- CMOC kept cobalt guidance unchanged while pushing copper growth (2).
- The DRC suspended cobalt exports from 22 February to 15 October 2025 and then moved to quotas for the fourth quarter of 2025 and for 2026 and 2027. Fourth quarter quotas were extended to 31 March 2026 because of slow processing (3).
- CMOC's export entitlement for 2026 is 31,200 t against a basic national quota of 96,600 t. That equals about 27 percent of its 2025 output [CO_CMOC_COBALT_QUOTA_2026] (3).
