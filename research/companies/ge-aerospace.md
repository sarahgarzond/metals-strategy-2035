# GE Aerospace (customer, United States)

## Snapshot
* Fiscal 2025 revenue was USD 45,855 million, up 18%, with an operating profit margin of 21.4% (non-GAAP) and about 57,000 employees [CO_GE_REVENUE_2025, CO_GE_OPMARGIN_2025, CO_GE_EMPLOYEES_2025]. Source: Form 10-K 2025, pp. 5 and 8.
* Aftermarket is about 70% of revenue and the installed base is about 50,000 commercial and 30,000 military engines (10-K p. 4).
* Remaining performance obligation was USD 190.6 billion, of which USD 163.0 billion is services [CO_GE_RPO_2025].
* After Q3 2026 guidance is adjusted revenue growth of 19 to 20% and operating profit of USD 10.9 to 11.0 billion [CO_GE_Q3GUIDE_2026].

## Segments and end markets
* Commercial Engines & Services: revenue USD 33,314 million (73% of group), segment profit USD 8,861 million, margin 26.6%, services 75% of segment revenue [CO_GE_CES_2025].
* Defense & Propulsion Technologies: revenue USD 10,554 million, profit USD 1,296 million, margin 12.3%, defense engine deliveries 635 versus 490 in 2024 [CO_GE_DPT_2025, CO_GE_DEFENG_2025].
* Commercial engine deliveries were 2,386 in 2025, of which 1,802 LEAP [CO_GE_LEAP_2025]. Internal shop visit revenue grew 24% [CO_GE_SHOPVISIT_2025].
* First nine months of 2026: LEAP deliveries 1,565 (up 26%), commercial engines 1,989 (up 25%), Defense & Systems engines 586 (up 11%) [CO_GE_9M26_DELIV, CO_GE_9M26_DEF]. Full year LEAP growth guidance is in the high teens [CO_GE_Q2LEAP_2026].
* Engine families: LEAP (CFM with Safran), GE9X, GEnx, F110, F404, F414, T408. Aeroderivatives move to the defense segment from 2026 (10-K p. 8).

## Relevance to the Client
* Primary demand pull for nickel superalloy: HPT blades, vanes, discs, seals and cases for LEAP, GE9X and GEnx, supplied as castings, forgings and powder products through its tier one suppliers.
* The Client is a customer of the supply chain, not of GE directly. Whether the Client holds GE specification approvals is TODO(data).
* Direct competitor interest: GE is vertically integrating castings, which reduces open market volume for alloy sold to independent foundries but raises total alloy demand.

## Capacity and investments
* Agreement signed in Q3 2026 to buy Consolidated Precision Products (castings and sub-assemblies) for USD 11.75 billion, about USD 7 billion cash, closing expected in the second half of 2027 [CO_GE_CPP_2026]. Source: 10-Q Q3 2026, p. 5.
* CPP supplies about a quarter of GE casting needs per Jefferies, and GE expects airfoil demand up more than 30% by 2030 from 2026 levels. GE keeps ATI and Howmet as suppliers [CO_GE_CPP_PRESS_2026] (secondary).
* USD 1 billion to expand MRO capacity, of which USD 500 million for LEAP MRO sites, and USD 1 billion for US manufacturing in 2025 with 5,000 hires [CO_GE_MROINV_2025]. Internal LEAP capacity is to roughly double [CO_GE_Q2LEAP_2026].

## Market statements
* "We operate in a supply-constrained environment that has impacted our industry for the past several years" (10-K p. 6).
* "Some of our suppliers or their sub-suppliers are limited- or sole-source suppliers" and a new supplier "can require substantial time to implement, particularly if it involves new regulatory certifications" (10-K pp. 6 and 27 to 28).
* "Global material availability and supplier delivery performance continue to cause disruptions" (10-K p. 8). Deliveries rose "primarily due to improved material supply" (10-K p. 9).
* Q3 2026: "priority supplier material input increasing by double-digits year-over-year again in 3Q, marking the 10th consecutive quarter of sequential growth" [CO_GE_Q3GUIDE_2026]. The 10-Q still expects supply constraints to affect production and inflation to persist (p. 4).
* GE9X engines were delivered in Q3 2026 with revenue recognition after midseal certification, targeted by end 2026.
* LEAP 1B durability kit with an upgraded HPT blade is certified and expected to roughly double time on wing (10-Q p. 6). This lowers shop visit frequency for that engine, a demand risk for spare part alloy.

## Customers and suppliers
* Airline wins 2026: IndiGo MOU for more than 1,000 LEAP 1A, Turkish Airlines up to 300 LEAP 1B, Ryanair material services agreement for about 2,000 CFM56 and LEAP engines, United and Delta GEnx (10-Q p. 6).
* Premier MRO network now includes Iberia as seventh partner.
* Named suppliers in the sources: CPP (to be acquired), ATI, Howmet. Long term supply agreements with metal producers: not disclosed, TODO(data).
