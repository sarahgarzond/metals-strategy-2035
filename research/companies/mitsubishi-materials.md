# Mitsubishi Materials (competitor, JP)

## Snapshot
For the fiscal year ended 31 March 2026, Mitsubishi Materials Corporation reported net sales of 1,844,053 million yen, operating profit of 60,502 million yen, an operating profit to net sales ratio of 3.3 percent, ordinary profit of 97,556 million yen, and profit attributable to owners of parent of 40,581 million yen (Earnings Highlights page, retrieved 7 November 2026). The forecast for the year ending 31 March 2027 is net sales of 2,400,000 million yen, operating profit of 130,000 million yen and ordinary profit of 180,000 million yen (Earnings Forecasts page). Consolidated employees were 18,452, and non consolidated employees were 5,315, as of 31 March 2025 (Corporate Profile, May 2025, p. 16). ## Segments and end markets
Under the current classification, Copper and Copper Alloy Products, inside the Materials business area, had net sales of 358.1 billion yen, operating profit of 15.2 billion yen and ordinary profit of 14.2 billion yen in the year ended 31 March 2026. The forecast for the year ending 31 March 2027 is net sales of 455.4 billion yen, operating profit of 10.7 billion yen and ordinary profit of 9.0 billion yen, so the published forecast has higher sales and lower profit (Earnings Forecasts page). Metals and Resources Circulation had net sales of 1,329.0 billion yen and ordinary profit of 29.6 billion yen. Metalworking Solutions, the cemented carbide tool business, had net sales of 147.2 billion yen and ordinary profit of 15.1 billion yen. An earlier series through March 2025 showed copper alloy sales of 433.6 billion yen and ordinary profit of minus 1.0 billion yen, on a different perimeter from the 2026 classification (Results by Segment page).

## Relevance to the Client
Mitsubishi Materials competes in copper and copper alloy strip, rod, wire and functional alloys for automobiles, semiconductors and electronic equipment. The corporate profile calls the group Japan's number one manufacturer of wrought copper products by market share, without stating the share (May 2025, p. 9). Products run from billets, cakes and wire rod to strip, sheet, bar, wire, lead frames, forgings, busbars, welding electrodes and superconducting wire (copper business page). The MSP series is a family of copper magnesium alloys. MSP5 is aimed at small terminals, MSP8 at high voltage and high current busbars, and MSP5 ESH, introduced in 2025, is described as a copper magnesium alloy capable of replacing beryllium copper and titanium copper. That substitution statement is the direct link to connector alloys in the electronics chapter. Oxygen free copper under the MOFC HR name is positioned for electric vehicle equipment, data centers and AI. Luvata, acquired in 2017, is described as 12 manufacturing and sales sites in 7 countries, including superconducting wire for MRI magnets. The overlap with the company is in copper alloy product forms for electronics and electrical uses, not in nickel superalloy plate.

## Capacity and investments
Three directly controlled Japanese bases are named on the current business page: the Osaka Plant, the Wakamatsu Plant, and the Onahama Smelting and Casting Division. Oxygen free copper is cast from electrolytic copper made at the Naoshima smelter and refinery and the Onahama smelter and refinery. The pages do not give strip tonnes or rolling mill capacity. A product note of 14 July 2026 says the new alloy MSP5 SSH combines strength in the 1 GPa class with high electrical conductivity, for smaller and higher current components in AI servers and robotics (mitsubishi-copper.com news list). On 28 October 2026 the group announced a reorganization of production in Malaysia. On 6 August 2026 it announced a technical assistance agreement with KITZ Metal Works Corporation so that supply of certain copper and copper alloy products would continue.

## Market statements
The business page says demand is growing with 5G, CASE and higher voltage automotive electronics, and that the group is raising production to meet it. It also says MOFC HR can replace copper alloys that have low electrical conductivity, and that MSP5 ESH can replace beryllium copper and titanium copper. Those are the substitution statements in the primary text. No selling prices or lead times are given.

## Customers and suppliers
Customer names are not disclosed. Licensing is named. ECOBRASS, a lead free brass, is licensed to Wieland in Europe and to Chase Brass in the United States. GloBrass, developed in 2020, has an exclusive license for Europe and North America held by Luvata Oy. Electrolytic copper feedstock is internal, from Naoshima and Onahama. The May 2025 profile mentions cobalt recovery at a copper mine in Chile and studies of lithium, cobalt and nickel recycling from batteries, without publishing tonnes (p. 8).
