# Vertiv (customer, US)

## Snapshot
* Net sales in 2025 were USD 10,229.9m, up 27.7%, with operating profit of USD 1,829.7m, an operating margin of 17.9% derived from both figures [CO_VERTIV_REVENUE_2025, CO_VERTIV_OPINC_2025]. Source: 10-K for 2025, Item 7, p. 39.
* Vertiv employed about 34,000 people at 31 December 2025, and 41% worked in manufacturing [CO_VERTIV_EMPLOYEES_2025]. Source: 10-K, p. 10.
* In the third quarter of 2026 net sales were USD 4,312.8m, up 61.2%, and operating profit was USD 983.8m, a margin of 22.8% [CO_VERTIV_Q3_2026]. Source: 10-Q filed 28 October 2026.

## Segments and end markets
Vertiv reports three regional segments. In 2025 the Americas delivered USD 6,386.3m (up 41.9%, margin 26.8%), Asia Pacific USD 2,019.2m (up 17.5%, margin 11.0%) and EMEA the remainder (10-K, pp. 39 and 40). End markets are data centres (hyperscale, colocation, neocloud and enterprise), communication networks, and commercial and industrial sites (10-K, Item 1). ## Relevance to the Client
Vertiv sells switchgear, busbar, busway, power shelves, coolant distribution units and chillers, so its metal basket is copper, aluminium and steel, as the 10-K names these three as raw materials (p. 18). It is the clearest example of a buyer that will be affected when NVIDIA moves racks from 54 VDC to 800 VDC. For nickel alloys the link is indirect, through liquid cooling parts such as heat exchangers, valves and fittings, where Vertiv names no nickel grades. ## Capacity and investments
Since late 2021 Vertiv has more than doubled its manufacturing capacity for switchgear, busbar and integrated power solutions [CO_VERTIV_CAPACITY_2025]. Capital expenditure was USD 226.4m in 2025 and is planned at USD 425m to 525m in 2026 (10-K, p. 41), and the third quarter 2026 filing says investment will rise further. Acquisitions include Great Lakes Data Racks & Cabinets for about USD 200m in August 2025, PurgeRite for liquid cooling services in December 2025, and further liquid cooling and heat rejection businesses for USD 336.5m gross consideration in 2026 (10-K p. 7, 10-Q p. 10). ## Market statements
* Backlog reached USD 15.0bn at the end of 2025 against USD 7.2bn a year earlier, with customers placing orders ahead of Vertiv's ability to fulfil them [CO_VERTIV_BACKLOG_2025]. The majority is firm and expected to ship within 12 to 18 months (10-K, p. 7).
* The 10-K says that raw materials including steel, copper and aluminium may face shortages or delays because of strong demand and supplier constraints (p. 18).
* Vertiv hedged 12,945.6 metric tons of copper and 12,333.0 metric tons of aluminium at 30 September 2026, up from 8,754.8 and 10,310.0 metric tons at year end 2025 [CO_VERTIV_COPPERHEDGE_2026]. These are hedge volumes and not total purchases.
* Vertiv stated in May 2025 that its 800 VDC portfolio, with centralised rectifiers, DC busways, rack DC to DC converters and DC backup, would be available in the second half of 2026, ahead of Kyber and Rubin Ultra, and that 800 VDC reduces copper use, current and thermal losses [CO_VERTIV_800VDC_2025]. ## Customers and suppliers
The 10-K names Microsoft and Amazon Web Services as hyperscale examples, CoreWeave and Nebius as neocloud examples, and Goldman Sachs and J.P. Morgan as enterprise examples (Item 1). Orders from NVIDIA are not stated. Metal suppliers are not named, and the 10-K says materials come from more than one supplier.
