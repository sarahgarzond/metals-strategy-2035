# Samsung Display (customer, end user, South Korea)

## Snapshot
* Samsung Display Corporation (SDC) is a subsidiary of Samsung Electronics and publishes no standalone annual report in the sources read, so the figures below come from the Samsung Electronics quarterly releases.
* Third quarter 2026 revenue (Samsung Electronics release of 29 October 2026) was KRW 8.0 trillion and operating profit KRW 1.1 trillion, a margin of 13.8% derived from both [CO_SDC_Q3_2026]. (Samsung Electronics release of 29 October 2026).

## Segments and end markets
SDC reports small and medium displays, mainly smartphone OLED, and large displays for monitors and TV. The third quarter release says large display sales grew on the gaming monitor market. For IT, SDC plans sales expansion through the ramp up of the new 8.6G line, which targets notebooks and tablets [CO_SDC_Q3_2026]. ## Relevance to the Client
SDC is an end user of FeNi36, also called Invar, because its vacuum evaporation lines deposit organic layers through fine metal masks (FMM) etched from FeNi36 foil. Both SDC and BOE use FMM evaporation with tandem OLED on their Gen 8.6 lines, whereas Visionox and TCL CSOT pursue FMM free routes [CO_SDC_FMM_8G6_2025]. The larger the glass, the more mask area and foil per line, which links 8.6G directly to demand for thin FeNi36 strip. SDC does not buy foil itself in the sources read, since mask makers do. ## Capacity and investments
* The A6 line at Asan, Chungnam, is a conversion of the L8 line with an investment of KRW 4.1 trillion and uses glass of 2,290 mm by 2,620 mm [CO_SDC_A6_INVEST_2023].
* Reported yields passed 90% in May 2026 and full scale production was targeted for June 2026, while earlier reports named January and May [CO_SDC_A6_YIELD_2026]. The exact start date of mass production therefore remains unresolved in the sources.
* Samsung Electronics plans SDC capital expenditure of KRW 3.9 trillion for 2026, of which KRW 2.4 trillion was spent by September, focused on upgrades to existing lines [CO_SDC_CAPEX_2026].
* One line is operating and a second may be activated depending on demand, according to trade reports. Monthly substrate capacity is TODO(data) as SDC does not publish it.

## Market statements
* Samsung Electronics wrote that SDC expects a positive year end seasonal effect in the fourth quarter of 2026 but only limited earnings improvement, and that it expects continued weak market demand in 2027 amid uncertainty, with IT sales expansion to come from the ramp up of the new 8.6G line [CO_SDC_Q3_2026].
* Trade reports say the A6 panels go into the first OLED MacBook Pro from Apple, with Samsung as sole supplier according to Counterpoint, and forecasts range from 3 million to 5 million units per year. These claims come from secondary coverage only and therefore carry a low confidence rating.
