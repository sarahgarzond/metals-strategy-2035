# Carpenter Technology (competitor, United States)
## Snapshot
- Fiscal 2026 (year to 30 Jun 2026): net sales USD 3,124.2m (+9%), operating income USD 702.0m, 22.5% of net sales, a record [1, p. 20, 23].
- About 4,500 employees [1, p. 5].
- Q1 fiscal 2027 (to 30 Sep 2026): net sales USD 798.2m (+9%), operating income USD 196.4m (USD 153.3m a year earlier) [2, p. 29].

## Segments and end markets
- SAO (premium alloys and stainless) USD 2,826.8m (+10%), operating income USD 782.9m (27.7%). PEP (titanium, powder, Dynamet, additive) USD 383.0m, operating income USD 30.1m (7.9%) [1, p. 28].
- Aerospace and defense USD 2,035.2m (65%, +15%), medical USD 278.4m (9%, down 21%), energy USD 230.6m (7%, +15%), transportation USD 100.4m (3%), industrial and consumer USD 401.8m (13%) [1, p. 20].
- Pounds sold 207.9m, up 8% [1, p. 20]. About 43% of net sales are under firm price arrangements [1, p. 21].

## Relevance to the Client
Competitor in nickel and cobalt superalloys, stainless and powders for aerospace engines, fasteners and defense (missile applications), and for medical and energy. ATI names Carpenter as a competitor in legacy nickel alloys [ATI 10-K]. Carpenter focuses on bar, billet, wire and powder and less on flat products or tubes, so overlap with a flat and strip nickel producer is partial.

## Capacity and investments
- Mills in Reading and Latrobe PA and Athens AL, plus Orwigsburg PA, Elyria OH, Hartsville SC; titanium in Washington PA and Clearwater FL; powder in Athens AL [1, p. 14].
- Capex USD 242.7m in FY2026 (FY2025: 154.3m), mainly the Athens brownfield expansion. FY2027 guidance USD 355m to 375m [1, p. 35; 2, p. 34].

## Market statements
- Aerospace growth is "driven by increasing build rates and the need to maintain and replace aging fleets" [1, p. 24].
- Price realisation: "pricing realization across both long-term and transactional business" [1, p. 22].
- Medical softness reflects customers "managing inventory levels closely" [1, p. 24].
- Risk factors: nickel, cobalt, chromium, molybdenum and titanium costs volatile, lead times long, substitution by lower cost materials possible [1, p. 7].
- Q1 FY2027: results reflect "strengthening demand, productivity improvements, mix management and the benefits of pricing actions" [2, p. 28].
