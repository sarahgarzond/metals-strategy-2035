## Relevance to the Client
* Competitor for nickel base and iron nickel superalloy bar, forging stock and wire for aero engines, gas turbines and energy plant, and for corrosion resistant remelted grades used in semiconductor equipment [CO_DAIDO_VAR_2025].
* Aims for what trade press calls Asia's first prime certification for nickel base alloy used in large rotating parts and turbopumps for aerospace engines. This claim has one source [CO_DAIDO_HIGHALLOY_2026].

## Capacity and investments
* High Alloy Process Reform Project, about JPY 36 billion (USD 232 million), restructures high alloy production [CO_DAIDO_HIGHALLOY_2026].
* Shibukawa plant becomes the mother plant for high alloy melting, with one of Japan's largest four side forging machines, more melting capacity and automated inspection [CO_DAIDO_HIGHALLOY_2026].
* Shibukawa today runs one VAR and a 25 tonne VIM, and has no space for another VAR [CO_DAIDO_HIGHALLOY_2026].
* Chita No. 2 plant receives the post forging steps, two special melting units and a new heat treatment, processing and inspection line [CO_DAIDO_HIGHALLOY_2026].
* Earlier plan: two VAR furnaces at Chita No. 2 for JPY 5.2 billion, start planned at the end of fiscal 2024 [CO_DAIDO_VAR_2025]. One search summary quoted JPY 3.3 billion for a superalloy process project. The two figures were not reconciled, so TODO(data).
