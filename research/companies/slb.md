## Segments and end markets
Divisions 2025: Digital USD 2.7 billion (margin 28%), Reservoir Performance 6.8 billion (18%), Well Construction 11.9 billion (19%, down 11%), Production Systems 13.3 billion (16%, up 12% with five months of ChampionX, flat without it) (10-K p. 17 and 18). Excluding ChampionX revenue of USD 1.5 billion, revenue fell 6% on lower activity in Saudi Arabia, Mexico and offshore Sub-Saharan Africa. Data Center Solutions grew 121% in North America. International revenue fell 5%.

## Relevance to the Client
SLB is a buyer of corrosion resistant alloy (CRA) components, not a producer of alloy. Production Systems includes completions, wellheads, trees and, through the 70% owned SLB OneSubsea joint venture (Aker Solutions 20%, Subsea7 10%), subsea trees, manifolds, flowline connectors and controls (10-K p. 4). These are forged or cast pressure parts in low alloy steel with weld overlay or solid nickel alloy (Alloy 625, 718, 825) in sour and high pressure service. ## Capacity and investments
Capital investments (capex, APS investments and capitalized exploration data) were USD 2.4 billion in 2025 and are expected at about USD 2.5 billion in 2026 (10-K p. 22). SLB acquired ChampionX in 2025 in an all stock deal, with USD 879 million of fourth quarter revenue (10-K p. 17). On 31 August 2026 SLB agreed to buy Kelvion (thermal management for data centers) for about USD 3.4 billion in cash plus USD 0.7 billion assumed debt [CO_SLB_Q3_2026].
