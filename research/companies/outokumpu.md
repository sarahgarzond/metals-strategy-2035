## Snapshot
FY2025 (calendar year ended 31 December 2025): net sales EUR 5,468 million (EUR 5,942m in 2024), adjusted EBITDA EUR 167 million (3.1% margin), EBIT EUR −134 million, net result EUR −137 million, 8,605 employees in close to 30 countries, 1,751 kt stainless steel deliveries. Shares listed on Nasdaq Helsinki. Headquarters in Helsinki, Finland. Source: Annual Report 2025 p. 4 and 6; Q4/2025 Financial Statements Release.

## Segments and end markets
Outokumpu reports three segments in 2025. Europe contributed net sales of EUR 3,600 million and adjusted EBITDA of EUR −46 million, reflecting sustained pressure from Asian imports and weak European demand. Americas generated EUR 1,676 million in sales and EUR 102 million in adjusted EBITDA, recovering through H2 2025 as U.S. tariffs redirected buyer orders to domestic producers. Ferrochrome generated EUR 462 million in sales and EUR 138 million in adjusted EBITDA, the third consecutive year of improvement; the Kemi mine in Finland is the only chromite mine within the European Union. Customer segments by sales value (FY2025) include distributors and metal processors (the largest share), automotive, consumer goods, heavy industries, and chemical and petrochemical (reported as approximately 2% of revenues). The Europe business area sells roughly 60% of flat products directly to end users and 40% through distributors; the Americas split is 27% end user and 73% distributor. The EVOLVE strategy, announced in 2025, adds advanced materials and alloys, ferrochrome and innovative technologies alongside the core stainless franchise.

## Relevance to the Client
Outokumpu competes directly with the company in flat stainless (duplex, superaustenitic, superferritic) for chemical processing, oil and gas, energy and heavy industry. Its Avesta (Sweden) and Degerfors operations produce grades that overlap with the company's portfolio: Forta DX 2205, Forta SDX 2507, Ultra 825 (Alloy 825 equivalent), Ultra 904L, Ultra 254 SMO and Ultra 6XN. The EVOLVE strategy announced a two-phase EUR approximately 150 million program at Avesta to add approximately 13 kt of flat high-nickel alloy capacity, targeting energy, oil and gas, electrical and electronics and defence markets. Phase 1 (EUR 30 million remelting equipment, operational early 2028) and Phase 2 (greenfield melt and casting, go/no-go decision H1 2027, production end 2029) would bring Outokumpu into direct competition with the company in high-nickel flat products, a market where the company currently holds strong positions.

## Capacity and investments
Tornio (Finland) is the most integrated stainless steel plant in the world, with annual melting capacity of 1.2 million tonnes; it focuses on austenitic and ferritic standard grades (Moda, Core, Supra families). Avesta (Sweden) has a melt shop of 500 kt/yr, hot rolling mill of 900 kt/yr and cold rolling mill of 160 kt/yr; it produces more than 200 unique alloy compositions and wide coils up to 2 m with thickness 1.5 to 13 mm. Degerfors operates a quarto plate mill. Krefeld and Dillenburg (Germany) are cold rolling units. Terneuzen (Netherlands) is a finishing unit. Total group production capacity by function: melting 2,800 kt (Finland and Sweden), hot rolling approximately 2,320 kt, cold rolling approximately 1,880 kt across all sites. FY2025 capital expenditure was EUR 145 million (FY2024: EUR 216 million). Outokumpu holds a 28% share of the European stainless steel flat market.

## Market statements
Outokumpu management stated in the Q4 2025 release and annual report that "demand in Europe and North America remained weak across major end-use sectors" throughout 2025, and that "recovery in key end markets in 2025 was slower than expected." European project business in energy, oil, gas and chemicals was described as "on hold." The company characterised the 2025 environment as one of "subdued demand driven by rising uncertainty and global trade disruptions." The investor presentation of August 2026 described high-nickel alloys (5.5 to 12% nickel content) as offering "attractive pockets of growth" of 5.5 to 12% per year, with higher margins and reduced cyclicality compared to standard stainless. A hot rolling capability was described as a barrier to new entrants requiring more than EUR 1 billion in investment.

## Customers and suppliers
Named customers include Metso (hydrometallurgical equipment in duplex and lean duplex grades). Sales channels are approximately 60% direct to end users in Europe and 73% through distributors in the Americas. The Kemi chromite mine and Tornio ferrochrome plant supply chromium internally. Chromium and nickel are the principal alloy purchases from external suppliers; names not disclosed.
