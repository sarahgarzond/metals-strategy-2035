# Framatome (customer and competitor, France)
## Snapshot
• Revenue in 2025 was EUR 5,399m, up 15.5% organically, with new orders of EUR 5,924m (trade press) [1]. EDF reports Framatome EBITDA of EUR 641m against EUR 629m, up 1.0%, and order intake of about EUR 5.9bn (EDF results release p.6) [8]. NEI Magazine gives EUR 665m for EBITDA, and the EDF figure is preferred as the primary source.

## Segments and end markets
• Framatome supplies nuclear reactor design, components, fuel, instrumentation and control, and services. The revenue split by segment is TODO(data) because it was not found.
• Growth in 2025 was driven by EPR projects, and Flamanville 3, Hinkley Point C and Sizewell C faced delays and cost overruns by March 2026 [1].
• Fuel contracts include two long term agreements signed in March 2026 with Vattenfall for Forsmark and Ringhals [3].

## Relevance to the Client
• Pressurised water reactor steam generators use thermally treated Alloy 690 tubes, which is the highest value nickel alloy tube application in the fleet. Alleima offers Alloy 690 and Alloy 800 steam generator tubing from Sandviken, Sweden, and expanded those facilities in 2024 [4].
• Framatome buys tubes and bars and builds steam generators and vessel components. Which tube makers supply it, and the tonnage per steam generator, are not published, so TODO(data).
• Zirconium alloy cladding for fuel rods is the second material link to the Client. Framatome's sources for zirconium sponge and cladding tubes are TODO(data) because none were found.
• Industrial relevance to a German producer is as a qualified Alloy 690 supplier for replacement and new build steam generators. This is the analyst assessment and it is not a statement made by the company.

## Capacity and investments
• The NRC approved fabrication with enrichments above 5% at Richland, United States, with production possible from 2027 [5]. A press estimate puts the cost at USD 300m to 400m, which is not a company figure.
• A new workshop for research reactor fuel and medical targets opened at Romans in April 2025 [6].
• A third party site reports a France 2030 supported Le Creusot forging press and more Romans fuel capacity, which is unverified, so TODO(data) [7].
