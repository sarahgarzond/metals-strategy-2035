# NVIDIA (demand driver, US)

## Snapshot
* Fiscal 2026 (year ended 25 January 2026) revenue was USD 215.9bn and operating income USD 130.4bn, an operating margin of 60.4% derived from both figures [CO_NVIDIA_REVENUE_FY2026, CO_NVIDIA_OPINC_FY2026]. Source: 10-K, Item 7, p. 40.
* NVIDIA had about 42,000 employees in 38 countries, of which 31,000 worked in research and development [CO_NVIDIA_EMPLOYEES_FY2026]. Source: 10-K, p. 11.
* In the quarter ended 26 July 2026 revenue reached USD 96.2bn and operating income USD 63.7bn [CO_NVIDIA_Q2FY27_2026]. Source: 10-Q filed 26 August 2026.

## Segments and end markets
NVIDIA reports two segments, Compute & Networking (USD 193.5bn, up 67%) and Graphics (USD 22.5bn, up 57%) [CO_NVIDIA_REVENUE_FY2026]. By end market Data Center delivered USD 193.7bn, split into compute USD 162.4bn and networking USD 31.4bn, followed by Gaming at USD 16.0bn and Automotive at USD 2.3bn [CO_NVIDIA_DCREV_FY2026]. Source: 10-K, p. 40 and p. 79.

Data Center grew from USD 115.2bn in fiscal 2025, and in the July 2026 quarter it was USD 89.0bn, up 117% on the year, with hyperscale at USD 48.7bn [CO_NVIDIA_Q2FY27_2026]. Taiwan headquartered customers bought USD 42.3bn, and 76% of their Data Center purchases were attributed to end customers in the United States and Europe (10-K, p. 79).

## Relevance to the Client
NVIDIA buys no nickel alloys directly, so it matters as a demand driver for the data centre supply chain. Racks, power shelves and cooling loops pull copper, aluminium and stainless steel, while nickel alloys would enter only indirectly, for example through connectors, spring contacts and precision parts at suppliers. ## Capacity and investments
NVIDIA does not run foundries. The 10-K names TSMC and Samsung for wafers, SK Hynix, Micron and Samsung for memory, CoWoS for packaging, and Hon Hai, Wistron and Fabrinet for assembly (p. 8). The company states that its supply chain is concentrated in Asia and that it is expanding into the United States and Latin America. ## Market statements
* Blackwell Ultra GB300 shipped from the second quarter of fiscal 2026, and Rubin is expected to start production shipments in the second half of fiscal 2027 (10-K, p. 6). The 10-Q reports that Vera Rubin began production shipments in the third quarter of fiscal 2027 and that NVIDIA currently experiences certain supply constraints [CO_NVIDIA_Q2FY27_2026].
* The 10-K warns that supply constraints on any single component of a data centre build can reduce revenue (p. 15).
* NVIDIA states that a 54 VDC rack at 1 MW would need up to 200 kg of copper busbar, and it claims about 45% less copper with 800 VDC, with full scale 800 VDC production coinciding with Kyber racks in 2027 [CO_NVIDIA_800VDC_2025]. This was read through a search summary, so the original blog needs a check.
* A trade article puts a Kyber rack at about 600 kW with up to 576 Rubin Ultra accelerators and end to end liquid cooling, with an 800 V sidecar shown at GTC 2025 [CO_NVIDIA_KYBER_2026]. This is a single secondary source.
