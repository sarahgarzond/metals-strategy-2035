# Haynes International (group peer, owned by Acerinox since Nov 2024, United States)
## Snapshot
- Haynes no longer files with the SEC after the Acerinox takeover (November 2024). Last stand alone 10-K: fiscal 2023 (to 30 Sep 2023), net revenues USD 590.0m (+20.3%), operating income USD 57.6m (9.8%, calculated) [1, p. 46].
- Employees: 1,248 full time plus 36 part time (30 Sep 2023) [1, p. 15].

## Segments and end markets
- One segment. Markets: aerospace, chemical processing, industrial gas turbine, other (oil and gas, flue gas desulfurization, automotive, heat treating) [1, p. 46 to 47].
- Fiscal 2023 aerospace revenue up 26.3% (USD 60.4m) on 10.5% more volume; chemical processing volume down 20.8% [1, p. 47]. Revenue by market in USD: TODO(data), not extracted.
- Acerinox 2026: Haynes order backlog grew on defense and aerospace, and industrial gas turbines for data center power [3, p. 8].

## Relevance to the Client
Direct competitor in nickel and cobalt corrosion and heat resistant alloys (HAYNES 282, Hastelloy type grades, C-276 family) in sheet, plate, strip, tube, wire and bar for aerospace and chemical processing. Haynes and VDM Metals belong to the same group, so count them as one competitor. VDM's portfolio is an application benchmark only.

## Capacity and investments
- Kokomo IN: ARC/AOD melting and vacuum induction melting, forge press, four-high Steckel mill (over 12m lb separating force, plate up to 72 inch), 3-high and 2-high hot mills [1, p. 12 to 13]. Arcadia LA: welded and seamless tube. Mountain Home NC: wire and small bar [1, p. 12].
- Acerinox plans about USD 200m in the US platform, ready by 2028: new induction furnace, rotary forge, large diameter bar finishing, long product hot rolling, split between Haynes Kokomo and NAS Ghent [3, p. 8]. In FY2025 long lead VIM components were ordered and the rotary forge frame cast [2, p. 10].
