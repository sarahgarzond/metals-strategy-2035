## Relevance to the Client
Glencore is a supplier of refined class 1 nickel (briquettes, cathode) and cobalt, and of nickel units for the alloy melt shop of a German nickel alloy producer. Cobalt matters for CoCr and Co base alloys and for Ni base superalloy grades. Glencore is not a competitor in alloy products. As a trader it is also a swing seller of Russian and Indonesian material.

## Capacity and investments
- 2026 own sourced nickel guidance is 70 to 80 kt, with the middle of the range expected. Output from January to September was 54.6 kt, up 4 percent, and Q3 alone 18.8 kt [CO_GLENCORE_NICKEL_GUIDANCE_2026, CO_GLENCORE_NICKEL_YTD_2026] (3).

## Market statements
- Own sourced cobalt output was 13.7 kt from January to September 2026, down 52 percent. The company attributes this to the DRC cobalt export quota regime and a focus on copper [CO_GLENCORE_COBALT_YTD_2026] (3).
- Expected DRC export allocation is 22.8 kt for 2026 including 2025 carryover and 18.8 kt for 2027 [CO_GLENCORE_COBALT_QUOTA_2026] (4).
- Glencore said its DRC assets prioritise copper because finished cobalt inventories suffice to deliver into near term quota levels, as reported by Semafor (5). This is supply discipline by quota, not by choice.
