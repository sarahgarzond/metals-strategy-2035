# Wieland (competitor, DE)

## Snapshot
Wieland is a private group, founded in Germany in 1820, and the releases reviewed here do not publish group revenue, an operating margin or a consolidated employee total. ## Segments and end markets
No segment revenue is published. The catalogue on wieland.com covers strip and foil in copper, high performance alloys, brass, bronze, copper nickel and nickel silver, plus sheet, rod, tube, wire, heat exchangers and finished parts for electrification. The commercial overlap with this report is copper alloy strip for electronics and automotive, together with tubes and heat exchangers for cooling and data centers.

## Relevance to the Client
Wieland is a competitor in copper alloy strip and other rolled copper products. The Buffalo plant, bought from Aurubis, supplies a primarily United States customer base with strip and sheet made from copper and copper alloys (Wieland release, 4 September 2024). The East Alton expansion is aimed at copper and copper alloy rolled products for electric vehicles, charging infrastructure and renewable energy (release of 17 January 2024). Copper nickel strip is listed in the catalogue, which is the product form closest to copper nickel uses in the report. Wieland is not described in these sources as a producer of nickel superalloy plate or bar. Mitsubishi Materials states that it has licensed ECOBRASS, a lead free brass, to Wieland for Europe (Mitsubishi Materials copper business page, retrieved 7 November 2026).

## Capacity and investments
On 17 January 2024 Wieland announced a 500 million US dollar investment, subject to state and local incentives, to re equip and modernize the East Alton, Illinois, rolled products plant, including an additional hot rolling mill. The plant's history at the site is described as about 100 years (same release). The Buffalo acquisition closed on 30 August 2024 (Wieland release of 4 September 2024, confirmed by Aurubis, company release 2024/25, p. 10). That Wieland release also refers to the commissioning of the recycling center in Shelbyville, Kentucky. Ground was broken there in June 2022, with operations then planned by the end of 2023 (Wieland release, 5 October 2022). At Vöhringen, the largest German site, Wieland announced an 80 million euro recycling center with about 80 thousand tonnes of annual recycling capacity (5 October 2022). A groundbreaking on 27 May 2024 said the expanded foundry would make pure copper bolts with 100 percent recycled content, create 25 jobs, and keep supplying plants in Europe and parts of Asia with semi finished copper. The 2022 release said the center was planned to start at the beginning of 2025 and that the worldwide recycled content rate would then reach an average of 80 percent. On 24 August 2026 Wieland announced a 15 million US dollar second plant in Delaware, Ohio, in a 100,000 square foot building, with 100 new jobs, to make heat exchangers for data center cooling. The existing Delaware operation had 41 positions. It joined the group through the 2024 acquisition of Onda S.p.A.

## Market statements
Dr. Erwin Mayr, chief executive, said in the East Alton release that copper is integral to the energy transition and is the foundation of a sustainable future, and that the investment positions Wieland as the leader in North American rolled products. Rodrigo De Luca, of Wieland Onda, said on 24 August 2026 that demand for high performance data center cooling is accelerating and that the Delaware investment gives the group the capacity to grow with its customers. No lead times or selling prices are published in these releases.

## Customers and suppliers
Named end customers are not disclosed. The Buffalo release says that plant's customer base is primarily in the United States. The named commercial relationship is the ECOBRASS license from Mitsubishi Materials for Europe. Aurubis was the seller of the Buffalo plant.
