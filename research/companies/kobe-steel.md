# Kobe Steel (competitor, JP)

## Snapshot
Kobe Steel, Ltd. (KOBELCO) reported consolidated net sales of 2,436,581 million yen for the fiscal year ended 31 March 2026, operating profit of 129,883 million yen and ordinary profit of 121,336 million yen (Consolidated Financial Results, 11 May 2026, p. 11). The management narrative rounds the year to net sales of 2,436.5 billion yen and operating profit of 129.8 billion yen, with profit attributable to owners of parent of 93.7 billion yen (p. 5). ROIC was 5.3 percent and free cash flow was 128.0 billion yen (supplementary slides, 11 May 2026, p. 3). The forecast given the same day for the year ending 31 March 2027 is net sales of about 2,560.0 billion yen, operating profit of about 150.0 billion yen and ordinary profit of about 120.0 billion yen (results, p. 7). ## Segments and end markets
The group reports seven segments. Steel and Aluminum had net sales of 996.9 billion yen and ordinary profit of 2.8 billion yen. Inside that, steel product sales were 822.2 billion yen and aluminum sales were 174.6 billion yen (results, p. 5). Aluminum rolled product volume was 263 thousand tons, after Kobelco Automotive Aluminum Rolled Products (China) moved from a subsidiary to an affiliate and dropped out of the consolidation (supplementary slides, p. 20). Advanced Materials, which includes titanium and titanium alloy, copper rolled products and steel powder, had net sales of 332.8 billion yen, up 5.0 percent, and ordinary profit of 8.7 billion yen (results, p. 5). Copper rolled volume was 54 thousand tons and aluminum extrusion volume was 36 thousand tons (supplementary slides, p. 21). Welding had net sales of 96.4 billion yen, up 2.7 percent, and ordinary profit of 5.8 billion yen (results, p. 6). Welding material volume was 227 thousand tons, flat on the year (supplementary slides, p. 22).

## Relevance to the Client
Kobe is a competitor in welding consumables and in titanium for aircraft, and it also rolls copper. The Welding segment sells covered electrodes, wires for automatic and semi automatic welding, fluxes, welding robots and welding systems (results, p. 20). The ESG Data Book 2026 describes consumables for LNG fuel tanks and welding methods for offshore wind (p. 47). Advanced Materials sells titanium and titanium alloy together with steel castings and forgings and aluminum products aimed at shipbuilding and aircraft (results, p. 20, and supplementary slides, p. 9). Those titanium products sit in the same aerospace uses that the report treats as the application benchmark for the company. The filings do not name nickel alloy filler metals. ## Capacity and investments
The published sales volumes are the capacity evidence in the filings. Welding material sales were 227 thousand tons in fiscal 2025. The fiscal 2026 plan is 235 thousand tons, net sales of 100.0 billion yen and ordinary profit of 6.5 billion yen (supplementary slides, p. 14 and p. 22). Named welding companies in the ESG Data Book 2026 are Thai Kobelco Welding Co., Ltd. in Thailand, Kobelco Welding of Europe B.V. in the Netherlands, and Kobelco Welding of Qingdao Co., Ltd. in China (p. 26, p. 27 and p. 38). An impairment of 20.9 billion yen was taken on assets used to make aluminum rolled products, after weak can stock and IT and semiconductor volumes (supplementary slides, p. 8). The Machinery segment, which includes ultra high pressure presses, took orders of 270.1 billion yen, helped by isostatic pressing equipment, and held an order backlog of 241.2 billion yen (results, p. 6). ## Market statements
Management said welding volumes were similar to the prior year in Japan and overseas, while selling prices rose because cost pass through progressed, even though labour and other fixed costs were higher (results, p. 6). For the year to March 2027 it expects welding volumes to rise on a recovery in domestic and overseas demand (p. 7). Aircraft demand is expected to increase, and the products named for that market are titanium and aluminum castings and forgings (supplementary slides, p. 9). Advanced Materials ordinary profit is guided to 10.0 billion yen, primarily because of higher sales volumes for aircraft and related uses (p. 14). Sales to the Middle East were about 1 percent of consolidated net sales in fiscal 2025 (p. 12).

## Customers and suppliers
Customer names are not disclosed. The demand slides group end uses as automobiles, shipbuilding, aircraft, IT and semiconductors, beverage cans, construction and energy (supplementary slides, p. 9 and p. 10). Suppliers of titanium sponge and of welding raw materials are not named.
