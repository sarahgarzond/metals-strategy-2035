## Relevance to the Client
BASF is a buyer of corrosion resistant equipment, not of alloy semi finished products directly. Nickel alloys (Alloy C-276, Alloy 625, Alloy 825) enter BASF sites as reactors, heat exchangers, columns and piping through fabricators and EPC firms. Demand is therefore driven by maintenance, turnaround and replacement budgets and by new Verbund capacity. ## Capacity and investments
1. Capex plan: about EUR 3.4 billion in 2026 and about EUR 13 billion for 2026 to 2029, against EUR 4,267 million in 2025. BASF states 2026 investment is planned below depreciation and amortization (BASF Report 2025, Material Investments and Forecast) [CO_BASF_CAPEX_2026].
2. The new Zhanjiang Verbund site in China is ramping up and about 1,000 staff were hired there in 2025 [CO_BASF_EMPL_2025]. This is where new process plant, and its alloy demand, is shifting.
3. European closures: adipic acid, cyclododecanone and cyclopentanone production at Ludwigshafen ended in 2025 (about 180 jobs), and hydrosulfites production closes after the September 2025 announcement (about 65 jobs) [CO_BASF_CLOSE_2025].
4. Ludwigshafen restructuring charges of EUR 937 million in 2025 and an annual cost savings target by 2026 that sources state as EUR 2.1 billion or EUR 2.3 billion (TODO(data): confirm in the report) [CO_BASF_RESTR_2025].
5. Ludwigshafen construction continues on a semiconductor grade sulfuric acid plant and an electronic grade ammonium hydroxide plant with start up planned for 2027, alloy content and tonnage TODO(data). The Zhanjiang steam cracker began commissioning at the end of December 2025.

## Market statements
1. The annual report forecast: "a further decline in chemical production" in the EU and United States "due to weak demand and, in the case of the EU, high import pressure", and a slight decline in automotive production in 2026 (BASF Report 2025, Outlook). BASF expects over 80% of chemical industry growth to the year 2035 to arise in Asia Pacific (Material Investments).
2. Outlook 2026 raised on 28 October 2026 to EBITDA before special items of EUR 7.4 to 7.8 billion (previous EUR 6.9 to 7.7 billion), free cash flow EUR 1.5 to 2.3 billion, assuming 1.8% growth in chemical production and Brent at USD 80 [CO_BASF_Q3_2026].
3. BASF describes its stance as "putting self help at the heart of our actions" with close attention to cost and cash, which implies tight maintenance budgets in Europe (BASF Report 2025, outlook, page TODO(data)).
