# Infineon (customer, Germany)

## Snapshot
* Fiscal 2025 (to 30 September 2025) revenue was EUR 14,662m with a segment result of EUR 2,560m, a margin of 17.5% against 20.8% a year earlier [CO_INFINEON_REVENUE_FY2025]. Source: Annual Report 2025, p. 3 for employees, group revenue as published in the release.
* Infineon had 57,077 employees at the end of fiscal 2025, down from 58,065 [CO_INFINEON_REVENUE_FY2025].
* Third quarter of fiscal 2026 revenue was a record EUR 4,172m with a segment result margin of 19.1%, and full year revenue guidance is about EUR 16.3bn [CO_INFINEON_Q3FY26_2026]. (analyst call introduction, 5 August 2026).

## Segments and end markets
In fiscal 2025 Infineon reported Automotive at EUR 7,402m (p. 48), Green Industrial Power at EUR 1,631m (p. 49), Power & Sensor Systems at EUR 4,208m (p. 50) and Connected Secure Systems at EUR 1,418m (p. 51) [CO_INFINEON_REVENUE_FY2025]. A revised segment structure applies from the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026. In the first quarter Automotive earned a margin of 22.1% and Green Industrial Power 8.9%, while in the third quarter Power & Sensor Systems earned 24.9% and Green Industrial Power 9.8% (search summaries of quarterly releases). AI data centre power is the growth driver for Power & Sensor Systems. Infineon targeted EUR 1.5bn of AI data centre revenue in fiscal 2026 and EUR 2.5bn in fiscal 2027 in February 2026, up from above EUR 700m in fiscal 2025 [CO_INFINEON_AIREV_2026]. One summary of the August call quotes a figure above EUR 1.6bn for fiscal 2026, and the two numbers are unresolved.

## Relevance to the Client
Infineon is a customer for materials in power module and discrete packaging. Power packages use copper and copper alloy leadframes, and supplier literature names Cu alloys such as C194 and CuNiSi type C7025, and also FeNi alloys, as common leadframe materials [CO_INFINEON_LEADFRAME_2022]. The grade that Infineon itself specifies is TODO(data) because the annual report names none. A German nickel alloy producer would reach Infineon through strip and foil converters, because Infineon buys customised leadframes from Taiwanese suppliers SDI and Jih Lin and other validated suppliers according to a 2022 trade report.

## Capacity and investments
* The Smart Power Fab in Dresden opened on 2 July 2026, several months ahead of plan, after construction began in May 2023 [CO_INFINEON_FAB_2026]. Investment is EUR 5bn, the largest in Infineon's history, and the fab doubles the 300 mm capacity of the Dresden site. It adds about 1,000 direct jobs and produces smart power stages for multiphase voltage regulators in AI servers. The annual report lists copper and gold among its raw materials (p. 76), and wafer starts per month are TODO(data).
* EU Chips Act support of about EUR 1bn is reported by press and is not confirmed in company documents.
* The annual report planned investments of around EUR 2.2bn for fiscal 2026 (p. 68), and the plan is now about EUR 2.7bn after a raise of EUR 500m in February to serve data centre demand [CO_INFINEON_Q3FY26_2026]. The report adds a 300 mm module in Dresden for AI data centres and automotive (p. 33).
* Infineon says it has concluded or is negotiating multi year capacity reservation agreements with leading AI customers, with a cumulative volume in the high single digit billions of euros (secondary summary of the August call).
* Furnaces and metal tonnes do not apply, since Infineon buys metal as strip.

## Market statements
* In May 2025 Infineon and NVIDIA announced work on a power architecture with central 800 V generation for racks of 1 MW and above before the end of the decade [CO_INFINEON_800VDC_2025]. Infineon expects the share of power semiconductors in a centralised HVDC architecture to be similar or higher than in AC distribution.
* The October 2025 follow up covered CoolSiC JFET hot swap and GaN conversion from 800 V to 50 V, 12 V and 6 V.
* Infineon announced a material upgrade of its fiscal 2027 AI revenue outlook for November 2026, which was not available on 7 November 2026.

## Customers and suppliers
NVIDIA is the named AI partner for 800 VDC, and Infineon appears among its silicon partners. Further AI customers are not named in any of the sources that were read here. Leadframe suppliers SDI and Jih Lin are named in a 2022 report. Plants include Villach in Austria and Kulim in Malaysia (trade sources), and metal supply contracts are not published.
