## Relevance to the Client
* Competitor in superalloy bar and forging stock (deformed superalloy) for aero engines, space vehicles and gas turbines in China. Titanium is a neighbouring material for the same engine and airframe buyers.
* Several superalloy grades passed certification with aero engine and space users, and ultra large bar for gas turbines was delivered [CO_WST_CAPACITY_2025]. Relevant for any Client plan that targets Chinese gas turbine demand.

## Capacity and investments
* A superalloy revert (return scrap) processing line was built in house and put into production [CO_WST_CAPACITY_2025].
* Titanium revert intelligent processing line operating, and a titanium electron beam melting furnace expected to start in the first half of 2026 [CO_WST_CAPACITY_2025].
* Superconducting wholly owned subsidiary in Northern Ireland, full production targeted for the end of 2026 [CO_WST_CAPACITY_2025].
