# Lincoln Electric Holdings (competitor in welding consumables and automation, United States)
## Snapshot
Fiscal year 2025 (31 December). Net sales USD 4,233.0 million (2024: USD 4,008.7 million), operating income USD 718.1 million, a margin of 17.0 percent (2024: USD 636.5 million). About 12,000 employees. Manufacturing in 20 countries including Germany, Austria, Italy, Poland, Romania and Spain. Sources [1] Item 1, Note on revenue, XBRL facts [3]. Page numbers are not available in the EDGAR HTML, so items and notes are cited.
## Segments and end markets
Net sales 2025 by segment: Americas Welding USD 2,723.6 million, International Welding USD 930.9 million, Harris Products Group USD 578.6 million [1] segment note. By product line: consumables USD 2,283.1 million (up from USD 2,088.7 million), equipment USD 1,079.6 million, automation USD 870.3 million [1] revenue note. Consumables are therefore 54 percent of sales (own calculation). Major end markets named without percentages: general fabrication, energy (oil and gas, power generation, process industries), heavy industries (heavy fabrication, shipbuilding), automotive and transportation, structural [1] Item 1. No customer exceeds 10 percent of sales [1] Item 1. The 2025 increase came from price on higher input costs, partly offset by lower volume [1] MD&A.
## Relevance to the Client
Lincoln is a customer and a competitor in filler metals. It sells stainless and nickel alloy covered electrodes, wires and fluxes, but the 10-K does not break out nickel based consumables or give their tonnage: TODO(data) searched the 10-K 2025. The raw material list names "steel, brass, copper, silver, aluminum alloys" and not nickel, so nickel is not a disclosed material risk [1] Item 1A. Services include additive manufacturing and wear solutions [1] Item 1. Nickel alloy wire demand therefore has to be sized from outside sources.

## Market statements
"Availability and prices for energy costs and raw materials, including steel, nonferrous metals and chemicals, are subject to volatility" and are influenced by tariffs, supply and demand balances and "anticipated or perceived shortages" [1] Item 1A. Most raw materials are "commercially available from a number of sources and in adequate supply" [1] Item 1A. Capital spending weakness reduced equipment and automation sales in 2025 [1] MD&A. Third quarter 2026 (27 October 2026): net sales USD 1,221.3 million, up 15.1 percent, organic up 14.5 percent led by volume, operating margin 17.3 percent, adjusted 18.0 percent [2]. The CEO cited "higher volumes" and "inflationary headwinds" [2].
