# Aurubis (peer, DE)

## Snapshot
Aurubis AG, whose fiscal year ends on 30 September, generated revenues of 18,171 million euro in 2024/25, up 6 percent from 17,138 million euro, and operating earnings before taxes of 355 million euro, compared with 413 million euro a year earlier (Company Release Fiscal Year 2024/25, p. 2 and p. 5). Operating EBITDA was 589 million euro, operating return on capital employed was 8.8 percent, capital expenditure was 771 million euro, and the average number of employees was 7,070 (Annual Report 2024/25, fiscal year at a glance, and company release, p. 2). IFRS earnings before taxes were 727 million euro (company release, p. 20). In the first nine months of 2025/26, to 30 June 2026, revenues were 17,769 million euro and operating earnings before taxes were 374 million euro. The group then expected full year operating earnings before taxes of 425 million euro to 525 million euro, towards the upper end of that range (Quarterly Report, first nine months 2025/26, p. 2 and p. 5). The full year 2025/26 report was not yet published on 7 November 2026. Average employees in those nine months were 7,266 (p. 2).

## Segments and end markets
Aurubis reports two segments. In 2024/25, Custom Smelting and Products had total revenues of 17,958 million euro, of which 17,722 million euro were with third parties, and operating earnings before taxes of 446 million euro. Multimetal Recycling had total revenues of 6,196 million euro, of which 449 million euro were with third parties, and operating earnings before taxes of 13 million euro (company release, p. 21). Third party product revenues included wire rod of 6,284 million euro and copper cathodes of 4,143 million euro (p. 22). Cathode output was 582,000 tonnes in Custom Smelting and Products and 514,000 tonnes in Multimetal Recycling. Wire rod output was 869,000 tonnes and shapes output was 175,000 tonnes. Output of flat rolled products and specialty wire fell to 90,000 tonnes from 131,000 tonnes, because the Buffalo plant was sold on 30 August 2024 (p. 9 and p. 10).

## Relevance to the Client
Aurubis is a peer in copper products, not a competitor in nickel superalloy plate, bar or tube. It sells cathodes, continuous cast wire rod, shapes, flat rolled products and specialty wire, together with sulfuric acid and minor metals (company release, p. 8). Flat rolled copper is the closest overlap with strip used in electronics and electrical equipment. That overlap narrowed when the Buffalo strip and sheet plant was sold to Wieland. The retained European flat rolled work includes the Finnish plants. The nine month report names Pori among the sites that produce flat rolled products and specialty wire (p. 8). ## Capacity and investments
The annual report says the group produces more than 1 million tonnes of copper cathodes a year (Annual Report 2024/25, PDF p. 59). The segment accounts give 582,000 tonnes in Custom Smelting and Products and 514,000 tonnes in Multimetal Recycling. Concentrate throughput was 2,180,000 tonnes and sulfuric acid output was 2,024,000 tonnes (company release, p. 9). The Pirdop tankhouse in Bulgaria is due to ramp in 2025/26 and add 120,000 tonnes of copper cathodes a year (Annual Report 2024/25, PDF p. 62). Capital expenditure in Custom Smelting and Products was 431 million euro, covering the Pirdop shutdown and tankhouse, Complex Recycling Hamburg, and the new precious metals refinery in Hamburg (company release, p. 10). The annual report places the Richmond, Georgia, multimetal recycling smelter milestone in late summer 2025 (PDF p. 65).

## Market statements
The company release says a higher metal result, higher sulfuric acid revenues and robust demand for copper products supported the year, while treatment and refining charges and concentrate throughput were weaker (p. 5). Wire rod demand stayed strong, especially from the energy sector, while construction and automotive demand was restrained (p. 9 and p. 10). For the rest of 2025/26 the nine month report expects fabricated product demand to stay high, with strong demand for wire rod, shapes slightly below the prior year, and stable demand for flat rolled products (p. 19 and p. 20). It also says a considerable share of revenues is based on the US dollar.

## Customers and suppliers
Named cathode and rod customers are not disclosed. The Buffalo flat rolled plant was sold to Wieland, with closing on 30 August 2024 (company release, p. 10, and Wieland release of 4 September 2024). Aurubis says all four of its Grade A cathode brands are registered for sale (company release, p. 17). Concentrate suppliers are not named.
