## Segments and end markets
Garrett reports one segment. Sales by product line in 2025 were gasoline USD 1,592 million (45%), diesel 837 million (23%), commercial vehicle 654 million (18%), aftermarket 438 million (12%) and other 63 million (2%) (10-K p. 34). By region Europe took 49%, Asia 29%, United States 19% (10-K p. 34). OEM sales were about 86% of revenue. Gasoline grew 6% on launches, diesel 1% and commercial vehicle 4% on off highway programs, while aftermarket fell 5%.

## Relevance to the Client
Garrett is a direct buyer of high temperature nickel alloys. The 10-K names "a nickel, iron and chromium based alloy" among its most significant raw materials, next to grey iron, aluminum and stainless steel (10-K p. 12). Turbine wheels and housings run at gasoline exhaust temperatures, and the 10-K stresses "high temperature materials" as a strength for gasoline growth (p. 12). Garrett buys mostly purchased components, so the alloy reaches it through casting and forging suppliers. The 10-K does not name the alloy grades: TODO(data).

## Capacity and investments
Garrett owns or leases 13 manufacturing sites (nine owned), five in Europe, Middle East and Africa, five in Asia, two in North America and one in South America (10-K Item 2, p. 30). More than 89% of products are made in low cost countries across seven plants in China, India, Mexico, Brazil, Romania and Slovakia (p. 10). Capex for 2026 is guided at about 2.4% of sales and rises on zero emission technology investment (10-K p. 42, Q3 2026 release). About 50% of research spend of USD 151 million goes to zero emission technology (p. 11).

## Market statements
1. "Total turbocharger production increased globally from approximately 49 million units in 2024 to close to nearly 50 million units in 2025 ... and is expected to decrease in 2026 and onward ... gradually declining to 2022 volume levels by 2030" (10-K p. 7, citing S&P Global Mobility).
2. Gasoline turbo penetration on light vehicles is expected to peak at 54% by 2032 and then plateau (10-K p. 11).
3. Commodity pass through: a 10% commodity price move changes cost of sales by up to USD 39 million a year. Nickel, aluminum and steel alloys are in about 79% of cost of sales through purchased components, with index based escalators at most suppliers (10-K p. 44).
4. Q3 2026: net sales USD 930 million, up 3%, commercial vehicle and industrial up 23%, gasoline down 5% on China. 2026 guidance USD 3.75 to 3.85 billion, light vehicle production down 2% to 4%, battery electric penetration about 19% [CO_GARRETT_GUIDE_2026].
5. "We have not experienced any significant shortage of raw materials" (10-K p. 12).
