# Aperam (competitor, LU)

## Snapshot
FY2025 (calendar year ended 31 December 2025): sales EUR 6,080 million (EUR 6,255m in 2024), adjusted EBITDA EUR 339 million (5.6% margin), operating income EUR 16 million, net result EUR 9 million, shipments 2,287 kt, more than 13,000 employees across four continents. Headquartered in Luxembourg, listed on Euronext Amsterdam and Brussels. Source: Aperam Q4 2025 press release, 6 February 2026; Annual Report 2025 press release, 26 March 2026.

## Segments and end markets
Aperam reports four segments in 2025. Stainless and Electrical Steel contributed EUR 3,823 million in sales and EUR 140 million in adjusted EBITDA (3.7% margin), with 1,668 kt steel shipments; European demand was described as weak and Brazilian volumes were also lower in H2 after a hot-rolling mill upgrade in H2 2024. Services and Solutions generated roughly EUR 1,100 million in external sales (down from EUR 1,210m in H1 2024; H2 2025 not separately published). Alloys and Specialties generated EUR 1,113 million in sales and EUR 114 million in adjusted EBITDA (10.2% margin), with 61 kt shipments at an average selling price of EUR 17,475/t; the segment includes Aperam Alloys Imphy (France) and, from January 2025, Universal Stainless and Alloy Products (United States). Recycling and Renewables contributed adjusted EBITDA of approximately EUR 32 million in 2025 (H2 press release notes the segment). Stainless flat capacity is 2.5 million tonnes in Europe and Brazil; Aperam is the sole integrated stainless producer in South America.

## Relevance to the Client
Aperam Alloys Imphy (Burgundy, France) produces nickel alloys including grades equivalent to Alloy 625, Alloy 718, Alloy 825, Invar 36 and controlled-expansion FeNi alloys for LNG tank applications, aerospace, oil and gas, electronics and medical industries. The Imphy plant has annual output of approximately 36,000 tonnes, produced across three plants using an arc furnace (coulées of 33 t giving three ingots each), plus two induction furnaces producing approximately 8,000 t/yr each; a vacuum induction furnace is also operated. This alloy production overlaps directly with the company's nickel and cobalt alloy portfolio. Universal Stainless (acquired January 2025, enterprise value USD 537 million, more than 750 employees, Bridgeville PA and additional U.S. sites) produces semi-finished and finished specialty steels, nickel alloys, tool steel and other alloyed steels for aerospace, energy and heavy equipment, extending Aperam's reach into North American alloy markets. Aperam announced EUR 160 million of investments including a new VIM furnace complex at Imphy, further expanding high-performance alloy capacity. As a stainless producer, Aperam also offers grades competing with the company in chemical, energy and oil and gas applications.

## Capacity and investments
Stainless melt and hot-rolling sites are located in Genk and Chatelet (Belgium) and Gueugnon (France) for Europe, plus three plants in Brazil (the main hot-rolling mill upgraded in H2 2024). Alloys and Specialties production: Imphy (France) approximately 36,000 t/yr nickel alloys across three plants (ARC furnace, induction, VIM); in H1 2025, Aperam doubled Imphy production capacity to meet growing demand from LNG tanker shipyards. Universal Stainless operates facilities in Pennsylvania and Ohio (USA). The EUR 160 million investment package (announced with FY2025 results) covers Genk, Chatelet, Gueugnon and a new VIM furnace complex at Imphy; individual site allocations were not disclosed. The Transformation Program (Phase 5 of Leadership Journey) realised EUR 100 million gains in 2025, reaching EUR 195 million over two years toward a EUR 200 million target over three years.

## Market statements
In the H1 2025 half-year report, Aperam stated that "some final use segments still enjoy a healthy demand, such as LNG carrier shipbuilding, aerospace, medical and electronics" while "oil and gas, petrochemical and process industries, automotive and electrical components suffer from depressed final demand or lower investments." The Q4 2025 commentary noted "persistent weakness of the aerospace supply chain for cold parts in the United States" and that weak oil and gas demand reduced Alloys and Specialties adjusted EBITDA in Q4 2025. Global stainless overcapacity was described as approximately 12 million tonnes globally and approximately 3 million tonnes in China, with little prospect of near-term reduction. CEO Timoteo Di Maulo stated at Universal acquisition closing: "This acquisition is a key step in our strategic plan to decommoditize our product portfolio and expand our Alloys and Specialties solutions, providing innovative, high-performance applications for critical industries such as aerospace."

## Customers and suppliers
Aperam describes customers in more than 40 countries but does not name individual accounts in its financial reports. Aperam Recycling is described as a global leader in collecting, trading, processing and recycling stainless steel scrap and high-performance alloys scrap, providing internal raw material supply. Biomass (charcoal from FSC-certified forestry) in Brazil replaces coal in certain furnace applications. Named industry end users of Alloys and Specialties include LNG tanker shipyards (not named) and the aerospace sector.
