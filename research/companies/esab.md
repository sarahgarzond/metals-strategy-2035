# ESAB Corporation (competitor in welding consumables and fabrication technology, United States)
## Snapshot
Fiscal year 2025 (31 December). Revenue USD 2,842.6 million (2024: USD 2,740.8 million), operating income USD 412.2 million, a margin of 14.5 percent (2024: USD 447.5 million). About 10,300 employees, of whom 9,200 outside the United States. Principal plants in 16 countries besides the United States. Sources [1] Item 1 and MD&A, XBRL [3].
## Segments and end markets
Two segments: Americas 40 percent and EMEA & APAC 60 percent of 2025 sales, and about 80 percent of sales arise outside the United States [1] Item 1A. Consumables were 66 percent of sales (USD 1,870.6 million) and equipment 34 percent (USD 972.0 million) [1] revenue note. The 10-K says the customer base is "highly diversified in the industrial end markets" but gives no end market percentages: TODO(data). ESAB sizes its addressable market at about USD 45 billion by end of 2028 [1] Item 1. 2025 sales from existing businesses fell USD 31.4 million on lower volume in Russia and tariff effects in the Americas, offset by price of USD 29.4 million and USD 115.9 million from acquisitions [1] MD&A.
## Relevance to the Client
ESAB is a global competitor in stainless and nickel alloy filler metals and a large buyer of drawn wire and strip. Largest material purchases are "steel, iron, copper and aluminum" and nickel is not named [1] MD&A. It also sells gas control equipment and medical regulators. ## Market statements
"In 2025, the United States expanded and increased existing tariffs on steel and aluminum, imposing 50% tariffs on steel, aluminum and products containing steel and aluminum" [1] Item 1A. Gross margin fell 100 basis points in 2025 on inflation and tariff costs [1] MD&A. Management says it has "generally been successful in passing raw material cost increases" to customers [1] MD&A.
