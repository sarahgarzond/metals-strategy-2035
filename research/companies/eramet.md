# Eramet (nickel ore, ferronickel, manganese and lithium producer, France)

## Snapshot
- Adjusted turnover excluding SLN 2025 about EUR 3,155m, uncertain because the same page shows EUR 3,377m for 2024 [CO_ERAMET_TURNOVER_2025] (1). Adjusted EBITDA fell about 54 percent and net debt was EUR 1,935m (1).
- Employees 10,337 at end 2025, of which 1,653 at the Weda Bay JV [CO_ERAMET_EMPLOYEES_2025] (2). Operating margin: TODO(data).
- Nickel is about 33 percent of adjusted turnover (2). Q1 2026 adjusted turnover EUR 840m, up 13 percent (3).

## Segments and end markets
Mining and metals divisions cover manganese (Gabon), nickel (Weda Bay in Indonesia, SLN in New Caledonia), lithium (Argentina) and mineral sands (Senegal). The 2026 targets are manganese transport of 6.4 to 6.8 Mt, lithium carbonate of 17 to 20 kt LCE and capex of EUR 250m to 290m (4). Mineral sands guidance was suspended in Q1 but a Q3 summary lists 300 to 400 kt HMC, so the status needs checking.

## Relevance to the Client
Eramet sells nickel ore, mainly to Chinese owned smelters at the IWIP park, and ferronickel through SLN. Ferronickel is a possible melt feed for stainless and some alloy grades. Manganese ore feeds alloying elements. Eramet is not an alloy competitor in the sources read. Eramet appears here as a raw material price signal.

## Capacity and investments
- Weda Bay (PT WBN, Halmahera) produced about 42 Mwmt of ore in 2025 and planned the same for 2026. The text says kth, which is likely a unit slip, so verify [CO_ERAMET_WBN_ORE_2025] (2).
- Initial 2026 RKAB approval was 12 Mwmt, about 70 percent below the 2025 level, with external sales capped at 9 Mwmt [CO_ERAMET_WBN_RKAB_2026] (5).
- Ore operations stopped in early June 2026 after the quota ran out at the end of May (6). The NPI plant of Tsingshan continued from stockpiles (7).
- A revision was filed for July to September. The Q3 call summary shows revised external ore sales guidance of 23 to 26 Mwmt against the 2025 target of 36 to 39 Mwmt [CO_ERAMET_ORE_SALES_GUIDANCE_2026] (8). The primary Q3 release was not read.
- Adjusted EBITDA guidance 2026 is EUR 540m to 610m (8).

## Market statements
- Eramet said IWIP smelters face a severe ore deficit without a revision of the Weda Bay RKAB (9).
- Dividends received from Weda Bay fell to EUR 34m in 2025 as JV EBITDA dropped sharply (1).
- Lithium carbonate price assumption about USD 13,000 per tonne (8). No nickel price quote captured: TODO(data).
