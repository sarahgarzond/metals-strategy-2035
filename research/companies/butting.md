## Relevance to the Client
BUTTING is the most direct clad pipe user in this set. It calls itself "the global market leader for clad pipes" with high internal corrosion resistance and external pressure strength, and claims "cost savings of up to 40 %" against solid wall pipe. Product forms are mechanically clad BuBi, bonded GluBi, metallurgically clad pipe, clad spools and the newer SmartClad line. Nickel alloy liner or cladding (typically Alloy 625 and 825) is bought as strip and plate, which is the product form a nickel alloy producer can sell. The website extracts do not name the alloy grades: TODO(data). Industry sources identify Alloy 625 and 825 as common overlay grades for sour (H2S) service, a secondary market summary only.
