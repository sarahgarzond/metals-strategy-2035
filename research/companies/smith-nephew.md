# Smith & Nephew (customer, United Kingdom)

Access note: tools/read_url.py returned HTTP 403 for sec.gov, so filings were read with curl and a research user agent. the 20-F (Annual Report 2025) was read from SEC EDGAR and the H1 2026 results from the company site. Page numbers are those printed in the annual report where they could be located, otherwise the section is named.

## Snapshot
* 2025 revenue was USD 6,164 million, trading profit USD 1,211 million (margin 19.7%) and operating profit USD 794 million (margin 12.9%) (pp. 1 to 2) [CO_SNN_REV_2025, CO_SNN_TP_2025].
* The average number of employees was 17,530, down from 18,060 in 2024 (financial statements, note 3) [CO_SNN_EMP_2025].

## Segments and end markets
* Orthopaedics earned USD 2,437 million (40% of revenue) and grew 5.7%, or 5.1% underlying. Sports Medicine and ENT earned USD 1,793 million (29%) and Advanced Wound Management USD 1,934 million (31%) (p. 2) [CO_SNN_ORTHO_2025].
* US Knee Implants are less than 9% of Group revenue, and the new LANDMARK knee system is due in the second half of 2026 [CO_SNN_USKNEE_2025].
* H1 2026 revenue was USD 3,097 million (underlying up 2.3%) with a trading margin of 18.3%. Full year underlying growth guidance was cut from about 6% to about 4% [CO_SNN_H1_2026].

## Relevance to the Client
* Smith & Nephew is the only profiled customer that builds a metal substitution argument into its portfolio. OXINIUM is an oxidized zirconium alloy with "virtually no nickel, cobalt and chromium", so implants carry no CMR labelling (Orthopaedics section, about pp. 36 to 41) [CO_SNN_OXINIUM_2025].
* This is a competing material to cobalt chrome femoral heads and knee femurs. The 20-F reports 94.1% survivorship at 20 years for OXINIUM on highly cross-linked polyethylene in total hip arthroplasty, citing the Australian registry [CO_SNN_OXINIUM_2025].
* Any nickel or cobalt alloy demand from the company is therefore limited to non-OXINIUM implants, trauma plating and instruments. Tonnage: TODO(data).

## Capacity and investments
* The company is building a new Advanced Wound Management factory at Melton near Hull, UK, starting operations in 2027, which is not a metals investment (p. 33 area).
* The Memphis orthopaedic site was "significantly improved" and capacity "right-sized" during the turnaround (strategy section). Penang (Malaysia) and Suzhou (China) have photovoltaic installations.
* The Integrity Orthopaedics acquisition costs USD 225 million plus up to USD 225 million over five years, with trading profit dilution of USD 20 to 25 million in 2026.

## Market statements
* China VBP "has included Orthopaedics and Sports Medicine, and a VBP process is expected in ENT in 2026" (market trends, p. 12) [CO_SNN_VBP_2025].
* The chief executive says margins rose "whilst also overcoming some 1,000bps of macro challenges from Volume Based Procurement in China and higher inflation" (p. 8) [CO_SNN_VBP_2025].
* Risks include "limited ability to pass on increased costs such as raw materials, freight" to customers (principal risks section).
* The ENT VBP is delayed to the second half of 2026 with a profit headwind of about USD 15 to 20 million per the H1 call coverage [CO_SNN_H1_2026]. The figure is from a search summary, so check it against the release.
