## Segments and end markets
Segments are mineral sands (zircon, rutile, synthetic rutile) and the rare earths business around Eneabba. Zircon goes mainly to ceramics, foundry and refractories. Sector split by end market: TODO(data).

## Relevance to the Client
Zircon is a minor input for a nickel alloy producer. It matters for zirconium sponge, zirconium alloys for chemical processing and nuclear, and for zircon flour in investment casting shell moulds used for superalloy castings. Relevance is as a supplier of a niche raw material and as a signal for the zirconium chain. Iluka does not make nickel alloys.

## Capacity and investments
- 2026 zircon production guidance is about 180 kt, in line with 2025, with a more even split between zircon sand and zircon in concentrate [CO_ILUKA_ZIRCON_GUIDANCE_2026] (2).
- Q2 2026 production of zircon, rutile and synthetic rutile was 58 kt against sales of 157 kt, as the Balranald ramp up was slower than expected [CO_ILUKA_ZIR_Q2_2026] (2). Sales above output show inventory being drawn down.
- Both synthetic rutile kilns and the Cataby mine remain idle and restart depends on market conditions (2).
- Eneabba rare earths refinery (Western Australia) has a budget of AUD 1.7bn to 1.8bn. Construction was about 60 percent complete at Q2 and 65 percent at Q3, with AUD 1,237m spent [CO_ILUKA_ENEABBA_2026] (3). Commissioning has moved to 2027. A government loan of AUD 1.65bn was announced in June 2026 (4). Refinery tonnage: TODO(data).

## Market statements
- Zircon sand price averaged USD 1,514 per tonne in H1 2026, and the Q3 expectation is USD 1,685 per tonne FOB including volumes contracted earlier [CO_ILUKA_ZIRCON_PRICE_2026] (2). The Q4 2025 price was USD 1,502 per tonne, lower on soft premium grade demand in China (5).
- Management cited demand uncertainty, particularly in the United States (5). Existing agreements were adjusted to underpin minimum contracted revenue of USD 240 million in 2026 (5).
- Iluka kept SR2 and Cataby idle through 2026, which is supply discipline (5).
