# Applied Materials (customer, United States)

Access note: the 10-K for fiscal 2025 (year ended 26 October 2025) was read as text from SEC EDGAR without page numbers, so sections are named. Q3 fiscal 2026 figures come from a search summary because the company site could not be opened. The Q4 fiscal 2026 report is not yet published at the date of this note.

## Snapshot
* Fiscal 2025 net revenue was USD 28,368 million, operating income USD 8,289 million and operating margin 29.2% (MD&A) [CO_AMAT_REV_2025, CO_AMAT_OPINC_2025].
* The company employed about 36,500 regular full-time employees in 25 countries (Item 1) [CO_AMAT_EMP_2025].

## Segments and end markets
* Semiconductor Systems earned USD 20,798 million (73%), Applied Global Services USD 6,385 million (23%) and Corporate and Other USD 1,185 million [CO_AMAT_REV_2025].
* Semiconductor Systems revenue split into foundry, logic and other 67%, DRAM 26% and NAND 7% [CO_AMAT_MARKET_2025].
* China took 30% of revenue (USD 8,529 million, down 16%), Taiwan 24% and Korea 20% [CO_AMAT_CHINA_2025].
* Backlog was USD 15,002 million, split almost evenly between systems and services [CO_AMAT_BACKLOG_2025]. Two customers accounted for 19% and 15% of revenue [CO_AMAT_CUST_2025].
* Q3 fiscal 2026 revenue was reported at USD 9.12 billion, up 25%, with Q4 guidance of USD 10.25 billion plus or minus USD 0.5 billion [CO_AMAT_Q3_2026].

## Relevance to the Client
* Applied is a tier one buyer of chamber parts and gas delivery content for deposition, etch and implant tools. Candidate alloys are nickel alloys such as C-276 and C-22 for corrosive halogen service and 316L for gas lines, but the 10-K names no alloy.
* Direct purchases from alloy producers are unlikely. Tool makers buy machined and welded parts from contract manufacturers in Singapore, Japan, China, Korea and Taiwan. Alloy volumes: TODO(data).

## Capacity and investments
* Capital expenditure was USD 2.3 billion in fiscal 2025 against USD 1.2 billion a year earlier, for real property, demonstration and manufacturing equipment (Liquidity) [CO_AMAT_CAPEX_2025].
* Manufacturing takes place in the United States, Singapore, Japan, China, Korea, Taiwan, Israel and Europe [CO_AMAT_SUPPLY_2025]. Specific expansions with tonnes: TODO(data).

## Market statements
* "Some key parts may be obtained from only a qualified single supplier or a limited group of qualified suppliers" (Item 1, Manufacturing, Raw Materials and Supplies) [CO_AMAT_SUPPLY_2025].
* Customer capex allocation to segments Applied does not serve, such as lithography, is named as a risk factor (Item 1A).
* The CEO reportedly said Semiconductor Systems revenue for calendar 2026 will grow faster than the market, led by DRAM, leading edge foundry logic and advanced packaging (Q3 FY2026 call, secondary).
