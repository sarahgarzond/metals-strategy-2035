# thyssenkrupp nucera (customer, Germany)
## Snapshot
• Sales in fiscal 2024/25 (to 30 September 2025) were EUR 845m, down 2% from EUR 862m, with EBIT of EUR 2m, an operating margin of about 0.2% (team calculation), as shown in the table on p.2 of the annual report [2].
• Order intake fell to EUR 348m from EUR 636m and the backlog dropped to EUR 606m at 30 September 2025 from EUR 1.1bn (annual report p.42) [2].
• The company reports 1,092 employees at the reporting date against 1,012 a year earlier (annual report p.2 and p.52) [2].

## Segments and end markets
• Green Hydrogen sales were EUR 459m against EUR 524m, and green hydrogen orders collapsed to EUR 26m from EUR 356m [1].
• Chlor alkali orders rose 15% to EUR 322m, so the chemical segment now carries the group [1]. In the third quarter of 2025/26 chlor alkali sales rose 34% to EUR 109m while green hydrogen sales were EUR 36m [3].
• Nine month order intake in 2025/26 nearly doubled to EUR 471m, yet third quarter green hydrogen orders were only EUR 3m [3].
• Guidance for 2025/26 was cut in August 2026 to sales of EUR 450m to 500m and EBIT of minus EUR 105m to minus EUR 75m [3].

## Relevance to the Client
• Alkaline water electrolysis uses nickel anodes and nickel Raney cathodes on woven or expanded nickel mesh, so nucera is a buyer of high purity nickel strip, wire and mesh rather than of superalloys.
• A market commentary gives a rule of thumb of 150 to 450 kg nickel per MW, another source gives 300 to 500 kg per MW [4]. • SOEC would have needed ferritic interconnect steel and nickel cermet cells, but nucera dropped its own stack mass production in August 2026 [5].

## Capacity and investments
• The annual report states more than 1.5 GW annual production capacity, more than 10 GW installed and more than 3 GW of alkaline projects under binding contract (p.3) [2].
• In August 2026 nucera abandoned its plan to build SOEC stack mass production, citing no attractive business model, with a one off EBIT effect of about EUR 30m from impairing the pilot plant [5].
• The 8 MW SOEC pilot at Arnstadt with Fraunhofer IKTS continues and the planned 300 MW factory is not being built (German press, secondary) [5].
• Net financial assets were EUR 627m at 30 June 2026, which funds the restructuring [3].

## Market statements
• The annual report says inventories rose by EUR 32m to EUR 179m because of strategic stockpiling of raw materials for the backlog (p.44), and that raw material price risk is passed on through customer contract structure (p.61) [2].
• Management expects additional green hydrogen orders not to affect sales materially until later years, and the backlog drives 2025/26 sales [1].
• The company describes the SOEC investment as not economically attractive under current conditions [5].
• Spotlight: the report names a preferred supplier position for a 1.4 GW electrolyzer (p.5) and states that SOEC was not yet offered as a product (p.28) [2]. The customer name and contract status for the 1.4 GW project are TODO(data) in the sources reached.
