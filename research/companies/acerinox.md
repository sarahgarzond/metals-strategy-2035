## Relevance to the Client
Closest German peer: VDM Metals (Werdohl, Altena, Unna) makes nickel alloys in strip, bar, wire, powder and welding wire. Haynes adds US aerospace and chemical alloys. VDM and Haynes are one competitor. VDM's portfolio is an application benchmark only. The stainless division (NAS, Acerinox Europa) shares nickel as a raw material.
