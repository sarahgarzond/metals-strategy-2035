# Linde (customer, United Kingdom domicile, operations global)
## Snapshot
• Sales in 2025 were USD 33,986m against USD 33,005m in 2024, with operating profit of USD 8,923m reported and USD 10,137m adjusted (annual report p.2) [1][2].
• The reported operating margin is about 26.3% (team calculation from 8,923 and 33,986) [1][2].
• Employees numbered 65,177 at 31 December 2025 according to the annual report (p.12) [1]. The SEC copy of the 10-K was not reachable, so the annual report PDF is used.
• Total project backlog was USD 10.0bn at the end of 2025 [2].

## Segments and end markets
• Reported segments are Americas, EMEA, APAC, Linde Engineering and Other, as listed in Linde filings. Segment revenue values are TODO(data) because the 10-K tables were not read.
• End markets are chemicals and energy, food and beverage, electronics, healthcare, manufacturing, metals and mining [1].
• Volume growth of 2% in the fourth quarter of 2025 came mainly from electronics and from chemicals and energy start ups [2].
• Third quarter 2026 sales were USD 9,404m, up 9%, with underlying growth of 5% [4].

## Relevance to the Client
• Linde Engineering designs and builds air separation, hydrogen, syngas and LNG plants. These use cryogenic nickel steel, stainless steel and nickel alloy in heat exchangers, reformers and high temperature hydrogen service.
• Linde acts as a buyer through engineering and procurement, and its hydrogen business is a downstream demand signal for nickel alloys. Alloy tonnage per plant is not published by Linde, so the value is TODO(data).
• Electrolysis is PEM based through the joint venture with ITM Power, including a 24 MW plant at Leuna, so the nickel content of that route is low [5].

## Capacity and investments
• Capital expenditure in 2025 was USD 5,261m according to the financial highlights (p.2) [1]. Capital expenditure for 2026 is guided at USD 5.5bn to 6.0bn, which includes the sale of gas project backlog [4].
• The contractual sale of gas backlog was USD 7.9bn at the end of the third quarter of 2026, and the third party equipment backlog was USD 2.9bn [4].

## Market statements
• Linde Engineering third quarter 2026 sales were USD 582m, up 12%, with a margin of 18.4% and order intake of USD 499m [4].
• Fourth quarter 2025 engineering order intake was USD 434m, below sales of USD 615m, so equipment backlog was drawn down [2].
• The shareholder letter (p.3) mentions oxygen and nitrogen supply to the Blue Point low-carbon ammonia project, and the 10-K describes blue and green hydrogen (p.10 and p.11) [1].
• Management highlights electronics and clean energy as secular growth trends in the 2025 shareholder letter [1]. No statement on nickel prices or nickel shortages was found in the sources reached.
• Spotlight: the Herøya 24 MW electrolyser for Yara was sold by ITM Power to Linde Engineering [5].
