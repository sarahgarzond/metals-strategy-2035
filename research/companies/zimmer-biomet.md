# Zimmer Biomet (customer, United States)

Access note: tools/read_url.py returned HTTP 403 for sec.gov, so filings were read with curl and a research user agent. the 10-K and the Q3 2026 release were read from SEC EDGAR. Page numbers come from the 10-K table of contents. The 10-K names cobalt chrome and titanium but gives no tonnages.

## Snapshot
* Fiscal 2025 net sales were USD 8,231.5 million (up 7.2%) and operating profit USD 1,098.1 million, a margin of 13.3% [CO_ZBH_SALES_2025, CO_ZBH_OPPROFIT_2025].
* The company employed about 17,000 people, of whom about 7,000 work in the United States and about 2,000 in research and development (Item 1, p. 4ff) [CO_ZBH_EMP_2025].
* Operating margin fell from 16.7% in 2024, reflecting restructuring, the Paragon 28 acquisition of 21 April 2025 and higher manufacturing costs.

## Segments and end markets
* Knees USD 3,322.3 million (up 4.7%), Hips USD 2,093.5 million (up 4.7%), S.E.T. USD 2,150.2 million (up 15.2%), Technology and Data, Bone Cement and Surgical USD 665.6 million [CO_ZBH_PRODUCTS_2025].
* The United States contributed USD 4,764.0 million and international markets USD 3,467.5 million [CO_ZBH_SALES_2025].
* In Q3 2026 Knees fell 3.1% to USD 767.5 million while Hips rose 6.0% to USD 536.5 million, and management raised 2026 organic constant currency growth guidance to 3.0% to 3.5% [CO_ZBH_Q3_2026].

## Relevance to the Client
* Zimmer Biomet is the closest customer to the Client's metals, since the 10-K states "We purchase raw material commodities such as cobalt chrome, titanium, tantalum, polymer and sterile packaging" (Item 7A, p. 40) [CO_ZBH_COMMODITY_2025].
* Knee femoral components in cobalt chrome and hip stems in titanium account for Knees and Hips sales of USD 5.4 billion. Nickel base alloys are not named, so overlap is limited to cobalt chrome and possibly stainless.
* Oxidized zirconium is a Smith & Nephew proprietary product. Zimmer Biomet has its own tantalum based porous metal. Tonnes bought: TODO(data).

## Capacity and investments
* About 25 manufacturing locations, with Warsaw, Indiana as the most significant site for Knees, Hips and S.E.T. Largest sites outside the United States are in Switzerland, Ireland and China (Item 2, p. 27) [CO_ZBH_PLANTS_2025].
* A 2025 restructuring plan was expanded in December 2025. Furnace or forging capacity: TODO(data), because the company buys metal rather than melting it.

## Market statements
* "China has implemented volume-based procurement (VBP) processes designed to reduce medical spending", which have caused reduced margins, distributor renegotiation and inventory charges (Item 1A, pp. 13 to 26) [CO_ZBH_VBP_2025].
* The company lists "dependence on a limited number of suppliers for key raw materials" as a risk factor (forward looking statements, p. 3).
* Supply contracts for commodities run generally 12 to 24 months "where available", which limits metal price pass through to short windows (Item 7A, p. 40) [CO_ZBH_COMMODITY_2025].
