## Capacity and investments
* Two electric arc furnaces at Baosteel Special Steel in Shanghai were retired in June 2023. A replacement project moves capacity to Baowu Special Metallurgical in Ma'anshan, with a 40 tonne EAF giving 140,000 tonnes per year and a 60 tonne EAF giving 220,000 tonnes per year of steel. The article date is unclear [CO_BAOWU_SPECIAL_CAPACITY].
* Group steel capacity target adjusted in November 2025 to over 80 million tonnes for 2026 to 2030, with value from existing resources rather than new volume [CO_BAOWU_GROUP_TARGET_2025].
