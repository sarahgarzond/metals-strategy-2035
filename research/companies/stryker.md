# Stryker (customer, United States)

Access note: tools/read_url.py returned HTTP 403 for sec.gov, so filings were read with curl and a research user agent. the 10-K was read in full text from SEC EDGAR and the Q3 2026 release from the 8-K exhibit. The filing text carries no page numbers, so Item references and the 10-K table of contents pages are given. The 10-K names no metal.

## Snapshot
* Fiscal year 2025 net sales were USD 25,116 million and operating income USD 4,889 million, which gives a computed operating margin of 19.5% (team arithmetic) [CO_STRYKER_SALES_2025, CO_STRYKER_OPINC_2025].
* Stryker had about 56,000 employees at year end 2025, of whom about 28,000 worked in the United States [CO_STRYKER_EMP_2025].
* The company operates 55 manufacturing locations among 27 owned and 306 leased sites (Item 2, p. 12) [CO_STRYKER_PLANTS_2025].

## Segments and end markets
* Orthopaedics generated USD 9,469 million (38% of sales, up 4.3%) and MedSurg and Neurotechnology USD 15,647 million (62%, up 15.7%) [CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025].
* Within Orthopaedics, Knees were USD 2,656 million, Hips USD 1,865 million and Trauma and Extremities USD 3,948 million, the largest line at 42% [CO_STRYKER_ORTHO_2025].
* Orthopaedic constant currency growth excluding acquisitions was 9.6% in 2025, driven by unit volume and held back by 0.1% lower prices [CO_STRYKER_ORTHOPRICE_2025].
* In Q3 2026 Orthopaedics grew 5.9% organically, with Knees at USD 635 million (up 1.1%) and Hips at USD 453 million (down 1.0%) [CO_STRYKER_Q3_2026].

## Relevance to the Client
* Stryker is an end customer for implant grade cobalt chrome, titanium and stainless bar and forging stock, mostly bought through forgers and bar processors. The Client is at best a second tier supplier.
* Nickel overlap is limited to cobalt chrome grades with small nickel content and to stainless instruments. Metal tonnage per year: TODO(data), because the 10-K names no alloy.
* Mako robotics and instruments pull stainless and precipitation hardening steels, but no figures are published.

## Market statements
* "China has implemented a volume-based procurement process designed to decrease prices for medical devices" (Item 1A, pp. 5 to 12) [CO_STRYKER_VBP_2025].
* "Raw materials essential to our business are generally readily available from multiple sources, however certain of our raw materials are currently sourced from single suppliers" (Item 1) [CO_STRYKER_RAWMAT_2025].
* The 2026 sales guidance "includes a modestly positive pricing impact", although the company cut its organic growth guidance to 6.5% to 7.0% and attributed the miss partly to softer hip and knee volumes (Q3 2026 release, p. 1) [CO_STRYKER_Q3_2026].
* Trade press attributes the softness to higher insurance deductibles and pre-authorization denials. This is a secondary source and not a company statement.
