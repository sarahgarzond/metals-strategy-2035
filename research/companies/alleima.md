# Alleima (competitor, SE)

## Snapshot
FY2025 (calendar year ended 31 December 2025): revenues SEK 18,630 million (SEK 19,691m in 2024), adjusted EBIT SEK 1,555 million (8.3% margin), reported EBIT SEK 938 million (5.0% margin), profit for the year SEK 671 million, 6,380 employees at year-end (plus approximately 440 third-party workers). Currency headwinds cost SEK 341 million of adjusted EBIT; on a constant-currency basis the adjusted EBIT margin would have been 9.8%. Listed on Nasdaq Stockholm, demerged from Sandvik in 2022. Source: Annual Report 2025 p. 33, 35 and Q4 and full-year 2025 report.

## Segments and end markets
Alleima operates three divisions. Tube produced SEK 13,063 million in revenues (70% of total) and SEK 1,159 million in adjusted EBIT (8.9% margin), making seamless tubes and other long products in advanced stainless and special alloys for oil and gas, industrial, chemical and petrochemical, mining, nuclear, transportation and hydrogen and renewable energy applications. Kanthal produced SEK 3,996 million (21%) and SEK 656 million in adjusted EBIT (16.4% margin), making heating technology, heating resistance materials, and ultra-fine wire for medical and industrial use. Strip produced SEK 1,571 million (8%) and SEK 61 million in adjusted EBIT (3.9% margin), making precision strip for consumer, transportation and medical applications. By customer segment in FY2025: oil and gas 24%, industrial 17%, chemical and petrochemical 16%, industrial heating 10%, consumer 8%, medical 7%, transportation 6%, mining and construction 6%, nuclear 5%, hydrogen and renewable energy 1%. Europe represents approximately 49% of Tube revenues, North America 22%, Asia 21% and other 8%.

## Relevance to the Client
Alleima competes with the company in seamless tubes and other long products made from special alloys. Its Sanicro 625 (UNS N06625, supplied in 6 to 42 mm OD) and Sanicro 925 (a niobium-modified alloy for oil and gas) compete directly with nickel-alloy tube offerings. SAF 2507 (UNS S32750 super-duplex) is the established industry standard for offshore umbilical tubing and competes with duplex products. Alleima's new SAF 3007 (UNS S83072 hyper-duplex) is designed to replace SAF 2507 for lighter, stronger subsea applications. For oil country tubular goods (OCTG), Alleima supplies corrosion-resistant alloy tubes; a large OCTG order of approximately SEK 250 million was cancelled in Q3 2026, reflecting market volatility. For the nuclear market, Alleima produces steam generator tubes and cladding tubes; it is reopening a dedicated mill (Mill 68) in Sandviken to increase nuclear tube capacity by approximately 60% by end 2026, targeting power utilities globally. The medical wire business (ultra-fine wire for glucose monitors, pacemakers and neurostimulation) made from precious metal alloys and stainless competes with the company's medical wire offerings.

## Capacity and investments
Main production facilities: Sandviken (Sweden) is the core site with fully integrated melt-to-tube production including three existing VAR furnaces and a new fourth VAR furnace (installed summer 2025, operational autumn 2025) adding capacity for high-nickel and stainless remelted bars and tubes for aerospace, medical and oil and gas; the Mill 68 nuclear tube plant is being reopened here. Chomutov (Czech Republic) is a dedicated mill for high-alloy hydraulic and instrumentation tubing and offshore umbilical tubing in coiled and straight form, using its own melting, bar production and extrusion. Scranton, Pennsylvania (USA) for North American market. Mehsana, Gujarat (India) investment of SEK 220 million approved in 2026 for chemical and petrochemical tube capacity. Zhenjiang, China plant completed in 2025 for Chemical and Petrochemical segment. Malaysia medical wire facility under construction. Capex FY2025 was SEK 1,089 million (5.8% of revenues), corresponding to 108% of scheduled depreciation. Free operating cash flow was SEK 1,100 million in 2025.

## Market statements
In the FY2025 report, management stated that "demand in Medical remained high and increased; demand in Oil and Gas and Nuclear remained stable at a high level; demand in the short-cycle business in Industrial and Chemical and Petrochemical segments declined." For 2026, the Q3 2026 report noted a cancelled OCTG order of approximately SEK 250 million as a negative order intake effect, while Mehsana investment was confirmed to serve growth in Indian chemical demand. Alleima described an "0% organic growth" in FY2025 despite currency headwinds and noted that "without currency effects the adjusted EBIT margin amounted to 9.8%, underscoring the robustness of the business model." Management stated at the Capital Markets Day (November 2025) that the strategy focuses on expanding toward more highly alloyed nickel grades in both bar and tubular products.

## Customers and suppliers
Specific customer names are not disclosed in Alleima's public reports. Alleima states it has a broad customer base across oil and gas operators and EPC contractors, chemical companies, nuclear utilities and medical device manufacturers globally. It describes itself as a fully integrated producer from steel melting to finished product, using approximately 80% recycled steel in Sandviken as feedstock. Nickel, chromium, molybdenum and other alloying elements are sourced externally; supplier names not disclosed.
