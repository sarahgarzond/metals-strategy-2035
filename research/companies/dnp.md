# Dai Nippon Printing (customer, Japan)

## Snapshot
* Fiscal year ended 31 March 2026 (called fiscal 2025 by DNP) net sales were JPY 1,512.5bn, up 3.8%, and operating income was JPY 101.0bn, up 7.9%, a margin of 6.7% derived from both [CO_DNP_REVENUE_FY2025]. (Consolidated financial results, 13 May 2026, p. 5).
* Employees are TODO(data) because the results release and presentation do not state them.
* Forecast for the year to March 2027 is net sales of JPY 1,530.0bn and operating income of JPY 108.0bn.

## Segments and end markets
DNP has three segments, and Electronics had sales of JPY 251.8bn (up 1.6%) and operating income of JPY 50.7bn (down 11.6%), after JPY 247.7bn and JPY 57.3bn the year before [CO_DNP_ELECTRONICS_FY2025]. The other two segments are Smart Communication and Life & Healthcare, which are not relevant to metals. Within Electronics the Digital Interface business covers OLED metal masks and optical films, and the semiconductor business covers photomasks. The Digital Interface business exceeded the previous year, although demand fell in the fourth quarter because memory shortages cut mid and low end smartphone output. Sales of metal masks alone are TODO(data) because DNP reports only the segment total. Electronics is forecast to reach JPY 274.0bn (up 8.8%) with operating income of JPY 54.0bn in the year to March 2027.

## Relevance to the Client
DNP makes fine metal masks for OLED evaporation from FeNi36 (Invar type) foil, so it is a direct customer candidate for thin FeNi36 strip or foil and a buyer of the same alloy family the Client melts. The company states that it holds the world's top share of OLED metal masks (results release, p. 7). The foil supplier of DNP is not named in DNP documents, and a Chinese report naming Proterial as exclusive supplier could not be verified, so the supply chain is TODO(data). The team profile for Proterial holds the same open question on foil supply. Foil below about 25 micrometres is a separate rolling capability and not simply an extension of strip production.

## Capacity and investments
* The new large metal mask line at the Kurosaki Plant in Fukuoka started in May 2024 for 8th generation glass, and DNP reports progress toward more than doubling its metal mask capacity [CO_DNP_KUROSAKI_2024]. (results release, pp. 5 and 7, presentation p. 16). The investment is reported as USD 140m by OLED-Info, which is secondary.
* The medium term plan names investment in facilities for Gen 8 metal masks within about JPY 100bn of infrastructure investment (presentation, p. 33). Foil tonnes per year are not published, so they stay TODO(data).
* Other Electronics investments are a 2,500 mm wide coating line at Mihara from September 2025 for optical films, EUV photomask and nanoimprint capacity, and a TGV glass core substrate pilot line at Kuki from December 2025.

## Market statements
* DNP wrote that large metal masks for Gen 8 glass outweighed the fourth quarter decline in smartphone demand (results release, p. 5).
* The company plans to expand digital interfaces by making 8th generation large format metal masks (results release, outlook section).
* A trade report says DNP signed exclusive supply of 8.6G masks to BOE, and that it ended its exclusivity with Samsung Display. The report is undated and needs confirmation [CO_DNP_FMM_SUPPLY_2025].

## Customers and suppliers
Named customers in trade coverage are BOE, CSOT and Tianma for earlier generations and Samsung Display for Korean lines. Competitors are LG Innotek, which supplies LG Display, and Korean and Chinese mask makers. Foil suppliers and any long term agreements are TODO(data) because none are published.
