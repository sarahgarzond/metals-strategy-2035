## Snapshot

## Segments and end markets
* Revenue by market was foundry 54% (2025: 45%), memory 39% (42%) and logic and integrated device makers 7% (13%) (MD&A) [CO_LAM_MARKET_2026].
* China was 34% of revenue, Taiwan 22%, Korea 19%, Japan 9% and the United States 7% [CO_LAM_CHINA_2026].
* Four customers accounted for 16%, 15%, 12% and 12% of revenue (Note 19) [CO_LAM_CUST_2026].
* The September 2026 quarter produced revenue of USD 8.22 billion, according to press summaries. Lam reportedly sees 2026 wafer fab equipment spending at USD 150 billion or more and 2027 above USD 200 billion [CO_LAM_WFE_2026].
