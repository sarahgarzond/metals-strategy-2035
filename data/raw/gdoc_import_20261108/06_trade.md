# 6. 6. Global Market Size and Trade

Trade data help estimate the market for qualified metal products and show where conversion takes place. This chapter examines market size, major trade routes, and policies that affect those routes.

### 6.1 6.1 Measuring the Market for Qualified Nickel Products

Official statistics allow the study to estimate apparent consumption for nickel mill products in Europe. Apparent consumption is production plus imports minus exports. In 2024, the EU consumed an estimated 57.8 kt of nickel plate, sheet, strip, and foil and 9.2 kt of tubes. The bar estimate depends on a confidential production figure (Eurostat, 2025a, 2025b, 2025d).

Eurostat's reported production values fall below the value of the nickel they contain. Table 10 therefore applies EU border unit values to the volumes. This gives an estimated EUR 1.8 to 2.8 bn for flat products and EUR 0.24 to 0.38 bn for tubes (Eurostat, 2025c). These codes only cover alloys in which nickel is the main metal, so Table 10 captures only part of the qualified market.

### 6.2 6.2 Conversion Creates the Trade Surplus

Germany leads the ten reporting countries in exports of nickel plate, sheet, and strip. It ranks second for bars and wire. Its exports across the three mill product codes rose from USD 0.968 bn in 2019 to USD 1.418 bn in 2024. This pattern shows the value of converting nickel into mill products (United Nations Statistics Division, 2026b, 2026c, 2026d).

Germany imports USD 736 million more raw nickel than it exports. It is a net exporter of every nickel mill product in Table 11. Its plate and strip show the conversion premium described in section 4.1. EU export values show a similar pattern, at EUR 31.0 per kg compared with EUR 15.5 for the contained nickel (Eurostat, 2025c; United Nations Statistics Division, 2026c; World Bank, 2026a).

Data from the reporting countries alone suggest that China leads unwrought nickel exports. Import data from their trading partners give a fuller picture. These mirror data show that Canada, Norway, Russia, and Australia each shipped about USD 1 bn to the ten reporting countries. These are major sources of class 1 nickel, and each shipped more than Indonesia reports (United Nations Statistics Division, 2026a). The same data identify Austria and Sweden as converters, similar to Germany. Section 6.3 follows the trade routes that connect these producers to their buyers.

### 6.3 6.3 Corridors and Dependencies

The main trade routes reflect where buyers operate. Germany exported about USD 111 to 112 million of nickel bar and wire to each of the United Kingdom and the United States in 2024. These are key aircraft engine markets described in section 5.4. Germany's plate and strip exports go mainly to Austria, Italy, China, and the United States, where chemical and electronics fabricators operate (United Nations Statistics Division, 2026b, 2026c). British exports of turbojets and gas turbines reached USD 39.8 bn in 2024, including re-exports (United Nations Statistics Division, 2026e).

### 6.4 6.4 Trade Policy Reshapes Sourcing and Access

Trade policy can change costs and market access quickly. In 2025, the United States raised Section 232 tariffs on steel and aluminium to 50 percent. From 1 August 2025, it taxed the copper content of semifinished copper products at 50 percent. Its tariff schedule lists general duties of 2 to 3 percent for nickel bars, plate, strip, and tubes under HS 7505 to 7507 (The White House, 2025; United States International Trade Commission, 2026).

The EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism entered its definitive phase on 1 January 2026. It covers ferrochrome and ferronickel and excludes nickel products in HS chapter 75. It can therefore raise costs for inputs without protecting nickel alloy mill products (Ernst & Young, 2025). The EU Critical Raw Materials Act lists nickel, cobalt, and tungsten as strategic or critical (European Parliament and Council, 2024). Together with the quotas and licenses in chapter 4, these measures give producers a reason to convert metals close to customers and qualify several sources for each critical input.
