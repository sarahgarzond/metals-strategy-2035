## 4.7 4.7 Other Selected Metals

Zirconium and the minor superalloy elements serve specialized uses. Copper, aluminium, and manganese have much larger markets, with smaller roles in high-performance applications.

### 4.7.1 4.7.1 Zirconium

World production of zirconium concentrates reached about 1.2 Mt gross weight in 2025. Australia led with 400 kt. Most concentrates go into ceramics, foundry sand, and refractories (U.S. Geological Survey, 2026m). The metal market is much smaller. Fuel makers and integrated producers such as ATI control their nuclear fuel cladding supply. The open market includes zirconium plate and tube for acid service (ATI, 2026a).

### 4.7.2 4.7.2 Copper

Copper connects high-performance materials to electrification. ICSG and IEA figures put world copper mine production at about 23.2 Mt in 2025. Flat-rolled products account for 11 percent of first use, compared with 63 percent for wire. Copper averaged USD 9,947 per tonne in 2025 and set records above USD 14,500 in 2026 (International Copper Study Group, 2025, 2026a; International Energy Agency, 2026b; The Oregon Group, 2026; World Bank, 2026b).

Under current policies, the IEA expects mine supply from current projects to fall to 20.7 Mt by 2035. Demand rises from 27.8 Mt to 32.4 Mt in the same outlook. The IEA estimates a 2035 primary supply deficit of about 25 percent (International Energy Agency, 2026a, 2026b). Specialized forms include copper-nickel and high-conductivity strip. Section 5.6 discusses data center busbars as an example.

### 4.7.3 4.7.3 Aluminium

Aluminium can replace heavier alloys when temperatures and corrosion conditions allow. World primary production reached about 74 Mt in 2025. China produced 61 percent. European primary production has fallen to about 955 kt, compared with annual demand of about 9 Mt (European Aluminium, n.d.; Federation of Aluminium Consumers in Europe, 2024; International Aluminium Institute, 2026; U.S. Geological Survey, 2026c).

### 4.7.4 4.7.4 Manganese

Manganese demand mainly follows carbon steel production. Mines produced about 20 Mt of contained manganese in 2025. South Africa, Gabon, Ghana, and Australia supplied about 81 percent of the total. The ore price fell to about USD 4.5 per metric ton unit. The study identifies limited supply risk and growth potential for alloy producers (International Manganese Institute, 2026; U.S. Geological Survey, 2026f).

### 4.7.5 4.7.5 Additional Metals

Iron is used in alloys such as FeNi36 for display masks and FeNi42 for lead frames. Their value depends mainly on the ability to roll the material to the required specifications.

Niobium and rhenium are used in superalloys. Alloy 718 contains about 5 percent niobium. Brazil mines about 93 percent of the world's niobium, and CBMM sells it as vacuum-grade nickel-niobium. Single-crystal turbine blades contain rhenium. In 2025, mines produced only about 81 t of rhenium worldwide (CBMM, n.d.; RTX, 2026; U.S. Geological Survey, 2026b, 2026i).
