# Framework in chapter 3

On the user's instruction the framework is the seven-link metal value chain:
Material (ch 4) > Product form (ch 4) > Application (ch 4) > Industry (ch 5) > Geography (ch 5) > Market size (ch 5 and 6) > Growth (ch 7).
It replaces the earlier Supply, Trade, Demand and Outlook description.

Changes made to follow the chain, for the team to accept or reject:
1. Chapter titles were renamed slightly: 4. Metal Markets: Materials, Product Forms and Applications, 5. Industry Needs: Demand,
   Geography and Market Size, 6. Global Market Size and Trade, 7. Growth Scenarios to 2035 (7.1 Growth Model and Scenario Assumptions).
2. Table 1 states for each link the guiding question, what is measured, the main evidence and the chapter.
3. Chapter 8 assesses opportunities on eight criteria. "Strategic fit" became "supply security", because the report no longer
   refers to a specific company and fit cannot be judged without one.
4. The stage discipline (mine, refined, melt, mill product never added) and the evidence hierarchy are stated as rules of the chain.
5. Appendix A8 (risks and kill criteria) was added to keep the main text within 30 pages.
