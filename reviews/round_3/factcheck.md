# Fact check, round 3

Scope: sections/*.md (chapters 1 to 9, then appendices), data/sources.csv, report/report.html. Sections were not edited.

Method and coverage
- Every [ID] marker in sections/*.md was parsed (187 distinct IDs). All map to a row in data/sources.csv. No orphan markers.
- All 187 cited rows have author, intext_author, pub_date, ref_type, title and url or doi. No incomplete rows.
- Numbers in cited sentences were compared with the cited rows (value, unit, notes), 18 sentences needed manual review (mostly unit conversions, all resolved except as listed below).
- Table 12 to Table A1 to Table 13 to chapter 7 text were recomputed from the formula: all 21 indices and all 5 product family averages are correct.
- Table 14 weighted scores were recomputed with weights 15/20/15/10/10/10/10/10 (sum 100): all 11 scores and the tier thresholds match. No inconsistency.
- Original source spot checks: USGS MCS 2026 PDFs (cobalt 310 kt, Congo 230 kt, tungsten 85 kt, China 67 kt: confirmed), UN Comtrade public API (Germany HS 7506 exports 2024, 17,775,010 kg: confirmed), Germany HS 7505 to 7507 exports recomputed from data/clean (USD 0.968 bn in 2019, USD 1.418 bn in 2024, +46.5 percent: confirmed). I did not re-run tools/data/fetch.py because it overwrites data/clean, so I queried the same endpoints directly. This is about 8 percent of the rows, below the 30 percent target. The executive summary headline numbers were not separately checked because no executive summary section exists in sections/.
- Client names: grep for VDM, Acerinox, Haynes, Münvest and "the company" in sections/*.md and in the body of report/report.html returns no hit. Only the generic words "company filings" appear, which is fine.
- Duplicate ATI reference: confirmed fixed. report.html now has ATI (2026a) for the 10-K and ATI (2026b) for the Q3 transcript, with different URLs.

Counts: 1 wrong, 2 inconsistent, 3 unsourced or incompletely sourced, 5 data or reference quality issues, 4 minor. Total 15.
