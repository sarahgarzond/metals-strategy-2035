## Citation coverage

All 35 bracketed data ids in `sections/*.md` resolve to a row in `data/sources.csv`. There are no duplicate data ids in the source log. The industry chapters also cite sources as (Author, Year). Those labels have imported `REF_*` rows. Many of those rows store the citation suffix, such as `2026a`, in the date field and use reference type `team`. They are stubs for the reference list, not full dated dataset records. That does not by itself make a number wrong. A number with only a `REF_*` stub and no value row is weaker than a number with a value row, which is why the oil, services and medical findings above are ranked on the value, not on the presence of a name.

USGS figures used in the metal chapters are from Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026. Airbus and Boeing figures that were checked are from the 2026 Global Market Forecast and the 2026 Commercial Market Outlook. The hydrogen figures that matched are from Global Hydrogen Review 2026. The electricity growth rate is the one place a later IEA edition supersedes the cited vintage. No SEMI statistic is asserted in the sections. Chapters 4.2, 4.5 and 4.6 correctly refuse an apparent consumption figure where the legs are not logged.
