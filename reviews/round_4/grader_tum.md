## Justification

**Research (1.7).** USGS MCS 2026, IEA Critical Minerals Outlook, INSG, Cobalt Institute, IMOA, ICSG, IAI, Eurostat PRODCOM, UN Comtrade (now under one author), World Bank, OECD, SEMI, WSTS, Cefic and filings from Q3 2026 carry the numbers, and the editions are current. Spotlights are strong in aerospace (Safran EUR 150 m, 30,000 t press, Gennevilliers, 2029, about 14,000 parts a year), energy (Siemens Energy 17 to above 30 GW), electronics (NVIDIA Kyber 2027, DNP Kurosaki Gen 8, Vertiv 12,946 t copper hedge) and medical (ASTM F75, F1537, F3213, F2384, OXINIUM 94.1 percent at 20 years). Deductions:
(a) The first numbers in the report (ch. 1, p. 3) still come from McKinsey and two paid market reports (IMARC, The Insight Partners). The source rules allow these only for triangulation.
(b) The copper price in Table 2 and 4.7.2 still rests on a commodity blog (The Oregon Group). Table 2 also shows "USD 9,947/t, LME record in 2026" next to a 4.7.2 record "above USD 14,500", which reads as a contradiction.
(c) CMOC Group is still listed as author of a Mining Weekly article and a mining.com article (References p. 33).
(d) The one risk that could demote a priority 1 (maskless OLED patterning, 8.1) rests on Display Daily, n.d.
(e) Chapter 3 states that "every key figure is confirmed by a second independent source". Many decisive figures are single sourced, for example Siemens Energy capacity, the BUTTING turnover, the Safran press and the Bloom capacity. Either the statement or the evidence has to change.
(f) Automotive (5.1) and chemical processing (5.5) still have no spotlight that names an alloy grade, a product form and a likely supplier together. BorgWarner, Garrett, BASF Zhanjiang and Alleima Mehsana each lack the alloy.

**Model (2.0).** The tier 1 formula, parameters (Table A1), backtest (Table A2), simulation settings (seed, copula, Dirichlet) and scoring rules (Table A4) are documented well enough for an examiner to rerun the model. What stops a 1.7:
(a) The scores break the team's own rules. Table A4 gives Market 1 for "no measured volume yet", and 5.6.1 says that "no public source measures FeNi ... tonnage", yet FeNi36 foil scores Market 3. At Market 1 it would score 3.60, below the 3.75 threshold, and drop to priority 2. Chemical plate scores Market 5 on the EUR 1.8 to 2.8 bn pool, but 5.5.1 itself says this pool covers all end uses, "of which chemical processing holds an unmeasured share". Fusion C-276 tape scores Supply 4 although C-276 contains tungsten, which is under an active Chinese licence (4.6).
(b) The FeNi growth score has the wrong driver. The 157 index is 0.60 electronics (driven by 300 mm fab equipment capacity) plus 0.30 electric vehicle parts. Neither drives OLED fine metal mask foil, which depends on OLED IT panel line capacity.
(c) The backtest validates the wrong industries. Aerospace and electronics, which supply two of the three priority 1 positions, have only nominal STAN series, so they receive no correction. Chapter 3 promises a correction "for the past optimism of official outlooks", but the backtest tests log linear trends. Only one row (IEA WEI 2025 against 2026) compares an official outlook, and it is not used.
(d) The aerospace engine output growth (mode 3.4 percent) is the Airbus fleet stock growth rate. Deliveries include replacement and are a level, not a growth of the stock. This needs one sentence of justification or a delivery based driver.
(e) The supply overlay tests cobalt, nickel and copper. Chapter 3 promises a test against "export quota and licence shocks", but the tungsten and molybdenum licences (the ones that touch cast superalloys and C-276) are not tested.
(f) Table 1 shows six rows, because Industry and Geography are merged, while the framework has seven links.
(g) A2 says the script rewrites "Figures 3 and 4", but the report has no Figure 4.

**Storyline (2.0).** Chapter 2 now asks a sharper question, gives the thesis and a roadmap, and the conclusion answers in the same words. The metal sections hand over cleanly. Deductions:
(a) Chapter 1 still opens with "For centuries, metals..." and a mining revenue figure. This was fix 5 last round and it was not done.
