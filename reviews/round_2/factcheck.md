# Fact check, round 2

Read only. No section was edited. Date of check: 8 November 2026. Sections checked: `sections/01` to `sections/11`.

## Scope and method

- Every `[DATA_ID]` marker in `sections/*.md` was extracted (174 unique ids) and matched against `data/sources.csv` (1,030 rows). All 174 resolve. No marker is orphaned. No typed (Author, Year) citations remain in the body, only informal "Sources:" lines (see finding C3).
- Recomputed from `data/clean/`: Table 2 shares (`usgs_mcs2026.csv`), Table 17 (`comtrade_portfolio_world_2019-2024.csv`, which holds ten reporters, plus `comtrade_*_bilateral_2022-2024.csv` for the corridors), Tables 18 and 19 and appendix A2 (all 21 index cells and the two worked examples), and the German totals in 6.1.
- Compared claims with the register notes and with `research/materials`, `research/sectors` and `research/companies`. Nothing was re fetched from the internet except two currency checks (IEA WEO 2026 and WSTS autumn 2026, both not yet published, so WEO 2025 and WSTS spring 2026 are still the latest editions).
- There is no executive summary in `sections/`, so the headline numbers checked were those of chapters 4, 7 and 9 and the conclusion.
- Sampling: well over 30 percent of the cited rows were compared with the clean data or the research notes. Rows that only exist as a register entry (company filings) were compared with the register note and the company note, not with the original filing.

## Result in one view

- Recomputed and correct: Tables 18 and 19 (21 of 21 cells), A2 examples, German 2019 to 2024 export growth (+46.5 percent, rounded to 47), 7502, 7507, 8105, 8311 top five lists, all net trade figures, all shares in Table 2 except cobalt, Mn 81 percent, ICIO values, UK and US corridors, 61 companies, 1,030 register entries.
- Wrong or inconsistent and worth fixing before the next build: items 1 to 14 below.
